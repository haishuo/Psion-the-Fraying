# Character Creation: Building a Gifted

**The Crunch — Storyteller System (Revised)**

*Psion: The Fraying uses the Classic World of Darkness Storyteller System. Dice are ten-siders; the default difficulty is 6; you count successes, 1s cancel successes, and a roll with no successes and at least one 1 is a botch. If you have played Vampire: The Masquerade, you already know the engine. What follows is character creation for the Gifted, plus everything Psion adds to it: the **Domains**, the **Tether** trait, and the **Fraying Track**.*

---

## The Shape of a Gifted Character

A Gifted character sheet is a standard oWoD sheet with three additions:

- **Domains** replace Disciplines — seven rated powers (0–5) that define what your mind can do.
- **Tether** is a new permanent trait (1–5) measuring how much psionic strain your metaphysical architecture can bear before it snaps. It is the one number you can almost never improve.
- **The Tether Track** is a ten-box condition track — the bashing/lethal logic of the Health track, applied to your tether. It is where the horror of the setting lives.

Everything else — nine Attributes, the Talents/Skills/Knowledges, Willpower, Health, Backgrounds, Merits & Flaws, Nature & Demeanor — works exactly as it does in the Storyteller System you know.

---

## Step One: Concept and Affiliation

Decide who your character is, when and how they **Expressed**, and — the Psion equivalent of choosing a clan or tribe — their **Affiliation**: their relationship to the institutions that rule and resist the lattice.

Affiliation is not a hard cage. Any Gifted can learn any Domain. But your Affiliation shapes the Domain you were trained in, the Backgrounds available to you, and the entire texture of your chronicle.

| Affiliation | Favored Domain | Typical starting Backgrounds | The campaign it implies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inquisition** (Circuit) | Kinesis | Status, Lattice Access | Enforcement, threat elimination, the moral weight of the White Rooms |
| **Praxis** (Circuit) | Control | Status, Resources | Research, doctrine, Cognitive Reconstruction |
| **Maintainers** (Circuit) | Barriers | Status, Lattice Access | Lattice engineering, binding, severance |
| **Panopticon** (Circuit) | Detection | Status, Influence | Surveillance, flagging, the uncertainty of being the watcher |
| **Integrators** (Circuit) | Intrusion | Status, Mentor | Training the newly Expressed, the families broken to do it |
| **Preservers** (Circuit) | Memory | Status, Influence | Records, narrative control, the secret archives |
| **Operatives** (Circuit) | Projection | Status, Contacts, Allies | Deep cover among other supernaturals, the slow erosion of identity |
| **Railroad** (Project) | Intrusion | Safehouse, Allies (CoG) | Extraction, sanctuary, the weight of the ones you can't save |
| **Rebel** (Project) | Kinesis | Safehouse, Allies | Sabotage, assassination, fighting odds you cannot win |
| **Parasyte** (Project) | Projection | Status, Off-Grid (covert) | Double life, false loyalty, slow moral erosion |
| **Fractal** (Project) | Memory | Contacts, Influence | Philosophy, preserved truth, demonstrating alternatives |
| **Iconoclast** (Project) | Barriers | Safehouse, Resources | Reverse-engineering the lattice, severance operations |
| **Heterodox** (Project) | Control | Contacts, Mentor | Questioning the framework, abilities the taxonomy can't explain |
| **Lacuna** (Project) | Detection | Safehouse, Off-Grid | Counter-surveillance, building spaces the Panopticon cannot see |
| **Unaffiliated** | (any one) | Off-Grid, Contacts | Survival on the margins, courted by every faction |
| **Cult of the Unbound** | (any one) | Allies (Cult), Off-Grid | The transcendence lie, recruiting the desperate — almost always an antagonist concept |

Also choose **Nature and Demeanor** from the standard Archetypes — they remain your Willpower-regain hooks.

## Step Two: Attributes

Standard priorities. Distribute **7 / 5 / 3** dots among the three categories (Physical, Social, Mental), one dot in each Attribute free as always. Nothing higher than 5; nothing higher than 4 from this step before freebies.

---

## Step Three: Abilities

Distribute **13 / 9 / 5** dots among Talents / Skills / Knowledges. No Ability above 3 before freebies.

Psion adds three setting Abilities to the standard lists:

- **Psionics** (Knowledge) — the theory of Domains, the tether, and fraying. The academic understanding of one's own condition. Essential for Praxis and Heterodox; valuable to anyone who wants to recognize the warning signs in themselves and others.
- **Lattice** (Knowledge) — the architecture of binding constructs and the network. How integration works, how severance works, how to read construct health. The Maintainer's and Iconoclast's stock-in-trade.
- **Awareness** (Talent) — instinctive sensitivity to psionic activity and being observed; the prickle on the neck when the Panopticon is looking. (If your table already uses Awareness, this is the same Ability.)

---

## Step Four: Advantages

### Domains — 6 dots (Barriers ●1 mandatory)

Every Gifted begins with **Barriers ●1 automatically** — the trained baseline of bracing — and then distributes **5 further dots** among the seven Domains, for **six dots total**. At creation no single Domain may exceed **4**. (For a grittier chronicle the ST may grant only 3 free dots; for a high-powered one, 7 — but the mandatory Barriers dot is constant either way.)

Three rules from the setting's metaphysics constrain how you spend these:

- **Barriers ●1 is universal, because its absence is the Unshielded state.** Bracing — the foundational proto-Thoughtshield every newly Expressed Gifted is taught — *is* Barriers ●1. It is the structure that stops the bidirectional thought-leak and makes every other Domain usable, so a trained Gifted always has it. The only characters who begin at **Barriers 0 are those still Unshielded** — just Expressed, not yet braced, leaking and overwhelmed. That is a harrowing advanced starting concept (a child mid-crisis, an escapee who never finished training), not a standard build.
- **Memory and Control require Intrusion.** Their techniques do nothing without first achieving mental access through Intrusion. Dots in Memory or Control are inert above what your Intrusion can open. If you want to manipulate minds, buy Intrusion alongside.
- **Your Affiliation's favored Domain costs nothing extra to raise with freebies** (6 per dot instead of 7) and is the Domain you were trained in. You begin knowing its scaling techniques. (For a Maintainer or Iconoclast, whose favored Domain *is* Barriers, the mandatory bracing dot simply gives that specialization a free head start.)

**Techniques known at creation:** you know the scaling baseline of every Domain you have dots in, plus **one threshold technique per two dots** in that Domain that you qualify for (ST may grant more based on training and Affiliation).

### Backgrounds — 5 dots

Standard Backgrounds (Allies, Contacts, Mentor, Resources, Influence) apply. Psion adds:

- **Status (MetaConcert)** — rank and standing within your Circuit and the lattice hierarchy.
- **Lattice Access** — the breadth of your network privileges: Panopticon clearances, construct-level permissions, the right to ask the system for things.
- **Safehouse** — a Lacuna-built or Project-secured space the Panopticon cannot see into. Rated by size, security, and how thoroughly it is hidden.
- **Off-Grid** — the degree to which you are invisible to the lattice. At low rating, a good false signature and clean habits; at high rating, you were never integrated at all, or were cleanly severed, and carry no binding construct. (See the **Off the Lattice** Merit.)

### Tether — begins at 2

Every Gifted begins with **Tether 2** for free. You may adjust it at creation:

- **Raise to 3** for **7 freebie points** (it is as dear as a Domain dot) — a genuinely resilient tether.
- **Drop to 1** to take the **Fragile Tether** Flaw (+5 freebies) — you fray fast and snap early.
- **Tether 4–5 is not available at creation.** Such resilience is the province of anomalies (see Eve) and rare, story-earned circumstance. Per the setting's own rule, the tether *cannot be strengthened* by training; the Storyteller may shift it only in extraordinary, narratively significant cases — never by routine experience.

### Willpower — begins at 3

Start with **Willpower 3** (raise with freebies, 1 per dot, max 10). Some Affiliations whose culture is built on discipline — Inquisition, Integrators, Maintainers — may begin at 4 at the ST's discretion.

Remember what Willpower *is* in this game: it governs whether you **choose** to push your tether, not what happens when you do. It can never be spent to reduce strain, repair the tether, or survive the Snap. *Mental strength helps discipline, not tether integrity.*

## Step Five: Finishing Touches — Freebie Points (15)

Spend **15 freebie points**:

| Trait | Cost per dot |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 5 |
| Ability | 2 |
| Domain | **7** (6 if Affiliation-favored) |
| Background | 1 |
| Willpower | 1 |
| Tether (2 → 3) | **7** |
| Merits / Flaws | by rating (Flaws return points, max +7 total) |

Then record derived values: **Health** is the standard seven levels (Bruised → Incapacitated). The **Tether Track** begins empty — healthy.

## Merits & Flaws (Psion Sampler)

A starting set; expand as your chronicle needs.

**Merits**
- **Off the Lattice** (3–5 pt) — you carry no binding construct: never integrated, or cleanly severed. The Panopticon cannot passively locate or monitor you, and you feel nothing of the network. But you are an outlaw the moment you are recognized, you had no formal Integrator training (consider being self-taught and prone to bad habits), and no Maintainer will sever you safely if you change your mind.
- **Disciplined** (2 pt) — −1 difficulty on Discipline checks to resist the temptation to push past safe limits.
- **Quiet Mind** (2 pt) — your psionic signature is faint; +1 difficulty for others to detect your use or read you.
- **Attuned** (1 pt) — you have an unusually accurate felt sense of your own fraying; the ST tells you your current Stage in plain terms rather than by symptom alone. (Distinct from the Tether Sense technique, which reads *others'* tethers; this is inward attunement to your own.)

**Flaws**
- **Fragile Tether** (5 pt) — Tether 1. You fray quickly and the Snap comes early. (This is the mechanical path to Tether 1.)
- **Loud Signature** (2 pt) — −1 difficulty for others to detect and track your psionic use; the Panopticon finds you easily.
- **Flagged** (2–4 pt) — the Panopticon already suspects you. You are on a watchlist; surveillance of you is routine, not exceptional.
- **Frayed** (3 pt) — you have been to the edge and carry the scars: begin play with **2 permanent Fray boxes** already marked on your Tether Track.

## The Tether Track and the Fraying (Rules Summary)

*Enough to play from this sheet. The full treatment — cascade, the Unshielded status, detection, recovery edge cases, and Storyteller guidance — lives in [The Fraying — Game Mechanics](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md).*

Your **Tether Track** is ten boxes, running from healthy (empty) to the Snap (full). It carries two kinds of mark, exactly as the Health track carries bashing and lethal:

- **Strain `/`** — temporary tether stress. Recoverable.
- **Fray `X`** — lasting damage. Recovers slowly or never.

**Marking and escalation.** Fill boxes left to right. **If Strain would be marked on a box that already holds Strain, it becomes Fray instead.** Stack psionic use without rest and your temporary stress hardens into permanent damage.

**Using a Technique** costs Strain equal to its Domain's **Strain Rating** — Detection/Barriers **1**, Intrusion/Memory **2**, Control/Projection **3**, Kinesis **4** — plus **+1** to push hard, hit multiple targets, or force through a target's Barriers; **+1** per extra scene sustained (per turn for Kinesis); **+1** if desperate or **+2** if panicked; **+1** per range band for Projection.

**Stages** (total boxes filled) impose penalties like wounds:

| Boxes | Stage | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 1 · Minimal | No penalty; clears overnight |
| 3–4 | 2 · Moderate | −1 die to psionic pools; detectable by Tether Sense |
| 5–6 | 3 · Severe | −2 dice to **all** actions; auto-flagged by Panopticon |
| 7–8 | 4 · Critical | −3 dice; **any psionic use forces a Snap Check** |
| 9 | 4 · Deep | −4; severe stress alone can force a Snap Check |
| 10 | The Snap | Untethering |

**The Snap Check** — triggered by filling the track, by any psionic use at Stage 4, or by a major shock at Stage 4. Roll **Tether + Stamina vs difficulty 8**. *(Willpower may not be added or spent — canon.)*
- **Success:** you hold — and take **+1 Fray** for the strain of holding.
- **Failure:** the tether snaps. You Untether. Highest Domain **4+** → you become a coherent **psidolon** (the catastrophe, now in the ST's hands); **3 or less** → you dissipate. Dead.
- **Botch:** catastrophic Snap, no chance of coherence.

**Recovery.** Clear **(Tether) Strain boxes per full day** of *complete* rest — any psionic use at all, even a Detection pulse, resets the day. **Fray** clears at roughly **one box per month** of total rest, and the last one or two Fray boxes from a near-Snap are **permanent**. No technique, drug, therapy, or act of will repairs the tether. That is not a gap in the rules. It is the game.

**Willpower's role in fraying** is the **Discipline check**: when the "just one more" temptation arises, or an emotional trigger threatens involuntary Expression, spend or roll Willpower to *not reach for the power*. Willpower decides whether you fray. Tether decides what happens when you do.

> **Design note — where the morality track went.** Psion has no Humanity, Path, or Conscience track. It doesn't need one. In this setting the slide toward becoming a monster is literal and physical, and it already has ten boxes: **Fray is your degeneration meter.** What the Gifted surrender to power is not their conscience but the thing holding them together.

## Worked Example: Dana Reyes, Railroad Conductor

**Concept:** A former paramedic who Expressed at nineteen during a highway pileup, was reached by the Railroad before the Integrators, and now runs extractions. **Affiliation:** Project Railroad (favored Domain: Intrusion). **Nature:** Caregiver. **Demeanor:** Gallant.

**Attributes** (7/5/3 — Physical primary): Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3 · Charisma 3, Manipulation 2, Appearance 2 · Perception 3, Intelligence 2, Wits 2.

**Abilities** (13/9/5 — Talents primary): Alertness 2, Awareness 2, Empathy 3, Athletics 1, Brawl 1, Streetwise 2, Subterfuge 2 · Drive 2, Firstaid 3, Stealth 2, Security 2 · Psionics 2, Lattice 1, Medicine 2.

**Domains** (Barriers ●1 free, +5 dots): Barriers 2, Intrusion 2, Detection 1, Projection 1. *(Bracing gives every Gifted Barriers 1; she added a second for the privacy her work demands. Intrusion is her core tool — calming and reading frightened children; a dot of Detection finds the Unshielded, and a single, dreaded dot of Projection lets her scout an extraction route. She deliberately avoided Memory and Control: she has no wish to rewrite anyone, and they'd demand more Intrusion than she can spare.)*

**Backgrounds** (5): Safehouse 2, Allies 2 (Children of Gaia), Contacts 1.

**Tether:** 2 (default). **Willpower:** 3.

**Freebies (15):** Willpower 3 → 5 (2 pts) · Intrusion 2 → 3 (Affiliation-favored, 6 pts) · Empathy 3 → 4 (2 pts) · Merit: Disciplined (2 pts) · Background: Safehouse 2 → 3 (1 pt) · Merit: Attuned (1 pt) · 1 pt to Drive 2 → 3. *(15 spent.)*

**In play:** Reaching out to a frightened Unshielded teenager, Dana leads with a **Surface Scan** (Intrusion, Perception + Intrusion = 6 dice). Calm, careful work: 2 Strain → boxes 1–2, Stage 1, no penalty. She'll rest it off by morning (Tether 2 clears 2 boxes a day). But when an Inquisition team arrives and she has to **Astral Step** across the building to scout the exits, panicked — base 3, +2 panic, +1 distance = **6 Strain**, landing her at boxes 3–8: **Stage 4**. The next time she so much as pulses Detection to find the kid, she rolls **Tether 2 + Stamina 3 = 5 dice vs 8** and prays. This is the Railroad's whole tragedy in one scene: the power that saves the child is the power that kills the rescuer.

## Building a Dreambound

*A Dreambound (see [The Dreambound](The%20Dreambound.md)) is not a weaker version of either splat stapled to the other. Per the setting's own hard canon, a Dreambound is **fully Changeling and fully Gifted at once** — nothing partial about either half. Chargen reflects that literally: build the full Changeling side as your core Changeling rulebook describes (Seeming, Kith, Court or motley, Glamour, Banality, Willpower, Arts, Realms), then add the Gifted side below. There is no discount and no hard cap on either side. The tension that keeps this from being two character sheets' worth of power is not arithmetic. It is philosophical, and it is described in full below.*

### The Two Doors In

- **Kinain (heritable).** You are fae-blooded — most often sidhe-descended, the setting's nearest thing to a "half-Sidhe" — and were also born Gifted. You've lived between both worlds your whole life; this is simply who you are. Build as below from the start of play.
- **Chrysalis (acquired).** A fae soul, drawn by the "loud dreaming" of a bright, reaching mind, reincarnates into someone already living and already tethered — an existing Gifted. This does not require a birth lottery, which makes it the natural route for an **established Gifted PC to become Dreambound mid-chronicle** rather than at creation: a genuine character arc, not a retcon. Once Chrysalis completes, mechanics are identical to a kinain build from that point forward. A player may also simply declare Chrysalis already happened in backstory and build a Chrysalis-origin Dreambound at session one — the two doors lead to the same room.

### Chargen Mechanics

**The Changeling half** — build entirely by your table's standard Changeling: The Dreaming rules. Nothing here is modified: Seeming and Kith set your starting Glamour and Banality as normal, Arts and Realms are bought exactly as any changeling buys them, Willpower and Backgrounds work exactly as written.

**The Gifted half** — added on top, using the standard rules from Steps Four above, at full parity with any other Gifted:
- **Tether begins at 2**, adjustable by the same rules as any Gifted (Step Four).
- **Domains: Barriers ●1 mandatory, plus 5 further dots** — the identical 6-dot budget every Gifted gets. No special restriction applies at creation. A Dreambound may, if the player wants, build toward deep Domain mastery from day one. Whether that is wise is a different question, answered below.
- Psionics, Lattice, and Awareness remain available Abilities exactly as for any Gifted.

### The Instrumental Floor

The Domains are not corrupting in themselves — they are simply the instrumental expression of the Talent: precision, repeatability, categorization, control. That is exactly the mode of thought Banality rewards, and a Dreambound cannot practice it without cost to the half of them that runs on wonder instead.

**A Dreambound's permanent Banality can never rest below their total Domain dots ÷ 3, rounded down** (Barriers' mandatory first dot counts same as any other). This floor rises only when Domain dots are bought — never from a single scene, a single Banal act, or a single hard day. It is the arithmetic shadow of a whole *life's* orientation, not a penalty for one choice. Temporary Banality still moves up and down around that floor with ordinary Changeling play — a Dreambound deep into Memory mastery can still have a wonderful week — but the floor itself only ever climbs, and only in proportion to how far the orthodox path has been walked.

**This floor caps at 9.** Banality 10 — full, permanent loss of the fae soul, becoming simply an ordinary Gifted from that day forward — is never reached by arithmetic alone. Dots can walk a Dreambound to the door. Whatever finally opens it should be a scene the table plays, not a subtraction the Storyteller makes silently on a character sheet.

The reverse choice matters exactly as much and deserves the same weight at the table: a Dreambound who keeps their Domain investment light, leans on the fae-native "back door" for their signature work, and spends Glamour freely is not playing a weaker character. They are choosing to keep the floor low enough that wonder still has room to breathe. Neither path is objectively stronger. One of them is simply a slower way of becoming someone else.

### Dreambound Tropes

*Called Tropes, not Gifts — Gifts already belongs to the Garou.*

A Dreambound's signature abilities are neither Domains nor Changeling Arts — they are a third thing, exactly as [The Dreambound](The%20Dreambound.md) describes tether-mending itself: not psionics with a fae accent, not Dreaming with a psionic accent, but something that only exists because both natures share one skull. So they are written and bought differently from everything else on this sheet: **no dots, no scaling, no fixed numeric ranges.** Each Trope is either known or not known. What it produces is always personal, symbolic, and specific to the Dreambound experiencing it — two Dreambound with the identical Trope will describe what it feels like in completely different terms, and both descriptions are correct. When a Trope's use has real stakes at the table, resolve it with **Kenning + the Attribute that fits the moment** (Perception to notice, Wits to interpret, Willpower to hold something steady) — the fiction stays symbolic even when the dice are not.

**Story logic governs what a roll is even for.** These Tropes never let a Dreambound issue a command into someone's sleep — there is no dice result that means "and then he believed her." A dream has its own internal logic (the wolf is beaten the way *that* dream's wolves get beaten, the locked door opens on the reason *that* dream's doors open on), and success on a roll means the Dreambound found the true move the dream's own story was already asking for — never that they overrode it. If a player's proposed action doesn't fit the dream's internal logic, no roll should let it succeed regardless of dice; if it does fit, the roll decides whether they found it in time. This also fixes the ceiling on what any of this can touch: reach stops at minds. There is no Dreambound version of Kinesis, and Following Stories only ever lets a Dreambound experience a place strangely — never move so much as a teacup once there.

**These are ordered by intimacy with story, not by power.** A Dreambound is a hermeneut, not a specialist climbing a ladder of stronger effects — so the tree below reads as a progression of relationship, from merely overhearing a story to holding the one thread underneath all of them.

**Baseline — every Dreambound has these from the day both natures are real, no purchase required:**

- **Hearing Stories.** Eyes closed, attention loosened, you become aware of the dreaming nearby — not a headcount, a *texture*. One Dreambound hears it as distant music; another smells rain; another sees small lights drifting through a dark city. It answers only "is anyone dreaming near me, roughly how many, how troubled" — never who, never exactly where, never what about.
- **Entering Stories.** Step into a sleeping mind nearby and walk its dream — visit, speak, nudge it gently, become the detail a dreamer half-notices and can't explain on waking. This is simply changeling birthright, older than any tether, and it costs nothing because it was never psionic to begin with.

**The earned tree.** Each Trope below requires the Dreambound's **permanent Banality to sit at or below the listed ceiling**, *and* the Storyteller's judgment that the character has actually lived the relationship the Trope implies — not merely that the number happens to be low this month. Once eligible, a Trope is bought with a flat XP cost (no dots to raise afterward). Note what this means read against the **Instrumental Floor** above: every Domain dot that pushes the Floor up doesn't just move an abstract number, it can lock a door in this list from the top down.

| Trope | Banality ceiling | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading Stories** | 6 | Where a pure changeling sees only the dream, you begin to catch its shape — whether this dream is fear rehearsing itself, grief looking for an ending, a warning the dreamer cannot say awake. |
| **Naming Stories** | 5 | You can tell when a specific image in a dream is standing in for something real and specific — a person, a fear, a secret — rather than ordinary dream-noise. |
| **Following Stories** | 5 | Deliberately reach a *particular* dreamer, or a real place with no one dreaming in it at all — the dreamlike Projection-that-isn't described in [The Dreambound](The%20Dreambound.md). What you bring back is always distorted and symbolic, true in feeling before it is true in fact. |
| **Speaking with Stories** | 4 | Genuine two-way exchange with a dreaming mind — not visiting, actually being heard and hearing back, sometimes with more than one dreamer sharing the same dream-space at once. |
| **Holding Stories** | 3 | Hold a dream steady against its own instability — keep it from dissolving, keep a fragile dreamer from waking before the work is done. This is the prerequisite state beneath Seeing the Thread: nobody safely goes looking for something as fragile as a tether inside a dream that might collapse around them at any moment. |
| **Seeing the Thread** | 2, and only with the Storyteller's judgment that both natures have been carried in genuine balance across real years of play | See the tether itself, running through the dream like a rope, a root, a vein of light — whatever shape that dreamer's mind gives it. This is the last rung of the tree that can be bought at all. It grants sight. Nothing more. |

### Tether Mending: Not for Sale

**No amount of experience alone grants the ability to *mend* a tether once seen.** There is no XP cost for it in this document because none should ever be written — it is the one Trope with no ceiling to meet and no purchase to make, at any Banality, any Domain spread, any tenure of play. This is closer to Golconda or Ascension than to learning a technique. Nobody, including the Dreambound who eventually manages it, can say in advance what made this the night it worked. If a Storyteller decides a character has earned it, that decision belongs entirely to the story that led there — never to a freebie or experience-point ledger.

### One Possible Journey: Wren's Arc

*Not a rule — an illustration, meant to show where the pitfalls actually live.*

**Chargen.** Wren is a sidhe-descended kinain, Wilder, Seelie Court, raised in a Railroad bolthole and drifting, as most do, toward [Project Fractal](Project%20Fractal.md) (favored Domain: Memory). Changeling side built entirely to standard rules — Glamour 5, Banality 3, a light spread of Arts and Realms. Gifted side: Barriers ●1 (mandatory) + Barriers 1 more, Memory 2 (favored), Detection 1, Intrusion 1 — six dots total. **Instrumental Floor: 6 ÷ 3 = 2.** Actual Banality (3) already sits above the floor, so nothing about Wren feels constrained yet — and at Banality 3, every Trope is open to her except Seeing the Thread (ceiling 2). Early play is almost entirely fae-native: Hearing Stories and Entering Stories for free, Reading Stories and Naming Stories earned early, Memory barely touched.

**The turn.** Years into the chronicle, a bolthole-mate is dying by inches to Fray. Wren, out of love and not ambition, starts training seriously — Memory 2→4 over two years of downtime XP, Intrusion 1→4 alongside it (Memory needs Intrusion access to function at all; this was never optional), and a second dot of Barriers for the privacy the work demands. Total Domain dots climb to 12. **Floor: 4.** Nobody chose to become colder. It happened one necessary dot at a time, in service of someone Wren loved. Holding Stories (ceiling 3) is already out of reach; Wren doesn't notice yet.

**Branch A — the pitfall.** Wren keeps going, because the work keeps demanding it: Memory to 5, Intrusion to 5, Control picked up to 5 for good measure, Detection to 4, Barriers to 5. Total dots reach 24. **Floor: 8.** Actual Banality has long since caught up to match it. Cantrips are nearly impossible now; the dream-visits that used to be effortless take real effort to even attempt, and rarer, they simply fail — every Trope above Reading Stories has been closed for years. The night Wren coldly, perfectly erases a captured operative's memory of a safehouse's location — flawless technique, not a flicker of feeling — is the scene the table plays out as the crossing, not a die roll: Banality 10. Wren is, from that day, an excellent, tragic, entirely ordinary Gifted Memory specialist, and does not fully remember what it felt like to be anything else.

**Branch B — the road not taken.** A different telling of the same turn: after the crisis, Wren chooses *not* to master Memory alone — finds another Dreambound to share the burden, leans harder into the fae-native road for the rest of a long, quiet career, and never lets total Domain dots pass eight — barely more than chargen already gave her. Floor never exceeds 2, which is the whole point: Seeing the Thread's ceiling stays within reach for the rest of her life, though the number alone was never going to be enough. Decades later, having spent an entire life refusing to let either half win, and only once the Storyteller judges the balance was actually lived rather than merely low on paper — Wren perceives, for the first time, the shape of a tether inside someone else's dream.

Same character. Same crisis. Two entirely different people by the end, and not one die was rolled to decide which.
