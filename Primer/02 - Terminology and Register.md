# Terminology and Register

*The vocabulary, and why which word you use is a political act. Read this before anything else in the Primer.*

---

Psion has two vocabularies for the same world. Nearly every important thing in it has an institutional name and a human one, and they are not synonyms — they are positions. A character's word choice tells you where they were raised and what they have decided to be. This is the setting's signature texture, and getting it wrong makes everything else read as a different game.

## The table

| The thing | The MetaConcert files it as | Everyone else says |
|---|---|---|
| the people | **the Noetic** | **the Gifted** |
| the ability | **the Noesis** | **the power** |
| the night it starts | **Onset** | **Triggered** |
| when it kills you instead | **Failed Onset** | **misfire** |
| a psionic ability | **protocol** | **technique** |
| the untrained leak state | **Exposed** | **Raw** *(neutral: Unshielded)* |
| a frayed mind's stray emission | **saccade** | **Bleed** |

**Default to the right-hand column.** It is what the book uses, what the Projects use, what a mother uses. The left column belongs to case notes, extraction orders, and Correction paperwork.

**Quotes follow the speaker, without exception.** A Praxis or MetaConcert voice always uses the left column — put *the power* in an Inquisitor's mouth and you have written a character who cannot exist, because the word is a shibboleth. A Project or vernacular voice uses the right. The book's narrator (a Heterodox defector) writes the right column by conviction, but switches to the left the moment he quotes the institution or names its machinery, and lets the coldness of the institutional word do the work. **Register is a border.** When the vocabulary changes, it is telling you whose frame you have crossed into.

## The individual words

**The Gifted.** The community's name for itself, worn with something between pride and irony. Not because they were given anything — because the community needed a name and this one stuck. *You are "Gifted" the way you are "tall": a description of what you are, not a name for what you were given.*

**The Noetic.** What the file calls a Gifted. Praxis coined *Noesis*, derived the adjective, and applied it to the population. A Maintainer says *the Noetic subject is stable.* The subject's family says *my daughter is Gifted.* Same child. The word appears in paperwork and never in a mother's mouth.

**The power** — lowercase, always. The lowercase is the entire point: Praxis capitalizes, and the vernacular declines to dignify it. Saying *the power* instead of *the Noesis* is not ignorance. Most Project members were raised in the lattice and know the word perfectly well. It is refusal — and organized refusal: Project Heterodox engineered the entire counter-vocabulary (*the power*, *Triggered*, *technique*) on purpose, word by word, as the working edge of its argument that Praxis's words are a cage. Saying *the power* is picking up a tool someone forged. It makes Heterodox's whole argument in two syllables, out loud, for free — which is why it has reached far more mouths than the theory behind it ever will.

**The Noesis.** Praxis's word, from the Greek for *direct, unmediated intellectual apprehension* — as against the mediated kind of knowing that must work through an interface, translating intention into paradigm before reality will answer. That is precisely the difference between a Gifted and a mage, and it is why the word names a *mode*, not a possession. Nothing was bestowed. There is no second thing living inside a Gifted. Their tether simply reaches, and the reaching is the Noesis. **Use it sparingly and never eagerly.** It should always cost the speaker something.

**Trigger / to Trigger.** The night the tether first reaches. Not a transformation and not an awakening — nothing arrives that was not already there; a threshold is crossed, usually under trauma, usually between twelve and sixteen, almost never on purpose. *"When did you Trigger?"* is the first question one Gifted asks another, and the answer is always a story. The MetaConcert's word is **Onset**, a noun and never a verb, because a file has no room for a night anyone remembers. *Date of onset: age 12.*

**Misfire.** A Trigger the tether cannot survive: immediately and catastrophically fatal, and distinct from fraying. Fraying is a wound that accumulates; a misfire is a structure that fails on first load. The word is doing what such words do — a child is dead, and the sentence that carries her is short enough to get through. The file says *Failed Onset*.

**Bleed.** Involuntary psionic emission from a badly frayed Gifted: a stray thought broadcast across the lattice, a small object twitching, someone else's surface thoughts leaking in. It is loud, and it is why the frayed cannot keep a secret. The MetaConcert files it as a **saccade**, borrowed from the jerk of the eye between fixations — and the borrowing carries a second meaning the Circuits do not advertise. *The eye is blind during its own saccade.* You do not perceive yours either. Everyone else does.

**Protocol / technique.** Identical things; the word is a shibboleth. To an Inquisitor, Thoughtshield is *the Thoughtshield Barriers Domain protocol*, catalogued and systematized by Praxis. Outside the lattice it is a technique. A character who says *protocol* was raised in the lattice. A character who says *technique* was not — or wants you to think so.

**Correction.** The institutional euphemism, and the setting in one word. **Cognitive Reconstruction** is the specific Praxis procedure. **Sterilization** is the euphemism for disposing of a subject after a failed or rejected Correction — *janitorial*, erasing a mistake the institution made, and carefully distinguished from **termination**, which forestalls a psidolon. The MetaConcert's clean clinical word for cleaning up its own failures.

## Taxonomy versus register

There is a line, and it explains why the book's vocabulary is inconsistent on purpose.

**Taxonomy** has no rival word. *Domains. Barriers. Detection. Intrusion. Kinesis. The tether. The Fraying.* Praxis built these and everyone uses them — including the Projects, including people who hate Praxis — because there is nothing else to say. You cannot refuse a word that has no alternative.

**Register** always has a rival. *Noetic or Gifted. Onset or Triggered. Protocol or technique. The Noesis or the power.* Here there is a choice, and every choice is a small argument.

The book refuses what it can refuse and uses what it cannot. That is not sloppiness. It is the narrator, and it is the thesis. *(See **Voice and Tone**.)*

## More than two registers — but only two are *words*

The table has two columns because those are the two **coined** vocabularies: Praxis built one on purpose, Heterodox built the other on purpose, and each is a fixed, catalogued term that works as a shibboleth. But real speech has more layers than that, and the setting should sound like it does:

- **Psion colloquial.** Off duty, among themselves, psions of any faction reach for neither coined term. They use the plain verb. Nobody at a safehouse says *"I'll deploy Puppeteer"* — they say *"I'll just override the guard."* Lowercase, unbothered, shop-talk. This is why *override* survives as an informal verb even though the technique is catalogued as Puppeteer: it's what the work actually sounds like.
- **Outsiders.** Non-psions have no reason to know any psion word, coined or otherwise. They describe what they see in *their own* frame — and which frame a speaker reaches for tells you who they are. A Kindred reaches for **Dominate**. A mage reaches for the **Mind sphere**. A cop says *"he made me do it."* A frightened mortal says *"mind control,"* or *"hypnosis,"* or *"some Jedi thing."*

**Only the coined two are catalogued.** The other layers are *not* a third and fourth column, because a column implies one fixed word, and natural speech doesn't have one — layer four isn't "mind control," it's *whatever that particular speaker's world happens to call it.* Freezing it would make it false. These layers live in **dialogue**, where the gap between how a psion names a thing and how an outsider names it does the characterization for free.

This is the register system pointing *outward.* Inside the psion world, vocabulary marks your faction. At its boundary, vocabulary marks whether you are a psion at all — and, for an outsider, *which kind* of outsider. The vampire betrays herself by reaching for Dominate; the mage by reaching for a Sphere. You never have to write that down as a rule. You only have to let each character use their own words.

> *"Oh — you mean like Dominate?"* — she maps it onto her nearest frame.
> *"Not quite."* [he explains] — he refines the mapping.
> *"So, basically, full-on mind control."* — she flattens it to the layman's word.
>
> Three lines, and both are drawn — a pragmatist who cares about no one's taxonomy but her own, and a man precise about what he is. Neither says *Puppeteer* or *Cognitive Command*, and that is correct: those words belong to the two factions, and she is neither.

## The two axes — never collapse them

Mage folds *how well an act fits reality* and *what it costs you* onto one line. Psion splits them, and the split is the mechanical heart of the setting.

**Dirty ↔ clean** is the **exposure** axis: what an act leaves behind for someone else to clean up. The MetaConcert, which does the cleaning, files it as *high-* and *low-attribution* — how much of an incident can be traced back to a mind. **Kinesis and Control are dirty.** Memory, Intrusion, Detection, Barriers and Projection are clean. This is about who *noticed*.

**Consonant ↔ dissonant** is the **cost** axis: how the act sits with the will you imposed it on. The tether frays in proportion to the resistance the projection overcomes. *You are not paying for the power — you are paying for the violation.* A nudge someone would have agreed to costs almost nothing. Forcing a man to do what he would sooner die than do is the road to the psidolon.

**These come apart constantly, and the gap is where tradecraft lives.** A consonant nudge in a crowded lobby is free and filthy. An anathema override in a sealed room is a **clean atrocity** — no witness, no cleanup, nothing to file — and it frays you white.

> *Paradox bites when they notice; the tether bites when you force.*

The Gifted who confuse the two do not last. They mistake *nobody saw me* for *it cost me nothing*, and the tether keeps its own books regardless of what the paperwork says.

## Dead words — do not use

- **The Talent** — replaced by *the Noesis* / *the power*. Also collided with the Storyteller System's own Ability category.
- **Expression / to Express** — replaced by *Trigger* / *Onset*.
- **Vulgar / subtle** — those are Mage's words for Mage's single axis. Psion says *dirty* and *clean*.
- **The Gift** — Garou have Gifts.

Ordinary English is untouched. A face has an *expression*; Eve can give Marcus a *gift* of flowers; someone can have a *talent* for negative space. Only the jargon senses are retired.
