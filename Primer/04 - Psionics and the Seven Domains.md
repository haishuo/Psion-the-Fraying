# Psionics and the Seven Domains

*What the power actually is, the seven things it can do, the two axes that price it, and the one room the Gifted own outright.*

---

## Projection

All psionics is **projection**: consciousness leaving the boundary of the body to impose will on reality. Every technique in every Domain is a version of that one act — a mind reaching past the edge of its own skull — and everything else is bookkeeping about how far it reached and what it pushed against.

Understand what that does *not* involve. No gesture. No incantation. No ritual, no muttered word, no moment of visible effort. **The mind is the entire action.** A Gifted standing still with their hands in their pockets, saying nothing, looking at nothing in particular, may be doing the most invasive thing that has ever happened to you. Nothing about it is visible from outside, and it takes no longer than deciding to do it.

The folklore says otherwise, and the folklore is embarrassing: newcomers learn the theatrical hand-wave and the unblinking stare from stories and swear by them, while veterans find the performance quietly mortifying. The wave is not the power. People do it anyway.

This is the Gifted's real advantage, and it is not a big number. It is the absence of a tell.

## The Seven Domains

The power organizes into seven Domains. Every Gifted has aptitude in some and struggles with others; mastery comes through practice, discipline, and accepting what each use costs the tether.

But the *core* of what a psion is comes free, the way a vampire's fangs come free. From the moment they Trigger, every psion **receives** — the surface thoughts, emotions, and images of unshielded minds nearby pour in, genuine and rich. Unbraced, it is a *firehose:* everyone at once, unlabeled, contextless, maddening. That raw reception is the innate floor of **Detection**, and the whole of the "reader" a psion is born as; nothing else in the World of Darkness comes with it. And every *trained* psion learns one thing, taught the way a fledgling vampire is taught to leash its Hunger: **bracing**, the membrane that gates the flood down to something survivable *and* keeps their own mind from leaking out. That is the floor of being Gifted — a receiver who has learned to gate.

Everything above that floor is *earned*, including the wall itself. The seven Domains — **Barriers** (the trained inbound wall), **Memory** to edit a mind, **Control** to seize a body, **Kinesis** to move matter, **Projection** to leave the flesh, and the trained heights of Detection and Intrusion — are all learned. So the truest one-line description of the breed is simpler than it first looks: a psion is, at heart, **a receiver** — and a wall, if they build one.

**Barriers** is the trained mental wall, and it is a different thing from bracing: bracing is the *skin* that regulates your surface both ways (baseline, below the Domains); Barriers is the *armor* against a directed reach past it. It is the art of mental invisibility and defense — muffling surface thought, then hiding from casual scrutiny, then from dedicated Panopticon monitoring, then genuine privacy — which is exhausting to hold, so most Gifted don't, and pay for it in exposure. A psion can even carry Barriers 0: braced and quiet, but with no wall raised, as open to a hostile mind as anyone. It has never quite hidden strong feeling. Barriers also holds the trap and the counterpunch: landmines that hurt whoever reaches in, analgesia that switches off pain (and, disastrously, the body-ache that warns of fraying), and the shield extended over a room, a crew, or a frightened family — the reason a safehouse can be more than four walls, and the reason whoever holds it is so tired.

**Detection** is the outbound read — everything a mind broadcasts — and it is **free.** Not cheap: free. Fraying is the price of *projection*, and reading a broadcast is not a reach; the signal was already arriving, and the Domain only opens a specific gate onto it. Nothing leaves you, so nothing wears. **You cannot fray by listening**, and a psion who only ever listens can live a whole long life undamaged and extremely well informed. (Reading *past* the range the broadcast already reaches you is a different act, and costs. So does putting a sweep out across a city.) **Skim** points the sense at one mind and reads the surface it is leaking: thoughts, feelings, images. Trivial on anyone unbraced (mundanes, sleepers, the Unshielded); silent against the composed, because a braced mind or a held Empty Mind broadcasts nothing to read. **Thoughtsense** is a radar pulse for minds: *here are conscious beings*, vague at first, then a precise map of a city block, then mental signatures across a city. Its siblings read emotion rather than thought (which is how you find a wraith, who has no thoughts and is nothing but feeling), sense psionics in use nearby, and identify what kind of thing you are looking at — Kindred feel *wrong*, dead thoughts; Garou feel *wild*; mages feel *fractured*. **Trace** turns all of it into an address: psionic use leaves a wake, and a specialist reads that wake the way a tracker reads bent grass. This is why the loudest mistake a fugitive can make is simply to use their power — and why a psion in a crowd is forever swimming in everyone's surface.

**Intrusion** is the reach *past* the surface — not what a mind shows but what it holds back: the lie under the calm answer, the feeling being suppressed, the buried memory. **Dig** is the baseline reach, and the access that Memory and Control require; it scales to targeted extraction (*where is the safe house*), then, on a target who cannot resist, to the deep dive that dredges buried memory and can break the mind it dredges. Skim reads the mask; Dig reads beneath it. This is the act Barriers defends against.

**And here is the single most important fact about the Gifted, the one that makes them a distinct thing and not a variety of mage: Intrusion is not resisted by Willpower.** A mind defended by will alone is *open.* Willpower is the general armor of the World of Darkness — excellent against everything built to attack the will (coercion, temptation, being ordered about), and useless here, because Intrusion does not attack the will; it goes *past* it to the mind itself. The only thing that keeps a psion out is a **purpose-built mental defense actively in place** — another Gifted's Barriers, a mage's *raised* mind-shield, an Akashic's held Empty Mind. Absent one of those, a neonate vampire, a Garou in Homid, a non-Mind mage, and a sleeping child are all *equally* open — not because any of them is weak, but because their formidable wills are the wrong armor for the one attack only the Gifted ever made. The test is never *how strong is the will;* it is *is there a wall, and is it up.* This is exactly why psionics is not Mind-sphere-without-Paradox: a mage reading a sleeper still rolls, can fail, risks Paradox, and can be refused by that sleeper's Willpower. A psion's read is none of those. It simply happens — and costs the psion, not the target, through the Fraying. Not all of it is violation: **Mindspeech** is real conversation mind-to-mind, effortless between willing minds — and unmistakable when forced, because a whisper inside the skull is very hard to mistake for your own thought. **Mirage** overlays a mind's live perception: not invisibility, which people notice the absence of, but becoming *not worth noticing* — the guard's eyes pass over the open door, and no alarm sounds because as far as every living mind in the building is concerned there is nothing to sound one about. Its great humility is the camera. Mirage fools minds and only minds, and more than one operative has slipped past every guard in a building and been undone by a thirty-dollar webcam.

**Memory** is the surgeon's scalpel: erase a memory, alter its details, fabricate a new one, or — at **Memory Rewrite** — reconstruct the whole architecture, building elaborate false histories over hours of patient work. It also turns inward: seal your own memories behind a lock even you cannot open, so a captured agent has nothing to surrender that they can reach, or hold information as a stable indexed construct — the mind-palace, which is how the Preservers *are* the MetaConcert's archive, and how one apostate keeps his murdered sister.

**Control** runs from a whispered nudge to total bodily seizure: slipping a plausible suggestion into a compliant mind, altering emotion directly, planting a compulsion — and, at its far end, **Puppeteer**, the technique the Domain is named for. Puppeteer is not a command. It is *continuous operation*: you drive another person's body, moment to moment, as if it were your own, for as long as you concentrate. It enters through the mind but does not alter it — Intrusion opens the connection, and Puppeteer follows it *past cognition* to the body's motor controls, reading nothing and editing nothing, which is exactly why the mind stays awake to witness. They feel their body stop being theirs and remember every second. This is the exact inverse of vampiric Dominate, which commands the *will* (one instruction, the victim's own mind carries it out, and the Blood can erase the memory of it). Puppeteer leaves the will intact and *imprisons* it — an aware mind made to watch its own hands obey.

The defense is different too, and it is the whole terror. Dominate is resisted by will: a strong enough will refuses the command. Puppeteer cannot be resisted that way, because it never addresses the will — once someone is past your Barriers, your Willpower is powerless, screaming in a room it has been locked out of. The only defense is the wall at the border. Let them in and it is already over. It is why the Domain is called Control and not Domination, why it makes the angriest witnesses in the world, and why it is the single most violating thing a Gifted can do.

**Projection** separates consciousness from body. **Astral Step** goes from the next room to across a continent to, at its limit, the far places of the Umbra; lesser forms extend awareness without full separation, or walk into a sleeping mind and speak to the dreamer unseen. Your body lies somewhere undefended the entire time. And if the tether snaps while you are out there, you do not come back — you are a psidolon, immediately and forever. Many Gifted refuse to learn this Domain at all.

**Kinesis** moves matter with thought: coins, then people, then cars, then buildings. It also stops a bullet, either with a sustained force wall or with a single desperate reflexive swat. It is the iconic power and the loudest, and it costs more tether than anything else the Gifted can do.

**One rule of grammar governs all of it: Memory and Control require Intrusion first.** This is not a licensing restriction; it is a fact about how minds work. You cannot edit or seize what you are not inside. Memory mastery with no Intrusion is not a specialist — it is a surgeon with no way into the room.

## Two Axes, Never One

The Gifted price an act twice, on two axes that must never be collapsed.

**Dirty and clean** is about **exposure**: what an act leaves behind for someone else to clean up. (The MetaConcert, which does the cleaning, files it as *high-* and *low-attribution*: how much of an incident traces back to a mind.) **Kinesis** is the worst offender — flying objects, physical evidence, witnesses by the dozen. *Great, you threw a car. Now we need Memory teams for an entire city block.* **Control** is nearly as bad in a different way: the victim knows, and remembers, and is telling the police about mind control. Everything else is clean. Memory is self-cleaning, because a mind accepts a false memory as its own; Intrusion leaves nothing if you're careful; Detection leaves nothing at all; Barriers leaves nothing unless someone attacks you; Projection is invisible until you touch something.

The MetaConcert's hierarchy of respect follows exactly from this, and reads like what it is — a bureaucracy grading its own cleanup burden. Memory first: *the scalpel — precise, elegant, self-cleaning.* Then Intrusion (*necessary for Memory*), Detection (*no cleanup needed; professional*), Barriers (*shows discipline*), Projection (*useful, but risky to yourself*), Control (*amateur hour; creates witnesses*), and at the bottom, Kinesis: *might as well announce PSIONICS HERE with fireworks.* Note what is not being graded. Not harm. Not consent. Paperwork.

**Consonant and dissonant** is about **cost**: how hard you pushed against the will you imposed on. A consonant act — a nudge in the direction someone was already going — is nearly free. Coercion frays you, and the more the mind under your hand refuses, the worse it frays you.

*Paradox bites when they notice; the tether bites when you force.* The two axes come apart constantly, and the gap between them is where tradecraft lives. A consonant nudge in a crowded lobby is free and filthy. An anathema override in a sealed room is a **clean atrocity** — no witness, no cleanup, nothing to file — and it frays you white. The Gifted who confuse the two do not last: they mistake *nobody saw me* for *it cost me nothing*, and the tether keeps its own books regardless of what the paperwork says.

## Broadcast, and the Law It Creates

The power was never point-to-point. Every unshielded mind pours its surface out for free, and a psion standing in a crowd receives *all of them at once* — the channel was always one-to-many, and simply spent most of its history pointed inward. Turn it outward and you get **broadcast**: one act, every mind in reach, at the same instant.

The cost is set by the **instruction**, not the audience. The useful comparison is a graphics processor — you do not address the thousands of cores one at a time; you hand them one instruction and they all run it at once, and it is fast precisely because nothing branches. As long as the instruction is identical for every mind and needs to know nothing about any of them, five thousand and five million are not meaningfully different jobs.

*"You did not see that."* Identical for everyone, needs to know nothing about anybody. A master can lay it across a plaza, and it costs real tether, and it is entirely payable.

*"Forget the man in the grey coat."* The grey coat is filed in five thousand different places in five thousand different lives. That is not one instruction. That is five thousand jobs, and nobody has that much tether.

**Which produces the law that shapes everything: violation cannot be parallelized.** Overcoming a will is a contest with one particular person, and a contest is not an instruction. So the entire coercive end of the power — everything that actually frays you toward the psidolon — is permanently, structurally *retail*. **You can make a city forget. You will never make a city obey.**

Nobody designed that mercy. It is a property of the machinery. And it is why the MetaConcert's authority over its own people has to be assembled out of leverage, dependency, Correction and the Inquisition instead of a single broadcast order — their tyranny is *labour-intensive*. If it were not, there would be no Projects left to write this.

This is also the sharpest line between a Gifted and a mage. A mage reaching a crowd fights arithmetic and Paradox at once, and hits a trap with no floor: **you cannot fix vulgar with vulgar**, because the cover-up is witnessed by the same crowd that witnessed the thing being covered. The Gifted has no such loop. The power raises no Paradox; the cost is private, and paid to the tether.

## The One Room They Own

Ask the oldest question in a crossover: *who do I least want to use mind control on?* The answer is a trained psion. Not one of several strong answers. **The** answer.

The crucial part is *how* they own it: **not numerically — categorically.** An Ahroun doesn't own melee by having a bigger pool; he owns it because swinging on him is how you die. Every other school of mental intrusion in the World of Darkness is a latecomer or a copy — Mind is one Sphere among nine, Dominate one Discipline in a rack, and Dominate itself is a diluted, instinctualized translation of the Gifted's own Override, handed to the Kindred in deep antiquity and reshaped by the medium of Blood until its makers were forgotten. The Gifted cut the original key, then built an entire civilization around not letting anyone else in.

So Barriers are kevlar — and being extraordinarily good at stopping bullets is not a flaw in kevlar, it is the specification, met. Every other splat is a knight in plate: their mind-attacks are arrows, superb against the armor of *their* world, which is Willpower. The Gifted walked onto that field with the next era's kit. Their Intrusion is the rifle that goes through plate; their Barriers are the vest cut for exactly that rifle. The knight who picks this fight is caught twice, and he is not weak — he is *anachronistic.* And kevlar stops bullets, not knives: the psion who cannot be touched inside his own skull is still the aging mortal who dies to a blade he never saw. Total in the one domain it was built for, worthless everywhere else. Both sentences are the same fact.

It is not impossible to breach — it is *hazardous.* A determined Archmage of Mind, or a Methuselah spending real capital, can in principle pierce a master's Thoughtshield. What none of them can do is pierce it **casually**. There is no rote for it, because the Barrier is not a static lock that a known key opens; it is an active, adaptive defense that changes shape as it is pressed. Getting through means improvising at the limit of your art, at full risk and no guarantee. A siege, not a spell.

And the door swings both ways. Throw a punch at a Garou and miss, and you have missed. Reach into a trained psion's mind and fail, and **you have opened a line, and it runs back to you.** Barriers alone can only hurt you and throw you out — but the psion's *other* Domains can walk back down the channel you just opened. God help whoever reaches into a master of Barriers and Memory, who catches the intrusion, rides the line back, and edits your recollection of ever having tried. The deterrent is not *you probably won't get in.* It is *you probably won't get in, and if you don't, you've handed a mind-surgeon a live channel into yours.*

Two things keep this honest. It **degrades along the Fraying axis** — the whole tragedy in miniature: the people who should be untouchable, undone by the slow failure of the one thing they were ever good at. And **the Dreaming walks straight through it.** A Dreambound's abilities are Glamour-derived and sideways to the entire Domain framework — not a stronger arrow, not an arrow at all. They do not contest the wall; they were never on the axis the wall defends. That is not a hole in the ruling. It is the ruling's horror: *the best defense in the World of Darkness, and the thing in the corner is not even using the door.*

## What Tether Sense Was Built For

The fraying gauge is the day job. **Tether Sense is a psidolon detector.**

It reads the metaphysical integrity — and the material — of a tether. Panopticon uses it to flag Gifted for White Rooms before they snap, and that is what it's known for. But everything living has a tether, and the Embrace reforges rather than deletes one, so the technique returns *something* on nearly everything with a mind: a mundane's hemp, a Gifted's steel, a Kindred's burned-and-recast third thing that is plainly there and plainly not living. None of that is an alarm.

The alarm is the reading that comes back **empty**. No tether where one must be. The answer is absolute because nothing else in the world gives it — and it is absolute *only* because everything else has one.

So a Panopticon analyst can sweep a room full of Kindred, read seven wrong-but-present tethers without a flicker of concern, and then go rigid at the eighth chair, where a charming man is telling a story and the world says there is no one sitting there.
