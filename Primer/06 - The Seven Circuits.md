# The Seven Circuits

*The MetaConcert's seven organs: what each one does, which Domain it carries, and the specific horror of the job.*

---

A Circuit is not a faction you join for its politics. It is an organ of the MetaConcert — a function the institution needed badly enough to build a culture around. There are seven, founded in order, each solving a problem the previous ones created: the Inquisition, Praxis, the Maintainers, the Panopticon, the Integrators, the Preservers, the Operatives. A Gifted's Circuit is the closest thing this world has to a clan or a tribe. It determines who trained you, whose ledger your name sits in, what you were taught not to look at, and which of your colleagues will be sent to kill you.

Each Circuit is paired with one of the seven Domains. The pairing is thematic, not exclusive — it says what the Circuit *is*, not what it is permitted to use. Every Circuit trains generalists; an Inquisitor who could only hit things would be a poor Inquisitor. The Domain is the Circuit's character rendered as a category of power.

## The Inquisition — Kinesis

*"Mercy is a bullet before the monster is born."*

Enforcement. They execute critically frayed Gifted, eliminate psidolons, dismantle Project cells, hunt apostates, and staff the White Rooms where the killing is done under clinical light. The MetaConcert could have distributed the work — let the Panopticon act on its own flags, let the Maintainers handle threats to the lattice. Instead it concentrated the killing in one institution, so that the Integrator who teaches a child to brace is not also the person who executes that child twenty years later. The Inquisition understands this perfectly. They exist so the rest of the MetaConcert can do gentler work without looking up.

Kinesis is the most straightforward pairing in the structure. The Inquisition applies force and does not need a metaphor for killing, because it does not pretend killing is something else. Destroying a psidolon is actually Memory work — dismantling the thing's accumulated memories until nothing coheres — and Kinesis is only what keeps everyone alive long enough for that. But the first thing you see is force, and that is not an accident.

The doctrine is Praxis's: ten Gifted executed on suspicion for every psidolon prevented. Inquisitors know, statistically, that most of the people they kill would not have Untethered. They do it anyway. Whether the math is right is a question the Inquisition does not ask and Praxis does not revisit — and it cannot be asked, because **the number has no denominator.** A prevented psidolon is a thing that did not happen to a person who is not here. You cannot count them. You cannot exhume them. The entire evidence that a monster was averted is that no monster arrived, which is also precisely what you would see if none had been coming. Ten to one is not a measurement; it is a doctrine wearing a number, issued once by the Circuit that had already lied about its archive, and never audited since because there has never been anything to audit. The Circuit selects for people who feel the weight and act regardless — feel it, file it, act. An Inquisitor who feels nothing is a liability. So is one who feels too much.

They are the MetaConcert's most honest institution. Not the most ethical — the most honest. They do not launder the killing through euphemism the way the institution launders Correction. Every kill is documented with unsentimental precision, not out of conscience but because losing count would be the beginning of losing themselves. The horror is not that the Circuit contains monsters. It is that it contains people doing something monstrous for reasons they have examined carefully and found sufficient.

Their oldest quarrel is with Praxis, and beneath the professional respect is a contempt they would never log in a report. *It is always Praxis's idea* — the lattice they built and abandoned, the doctrine they wrote and never had to pull the trigger on. But the coldest part is simpler than grievance: the Inquisition kills people and *knows* they are people. Praxis looks at the same Gifted and sees a specimen. *The Circuit that pulls the trigger has never once stopped seeing the one it points at. The Circuit that would save you stopped seeing you a long time ago.*

## Praxis — Control

*"Knowledge is the antidote to chaos."*

Research. Praxis built almost everything: the Domain taxonomy itself, the lattice, bracing, the tether strain classifications, Cognitive Reconstruction, the actuarial studies the Inquisition's doctrine rests on. The word is deliberate — not research in the abstract but *applied practice*, the loop between theory and intervention. Praxis does not observe. It intervenes, and then it writes down what happened.

**And here is the key to all of it: Praxis wants research and nothing else, and the enemy of that is its own output.** A discovery that works stops being a discovery and becomes a thing that must be run, maintained, taught, explained and governed — every hour of it subtracted from the next question. So for Praxis, uniquely, **a completed success is not an asset. It is a debt**, and the whole recorded behaviour of the Circuit is an institution discharging that debt onto anyone who will take it. They built the lattice and would not run it. They caused a government and would not name it. They were handed the children and taught them badly and resented every hour.

The consequence is what this setting is about. Praxis is not *indifferent* to what its work is used for — indifference implies a decision. **Praxis is absent**, because caring what a thing becomes means staying with the finished thing, and staying is exactly what they are built to escape. The lattice became a surveillance state, the taxonomy a cage, Cognitive Reconstruction became Correction — and in every case Praxis had already gone. **A villain who chooses can be argued with. Praxis never chose; Praxis never stayed**, and that has done more damage than malice ever managed.

It is also why they have never ruled anything, and why three of the seven Circuits exist in the negative space of one refusal: the **Maintainers** because Praxis would not run the lattice, the **Panopticon** because Praxis would not watch it, the **Integrators** because Praxis would not teach the children it produced.

Which is why their Domain is Control and not Detection. The Panopticon watches; Praxis acts on what it finds. Watching a system tells you what it does. Intervening tells you what it *is*. Control is the Domain of acting on a mind rather than reading it — the most morally scrutinized power the institution has — and Praxis holds it because they have measured what having your will overridden feels like from the inside. They also invented the language every Gifted uses to understand themselves. That is an extraordinary and largely invisible form of power, and Praxis has a classified paper about it.

The thing that separates them from every other dangerous power in this world is not cruelty. It is the question. Ordinary monsters ask *how does this work, how can it be used* — terrible but bounded; capability has a destination. Praxis asks: *how much can a mind be altered and still be a mind? What is the most you can take out of a person before the person is gone?* Questions about the floor of personhood, asked the way one measures the tensile strength of a cable — by pulling until it parts, and writing down the number. And it is the writing-down that curdles the blood. No rage, no ideology. Only measurement.

They also keep the Black Archive: the record of what the Circuit did before any doctrine existed, burned off paper and encoded into the minds of a handful of senior members, off the lattice, unreachable. They do not hoard it out of pride. They hoard it because it is a bomb beneath their own floor — expose it and the other six Circuits unite, with the Inquisition leading the column. The secrecy is not arrogance. It is survival.

## The Maintainers — Barriers

*"We hold the network. We hold you."*

Infrastructure. The lattice is not a web minds fall into naturally; it is thousands of individually installed binding constructs, each degrading if untended. The Maintainers build it connection by connection, monitor it continuously, and manage severances so a death does not cascade into the connections beside it — a forcible severance ruptures rather than fades, and the shockwave kills Gifted who were nowhere near it. The lattice has never *just worked*. It works because Maintainers are watching it, including at three in the morning.

Barriers surprises people who expect the lattice engineers to be Projection or Memory. But Barriers is the Domain of structure, containment, endurance — of building something that absorbs shock without failing. The lattice is the largest, most continuously maintained Barrier construct in the world. It just doesn't look like a wall from the outside. Neither do the Maintainers: not dramatic, they do not strike, they simply endure, and everything the MetaConcert does depends on that endurance being continuous.

Their horror is quiet and total. Every binding a Maintainer installs is a choice to attach another person — usually a child, incapable of meaningful consent — to the surveillance network, permanently. And the architecture does not permit selective maintenance. The same infrastructure that catches a fraying Gifted before they Untether monitors dissidents; the same construct that connects a child to other minds makes that child permanently addressable. You cannot keep the parts you approve of running and shut down the rest. The Maintainers have done the math. The math is why they stay.

With Praxis the relationship is the most complicated in the MetaConcert: Praxis conceived the lattice, proved it worked, decided maintenance was beneath its attention, and walked. The Maintainers hold the engineering; Praxis holds the theory. When something breaks at a level the documentation doesn't explain, the Maintainers must go and ask — which requires admitting they do not fully understand what they maintain.

They are the Circuit most dangerous to underestimate. If they simply *stopped* — not rebelled, stopped — the MetaConcert would degrade to incapacity within weeks. Every Circuit knows this. Nobody discusses it, because discussion would turn a structural dependency into a political threat, and the Maintainers have no interest in being one. They are engineers. They want to be left alone.

## The Panopticon — Detection

*"We see what you cannot hide."*

Surveillance. They monitor the lattice for critical fraying, run the sweeps that find newly Triggered children by their Unshielded signature, track the Projects, and keep a file on every initiated Gifted alive. The name is not a metaphor they borrowed; Bentham's design is a specification they implement. They cannot watch everyone at once — but any Gifted can be watched at any moment, and no Gifted can know whether they are being watched now. The surveillance does not need to be total to be totalizing. It only needs to be possible.

Detection is the second-most obvious pairing after Kinesis, and the lesson is in the scale. A Gifted who learns Empathic Sense to better understand a friend has learned the same technique the Panopticon uses to decide whether to flag someone for the Inquisition. They have not corrupted Detection; they have revealed what Detection always was at sufficient scale. The line between care and control is not a line between capabilities. It is a line between institutional contexts for the same capability.

The sweeps are their strongest justification and their sharpest indictment. They genuinely find children in distress — and the same analysts, the same coverage, the same funding justification also deliver adult apostates to the Inquisition. The Panopticon that saves children is the Panopticon that hunts fugitives. Not because the two are indistinguishable, but because the moral authority of the first provides political cover for the second.

Their damage is the slow kind. No analyst kills anyone directly. What happens instead is the erosion of the capacity to relate to other minds as private. They know what a subject is worried about, who they love, the shape of their anxiety at 3 AM when the Barriers thin and surface thoughts get honest. They file it. They cannot not-perceive; even off duty the habits persist.

Defections are rare and always personal. A Panopticon defector does not leave because they discovered the system is unjust; they knew that when they joined. They leave because one specific case resolved the abstraction into a person they could not send. The system survives moral objection. It cannot survive *not this one.*

## The Integrators — Intrusion

*"We shape the mind. We save the life."*

Education. They find the newly Triggered child, resolve the Unshielded state through bracing, teach the foundations, and prepare the child for binding. They are the warm, patient presence a frightened kid first encounters — the ones who sit on the floor and say *I know this is scary. You're going to be okay.* Many of them mean it. That is the Circuit's defining characteristic and its defining problem: they are genuinely kind people doing genuinely kind work inside an institution that will use everything they build in ways they did not intend and cannot prevent.

Intrusion is the most productive tension in the structure — the invasive Domain, the one every Gifted learns to defend against first, held by the kindest Circuit. But you have to read someone to teach them. Bracing *is* an Intrusion: the Integrator enters the child's mind gently and holds, so the child can feel what a foreign presence feels like, and then says *push me out.* The child must produce the boundary themselves; it cannot be installed. It is the one genuinely collaborative moment in MetaConcert practice. It is also the same technique that will be used on that child if they are ever interrogated. The Integrators know. They use it the way a doctor uses a scalpel, aware that the distinction depends entirely on the hand.

They do the worst thing in the book, and they do it by not being in the room. When a child is integrated, the family is corrected — a false memory of the child's death, or complete erasure of the child's existence. The Operatives perform it; the Integrators are told it has been handled. It is not an emergency measure and not a threshold requiring authorization. It is a checklist item. The training manual calls it mercy, and the word is not cynical — it is believed. This is not evil requiring evil people. It is evil requiring people who are good at their jobs.

Their friction with the Inquisition is the ugliest in the institution. Before the Integrators existed, the Inquisition handled newly Triggered children by default — threat assessment first, from people trained to kill threats. When it was finally said out loud, every other Circuit reacted with horror. The Inquisition's response — *we've been handling it fine* — remains the most revealing thing they have ever said about themselves. The friction point now: an Inquisitor dispatched to handle someone an Integrator trained. The Integrator remembers teaching that person to brace. The Inquisitor does not have the luxury of remembering.

And the kindness is the trap. A Gifted's foundational memory of the institution is *someone helped me when I was scared* — true, genuine, ineradicable, and the MetaConcert's most effective defense against dissent. The Integrators cannot stop being kind without ceasing to be Integrators. They have the highest defection rate in the institution, and the defector always has one specific student in mind.

## The Preservers — Memory

*"Memory is power. We keep both."*

The archive — except the word misleads, because the Preservers do not keep paper. They keep the record the way a Memory specialist keeps anything: inside their own minds, as living constructs behind Barriers no lattice sweep reaches. Documents exist — working copies, the editable surface the rest of the institution reads. The real record is the Preservers themselves. They are not the keepers of the archive. They are the archive.

Memory is the most thematically complete pairing. The specialist who rewrites a witness's recollection and the Preserver who rewrites an institutional record perform the same act at different scales; one operates on a mind, the other on an organization. And so the MetaConcert is, in an uncomfortably literal sense, a subject of its own Memory Domain: it makes decisions based on a past curated to produce those decisions, and does not know its memory has been altered.

When the MetaCouncil orders an edit, they implement it — with the precision of forgers, adjusting language, shifting emphasis, cross-referencing until the new version looks organic. And they keep the original. Not in a drawer: in their own head, carried beside the lie. *They are scholars who burn books — except the books are memories and the fire is their own Domain.* They are the only people in the institution who know, with certainty, which version is real.

Their danger runs on its own axis. Holding an encoded construct costs almost nothing; a Preserver can carry a wing of the archive for forty years and fray no faster than a clerk. But a mind has a ceiling. Every construct is *borrowed* — tagged as archive, not self — and that tag is what a lifetime of training is really for. Packed past capacity, the tag wears through, the borrowed memory crowds the self, and the keeper drowns in the record. The Preservers do not fray. They **saturate** — and a mind saturated past recovery arrives at the same cliff every other Gifted reaches by strain. So no one holds everything; each keeper carries a bounded wing, and a keeper nearing the ceiling must offload into a younger mind. A Preserver who dies with an untransferred wing takes it with them, and the institution suffers a genuine, unrecoverable amnesia — a decade, a doctrine, simply gone, recoverable from no shelf because there was never a shelf.

The quiet horror they do not say aloud: the discipline that decides a keeper is *full* can also order them to let go — to surrender, on schedule, a wing they have carried until it feels like their own. The memory-keepers are the one Circuit that can be compelled, by their own colleagues and for their own good, to forget.

An archive that edits on command corrodes everyone who must use it. Praxis keeps its own research records; the Inquisition keeps its own kill documentation, because it does not trust anyone else to preserve the unedited count. Circuits all keeping their own books, none confident in the central version — and the Preservers find this a professional insult and an accurate one.

## The Operatives — Projection

*"In the shadows, we keep the peace."*

The boundary. They handle diplomacy with the Camarilla, intelligence on the Technocracy, the barely functional Garou channels, Masquerade cleanup, infiltration of Project cells — and the parental corrections. Every other Circuit works inside the institution. The Operatives work outside it, in spaces where the MetaConcert's authority does not reach and survival depends on being someone other than who you are. This makes them simultaneously the most autonomous and the most isolated Circuit.

Projection is the cleanest metaphor in the structure, and the Operatives find it more uncomfortable than poetic. Projection extends consciousness beyond its natural anchor; the further out, the more strain, the nearer the snap that makes you something other than what you were. The Operative extended from the institution for years is under the analogous strain. Going native is the Operative's version of Untethering — the moment the extended consciousness decides it belongs where it is rather than where it came from.

For deep assignments they carry the identity overlay — a Praxis technique derived from the study of how a psidolon suppresses its host. The operative's true self is suppressed beneath a constructed one, with a restoration trigger the psidolon mechanism lacks. They do not *perform* loyalty to the New World Order; they *are* loyal, until the handler says otherwise. Mind-scanning finds exactly what it expects, because what it finds is real. What nobody discusses: a consciousness suppressed beneath another consciousness erodes, for the same structural reasons a host erodes. The overlay is less destructive than possession. The dynamic is the same.

Some Operatives sent into the Projects went native and took the technique with them. Project Parasyte is the result — the MetaConcert's own infiltration tool turned against it, defeating surveillance the technique was built to defeat. The Panopticon's systems assume overlays are friendly, because overlays are what Operatives use. Not an oversight; a design feature that became a vulnerability.

They have the highest rate of unclear defections. When an Operative disappears, nobody knows whether they are dead, captured, or switched. Sometimes the Operative is not sure either. The institution needs them to go deep enough to understand, cannot afford them going so deep they are convinced, and has never found a reliable way to ensure they come back from the right side of that line.
