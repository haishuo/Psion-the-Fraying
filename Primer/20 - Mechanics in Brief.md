# Mechanics in Brief

*The one crunch file. Everything else in this Primer states rules as facts about reality; this states them as rules, so that questions about how the game actually resolves can be answered without the full rulebooks. It is a **reference, not a replacement** — worked examples, edge cases and Storyteller guidance live in* The Seven Domains — Game Mechanics*,* The Fraying — Game Mechanics*,* Character Creation*,* Combat and Conflict *and* Antagonists*. Where this file is thin, those are thick.*

*Classic World of Darkness throughout: d10 pools, successes counted, 1s cancel.*

---

## 1. Rolling

**Attribute + Domain vs difficulty 6** unless an entry says otherwise. Successes set degree.

- **Scaling** techniques grow with the Domain rating — rating is the ceiling, successes are the reach within it.
- **Threshold** techniques need a minimum rating to learn; once known they simply function.
- **Botches** bite: feedback, a slammed connection, or — at Stage 3+ — an involuntary **Bleed** (loud, detectable, +1 Strain).

**No gestures, words or focuses are required.** Psionics is thought; there is no external tell. A focus may earn −1 difficulty if it genuinely aids concentration, and does nothing otherwise.

## 2. What resists — the keystone

**The most counterintuitive rule in the game, and the one most often got wrong.**

> **Raw Willpower does not resist Intrusion, Memory, or Puppeteer.** A mind guarded by will alone is *open*, and the psion rolls **unopposed**.

What actually cancels successes is a **purpose-built mental defence that is currently up**:
- another Gifted's **Barriers** (reflexive **Wits + Barriers**)
- a mage's **actively raised** Mind-sphere shield or countermagic
- a held **Empty Mind**
- a comparable ward the Storyteller rules purpose-built and switched on

Age, sheer will, and being supernatural do **not** count. The test is *is there a wall, and is it up* — never *how strong is the will.* A Methuselah with no active mental Discipline is as open as a sleeper; a mortal monk holding Empty Mind is not.

**Three-tier Willpower rule:**

| | Willpower applies? |
|---|---|
| **Access** (Intrusion) | **No.** Only a wall resists. |
| **Will-targeting Control** (Suggestion, Compulsion, Emotion) | **Yes** — the target may spend or roll Willpower to refuse an *objectionable* instruction, wall or no wall. |
| **Will-bypassing** (reading, Memory-editing, Puppeteer) | **No**, even after access is held. |

**Two deliberate exceptions** (do not "fix" these): **the Fugue** and **Possession** are both resisted by **Willpower + Barriers**, because both are mutual struggles rather than one-way reaches.

**Being resisted is always felt.** Rolling Barriers against an incoming technique registers as contact, win or lose. This does *not* apply to the MetaConcert's ambient lattice access, which runs through the binding construct rather than a technique that must beat your wall. *You can always tell when a person just tried you. You can never tell when the lattice is simply there.*

**The Intrusion prerequisite.** Any **Memory or Control** technique requires **held Intrusion access** — succeed on **Dig** (or stronger) and keep concentrating. Reading a surface with **Skim** is *not* access. Lose concentration or range and all Memory and Control work ends instantly.

**The innate floor is Detection, and only Detection.** Below any dot, a psion *receives*: an unbraced one takes in the genuine surface cognition of every unshielded mind nearby — rich, true, and unusable, because it arrives indiscriminate and unattributed. Dots buy **processing**, never signal. **Intrusion has no floor.**

**Bracing vs Barriers.** *Bracing* is the **skin** — free, below the dot scale, bidirectional, gates the inbound flood and contains the outbound broadcast in one act. *Barriers* is the **armour** — the trained inbound wall, rated 0–5. A **braced, Barriers-0 psion is as open to a Dig as any sleeper**: quiet, but unwalled. An **Unshielded** psion has neither.

## 3. The Fraying engine

### What fraying is

> **Fraying is the cost of projection, and of nothing else.**

| | What it is | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Reception** | The world arriving at you. Nothing leaves. | **Nothing.** No Strain, no Fray. |
| **Containment** | Holding your own boundary. Nothing leaves. | **Strain that never becomes Fray.** |
| **Projection** | Reaching outward past yourself. | **Strain, escalating to Fray.** |

**Reception is free** — the whole Detection Domain. You cannot fray by listening. *Exceptions, both of which are reaching:* reading past the range the broadcast already reaches you, and **Trace**.

**Containment tires but does not scar** — bracing is free outright; holding a **Barriers** wall costs Strain that never escalates and clears entirely with rest. No empty box to pay it? The wall does not go up.

**Everything else frays**: Intrusion, Memory, Control, Projection, Kinesis, the *offensive* uses of Barriers (Mind Barbs, Feedback, Aegis over others), and **originating a broadcast**.

### The track

Ten boxes. **Strain `/`** (bashing analog, rests off) · **Fray `X`** (lethal analog, permanent at the deep end). Fill from the left.

**Escalation:** Strain marked on a box that already holds Strain becomes **Fray** instead. *(Exception: containment Strain never escalates.)*

### Cost per projective use

**Base = the Domain's Strain Rating**, then the disposition dial:

| Rating | Domains |
|:---:|---|
| **0** | Detection — reception is free |
| **1** | Barriers *(defensive; never escalates)* |
| **2** | Intrusion, Memory |
| **3** | Control, Projection |
| **4** | Kinesis |

| Disposition | Test: *would they have said yes if asked aloud?* | Modifier |
|---|---|---|
| **Consonant** | already inclined | **−2**, floor 0 |
| **Neutral** | no stake | base |
| **Dissonant · Contrary** | would refuse | **+1** (+2 strongly) |
| **Dissonant · Anathema** | would sooner die — self-harm, betraying a loved one, unmaking the core self | **+4 and an immediate Snap Check** |

*Kinesis has no will to consult; its disposition is physics — riding momentum is consonant, arresting mass head-on is dissonant.*

**Cumulative modifiers:** Push +1 · Sustained +1/scene (+1/turn in combat) · Desperate +1 / Panicked +2 · Range +1/band · Broadcast +1 per order of magnitude of audience (max +4).

**Cleverness lowers it.** A genuinely more efficient method backed by a relevant Ability cuts **1–2** (floor 1).

**Holding is not sustaining.** A set construct — mind-palace, Lockbox, installed binding construct — costs to encode and to open, and **nothing to hold**. This is why a Preserver carries a lifetime's archive without fraying.

**Strain is paid on the attempt, success or failure.**

### Stages

| Boxes | Stage | Penalty | Detection |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Minimal | none | invisible |
| 3–4 | Moderate | −1 psionic | Tether Sense, if actively examining |
| 5–6 | Severe | −2 all | **auto-flagged** by ambient sweeps |
| 7–8 | Critical | −3 all | flagged + actively monitored. **Any psionic use forces a Snap Check.** |
| 9 | Deep | −4 all | as above. **A severe shock alone forces a Snap Check.** |
| 10 | The Snap | — | Untethering |

### The Snap Check

**Triggered when** the tenth box would be marked · *any* psionic use at Stage 4 · a major shock at Stage 4.

**Roll Tether + Stamina, difficulty 8. Willpower may not be added or spent.** One success holds (take +1 Fray). Failure = **Untethering**:

- **Highest Domain 4+** → the character becomes a **psidolon**, and passes to the Storyteller.
- **Highest Domain 3 or lower** → the consciousness **dissipates**. Dead.

**No-room spiral:** if a held check would force Fray and every box is already Fray, the next trigger of any kind is an automatic failure.

### Recovery

- **Strain:** remove **(Tether) boxes** per full day of complete rest from all *projective* use. One reach resets the day. Light activity clears half. **Detection does not break rest** — reception costs nothing, so a convalescing psion may read all day.
- **Fray:** roughly **one box per month**, at Storyteller discretion. The final one or two boxes earned at the brink **never clear**.
- **Nothing else works.** No Domain, drug, therapy, ritual or Willpower expenditure repairs a tether. The sole exception is a Dreambound's tether-mend, which is story-gated and cannot be bought.

**Cascade:** a lattice Snap or death nearby costs bystanders **2 Strain** (3 if bonded).

## 4. Broadcast

**One act, every mind in reach, at once.** The cost tracks the **instruction**, not the audience.

**The convergence test.** A broadcast carries only an instruction **identical for every mind that consults no individual's contents.** If resolving it means entering a particular head — finding a specific memory, overcoming *that* person's will — it **diverges**, and becomes as many jobs as there are people.

| Converges | Diverges |
|---|---|
| *"You did not see that."* | *"Forget Marcus Thiel"* — different memories in every head |
| *"Look away."* | *"Walk into the harbour"* — every will beaten separately |
| *"Stop forgetting."* | Any Deep Dive, Thought Probe, or read of particular content |

> **Violation cannot be parallelized.** Coercion is a per-mind contest, so the entire coercive end of the catalogue stays retail. **You can make a city forget. You will never make a city obey.**

- **Gate:** Domain **●●●●** to originate. **Kinesis can never broadcast** — no minds in the circuit to address.
- **Cost:** base Strain **+1 per order of magnitude of audience, max +4.** Range bands stack. Originating always frays, **including a Detection sweep** (charged as if the Domain rated 1).
- **Resistance:** roll **once**. Defended minds are simply **excluded**, not contested.
- **A construct answers only the key it was cut for.** Convergence is necessary, not sufficient — a generic *"release all suppressions"* does nothing.
- **The lattice** removes range as a term. **Receiving is ambient; originating requires the activation key.**

## 6. Character creation

1. **Concept**, Nature & Demeanor, and **Affiliation** (which sets a favored Domain and typical Backgrounds; it is not a cage).
2. **Attributes 7 / 5 / 3.** Max 4 before freebies.
3. **Abilities 13 / 9 / 5.** Max 3 before freebies. Setting Abilities: **Psionics** and **Lattice** (Knowledges), **Awareness** (Talent).
4. **Domains: 6 dots**, none above 4. **No mandatory dot** — bracing is free and the innate read needs none. *(ST dial: 5 for grittier, 7 for more capable.)*
5. **Backgrounds: 5 dots.** Adds Status, Lattice Access, Safehouse, Off-Grid.
6. **Tether 2.** Raise to 3 for **7 freebies**; drop to 1 via the Fragile Tether Flaw (+5). **4–5 are unavailable at creation, and Tether can never be raised with experience.**
7. **Willpower 3** (4 for some disciplined Affiliations).
8. **15 freebie points:** Attribute 5 · Ability 2 · **Domain 7** (6 if Affiliation-favored) · Background 1 · Willpower 1 · Tether 2→3 is 7 · Merits/Flaws by rating (Flaws return at most +7).

**Techniques known:** the scaling baseline of each Domain you have dots in, plus one qualifying Threshold technique per two dots.

**There is no Humanity or Path track.** The Tether Track is the degeneration meter.

**Merits:** Off the Lattice (3–5) · Disciplined (2) · Quiet Mind (2) · Attuned (1).
**Flaws:** Fragile Tether (5) · Loud Signature (2) · Flagged (2–4) · Frayed (3 — begin with 2 permanent Fray).

## 7. Conflict

**Psionics is instant and tell-less.** Thought is the whole action: no gaze, gesture, incantation or transformation to interrupt or flinch from. A technique and movement do not compete — **read and run**.

**Combat means you already lost.** A Gifted in a stand-up fight is a mortal with a headache. The professional norm is to make the fight unnecessary, and the mentor's question after almost every operation is *"was there a way to do that without Kinesis?"* — to which the answer is almost always yes.

**The Fugue** (Intrusion ●●●●●) is the exception, and the splat's one owned arena: two minds in unmediated contact, each rendering the exchange as symbolic architecture because a mind cannot process it otherwise. **Kinesis does not function there** (no physical reality) and **neither does casting** (a spell is performed from outside the system it edits, and the Fugue has no outside). A non-Gifted substitutes one broad trait for every duel role at +2 difficulty, against a Gifted's six specialised tools. Use it the way a Garou uses Crinos — an arena, not a last resort.

## 8. Psidolons

**No tether, no fraying, no Strain.** A psidolon uses its Domains freely and forever, and never makes a Snap Check. **Two tracks:**

- **Coherence** — the accumulated memory-structure it cohered around in place of a tether. *What it is.* Destroyed by the **Memory-wipe**; **0 = permanent destruction**.
- **Stability** — whether the form holds with no body around it. Full while hosted; **drains 1/hour when evicted**; 0 = it comes apart. **Eviction is a deadline, not a wound** — a psidolon that finds a host in time is entirely itself.

**The wipe:** hold Intrusion access on the host (opposed by the psidolon's Barriers), then **Intelligence + Memory** each turn vs difficulty 6 fresh / 7 coherent / 8 ancient; each success destroys one Coherence box. A botch destroys *host* memory instead. **Stop above one-third and it recovers, and knows your face** — *if you cannot finish, do not begin.*

| Tier | Coherence | Stability | Notes |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Fresh** | 3 | 3 | Burning Out; desperate, poor at deception. What a snapped PC becomes (Coherence = highest Domain −1). |
| **Coherent** | 6 | 5 | Established cover. |
| **Ancient** | 12 (3 load-bearing) | 8 | Load-bearing boxes cannot be wiped without a scene-long procedure. |
| **Old One** | ~18 (5 load-bearing) | ~12 | Not hunted in a session. |

**Immunities:** cannot be Overridden, Compelled, Memory-*edited* (only destroyed), or Emotion-Manipulated. **Psidolons cannot cooperate** — never run a coordinated pack. **They cannot take Kindred hosts** (the Beast holds the seat).

## 9. Against the other splats

| Target | Ruling |
|---|---|
| **Mundanes, most Kindred, Garou in Homid, unprepared mages** | **Open.** Will alone is not a defence; the psion rolls unopposed. |
| **Garou in Crinos or frenzy** | Mental work **fails** — no coherent will to seize. Kinesis unaffected. |
| **Kindred generally** | Feel *wrong* to Detection. Vampiric **Dominate vs Barriers is not a threshold** — the attempt is hazardous at any rating, there is no rote, and failure opens a two-way door. |
| **Mages** | Only an **actively raised** Mind shield resists. A prepared Mind mage is a peer; an unprepared one is a sleeper. |
| **Akashics / disciplined mortals** | A **held** Empty Mind genuinely resists — the signature inversion. |
| **Dreambound** | Their fae road is **not psionics** and Barriers does not stop it. Keep the exception. |
| **Wraiths** | No thoughts to read; **Empathic Sense** finds them. |

**The test for anything not listed:** *does the effect reach **into** the mind, or act on the **world** the mind perceives?* A reach meets Barriers. A stimulus never touches it.

| Discipline | Ruling |
|---|---|
| **Auspex** | Telepathy finds **nothing** on a braced psion (no surface leaking — same defence that beats Skim). Pushing past is a reach: **Barriers**, plus the two-way door. Aura Perception works normally and **fraying shows**. Auspex detects psionic activity in progress — the only way a Kindred learns they were read. |
| **Dominate** | Not a threshold. Hazardous at any rating, **no rote**, two-way door on failure. Needs eyes and proximity; psionics needs neither and has no tell, so the psion acts first and invisibly. *Held* defensive Dominate is a real wall. |
| **Presence** | **Works normally on psions.** Barriers stops intrusion, not stimulus. Resist as anyone would. (Emotion Manipulation is Barriers-resisted because it *reaches in*; Presence broadcasts a quality the target's own mind answers.) |
| **Obfuscate** | The eyes lose; Detection does not. The psion registers **a mind is there and something is wrong** — no clean read, no fix, no ID. Concealment survives; certainty of being alone does not. Same for **Chimerstry**. |
| **Mind Sphere** | Symmetrical. A **raised** shield resists psionics; an unraised mage is a sleeper. Barriers resists Mind magic under the same no-rote rule. |

**Three guardrails on the Barriers ruling:** it is **inward-facing** (weaponising the wall costs like any projection) · **the Dreaming walks through it** · **it degrades along the Fraying axis** — not because defending scars you, but because Fray from everything else drags down the pool and leaves no box to pay containment with.
