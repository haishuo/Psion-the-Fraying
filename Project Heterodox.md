# Project Heterodox

**Mission:** Develop alternative frameworks for understanding and practicing psionic ability outside the MetaConcert's epistemological monopoly

**Philosophy:** "What if they're not wrong about everything — just wrong about the map?"

**Methods:** Independent research, alternative taxonomy development, experimental technique

---

## What They Do

Heterodox is the Project that Praxis takes personally.

Praxis built the Domain taxonomy — the seven-Domain framework that every Gifted uses to understand their own abilities. They systematized bracing, codified tether strain classifications, developed the theoretical foundations that every Circuit's operations rest on. Their epistemology became the MetaConcert's epistemology. Their map became the territory.

Heterodox asks: what if the map is wrong?

Not wrong in the sense of fabricated — Heterodox does not claim Praxis invented the Domains out of nothing. The taxonomy describes real phenomena. Abilities do cluster. Techniques do have prerequisites. Tether strain does follow recognizable patterns. Praxis observed something real and built a framework around it.

But a framework is not the thing it describes. The Domain taxonomy reflects the assumptions of the people who built it — which Gifted they had access to, which theoretical commitments they brought, which edge cases they forced into categories rather than allowing to suggest the system was incomplete. Praxis itself acknowledges this, quietly, in classified internal documentation. The taxonomy might be slightly wrong. Heterodox takes that "might be slightly wrong" and follows it as far as it goes.

**Primary activities:**
- Independent research into psionic mechanics outside the Domain framework
- Development of alternative taxonomies and theoretical models
- Experimental technique development — abilities that don't fit cleanly into the seven Domains
- Study of edge cases the MetaConcert's framework can't fully explain (Eve Taranis Vire being the most prominent)
- Cross-tradition research with Mage practitioners whose paradigms offer different lenses on mental phenomena
- Documentation and dissemination of findings to other Projects

**What they've found:**

Heterodox's research is preliminary, fragmentary, and maddeningly suggestive. They have documented cases where:
- Gifted develop abilities that sit between Domains in ways the taxonomy can't cleanly categorize
- Technique prerequisites that Praxis considers universal turn out to be artifacts of how Praxis teaches, not of how the power works
- Tether strain profiles don't match Praxis's models for practitioners who developed their abilities outside MetaConcert training
- The seven-Domain structure may be an artifact of the seven-Circuit institutional structure rather than a fundamental property of psionic mechanics

None of this is conclusive. All of it is unsettling to anyone whose practice is built on the Domain framework — which is every Gifted trained inside the MetaConcert.

## Who They Are

**Typical Heterodox member:**
- Former Praxis researchers who encountered edge cases the framework couldn't explain and were told to stop asking
- Self-taught Gifted who developed abilities outside institutional training and found the Domain taxonomy didn't match their experience
- Cross-tradition scholars with exposure to Mage paradigms that describe mental phenomena differently
- Gifted from non-Western traditions whose cultural frameworks for understanding mental ability predate and differ from Praxis's European rationalist model
- Intellectually restless minds who find orthodoxy uncomfortable regardless of which institution enforces it
- **Former Preserver archivists** who broke over the editing — and who could not walk out empty-handed if they tried, because a Preserver carries the unedited originals of what the Circuit falsified *in their own memory,* the living receipts behind their own eyes

**Organization:**
- Research collectives rather than cells — organized around specific questions rather than operational objectives
- Distributed, decentralized, held together by shared curiosity rather than shared ideology
- Extensive written correspondence (encrypted, anonymized)
- Occasional in-person research sessions when security permits
- More academic conference than resistance movement

**Cultural identity:**
- Researchers first, everything else second
- Genuine intellectual humility — they know their alternative frameworks might also be wrong
- Frustration with Praxis not because Praxis is evil but because Praxis stopped asking questions
- Deep awareness that questioning the framework is itself a framework-dependent activity (they cannot fully escape the epistemology they're critiquing)
- The specific excitement of people who have found something real and can't yet prove it

The rarest and most dangerous Heterodox are those last recruits. Most of the collective argues that the *framework* is wrong — the Domain taxonomy, the official account of what the Gifted are. A defected Preserver escalates the heresy from epistemology to evidence: not *the map is wrong* but *the institution has been lying about its own past, and I am the proof — I am the unedited copy of what they rewrote.* They cannot publish it; a living memory is not a document. But they *are* it, and in a movement built on the conviction that the official story cannot be trusted, a person who holds the true one is the closest thing Heterodox has to a saint. They are also, cut off from the Circuit's load-management and succession, a slow [saturation](Psidolons%20-%20The%20Untethered.md) risk with no one to offload to — a walking archive that may, in time, have no one to inherit it.

## External Alliances

**Order of Hermes (PRIMARY ALLY — complicated):**
- Hermetic scholars have the most sophisticated alternative frameworks for understanding mental phenomena
- Mind Sphere theory offers a lens on psionic mechanics that differs fundamentally from the Domain taxonomy
- Joint research produces genuinely novel insights — perspectives that neither tradition could reach alone
- Hermetic interest in psionic mechanics is partly academic, partly strategic (understanding a potential rival's capabilities)

**Why this works:**
- Both traditions take knowledge seriously as a value in itself
- Both have institutional orthodoxies they're pushing against (Hermetic tradition is as rigid as Praxis in its own way)
- The intersection of Domain theory and Sphere theory produces questions neither framework can answer alone — if psionics and Mind magic describe overlapping phenomena through different lenses, the overlap itself is data
- Mutual respect between scholars who understand what it costs to pursue heterodox ideas inside orthodox institutions

**The productive collision:**

Hermetic Mind Sphere theory and Praxis Domain theory describe overlapping phenomena through fundamentally different paradigmatic lenses. When a Hermetic scholar and a Heterodox researcher sit down to compare notes, neither framework fully explains what the other observes. The gaps are where the interesting work happens.

Specific examples: Hermetic theory predicts certain mental phenomena should be possible that the Domain taxonomy says shouldn't exist. Heterodox has documented cases where those phenomena appear in Gifted who developed outside institutional training. The Hermetics say "our framework predicted this." Heterodox says "your framework predicted it but couldn't produce it without Awakening — ours can't produce it within institutional training but can produce it outside it." Neither conclusion is satisfying. Both are significant.

**Tensions:**
- Hermetic scholars sometimes treat psionics as "lesser" mental magic — a provincial framework for phenomena the Spheres describe more completely. This is intellectually arrogant and also partially defensible, which makes it more irritating.
- Heterodox researchers sometimes find Hermetic paradigm dependence frustrating — Sphere theory has its own unexamined assumptions, and Hermetics who believe they've transcended paradigmatic limitation are exhibiting a different form of the same blindness Praxis has
- The power dynamic is unequal — the Traditions have institutional resources Heterodox lacks
- Some Hermetics are more interested in studying Heterodox than collaborating with them — treating Gifted psionics as a research subject rather than a peer tradition

**Other allies:**
- Dreamspeakers (alternative frameworks for understanding consciousness rooted in spiritual traditions the European rationalist model doesn't engage with)
- Cult of Ecstasy (experiential approaches to mental phenomena that bypass taxonomic frameworks entirely — if you stop categorizing and just *experience*, what do you find?)
- Verbena (holistic models of mind-body-spirit that challenge the Cartesian assumptions underlying Domain theory)
- Occasionally Praxis defectors who left because the Circuit wouldn't let them follow their findings — the rarest and most valuable recruits, who bring institutional knowledge and institutional frustration in equal measure

## Praxis With Follow-Through

It is worth saying plainly what this Project is, because from the outside it looks like a rejection of Praxis and it is very nearly the opposite.

Heterodox has Praxis's training, Praxis's instruments, Praxis's vocabulary, and Praxis's conviction that a thing can be understood if you are rigorous enough about it. What it does not have is the reflex that defines the parent Circuit. **Praxis's organising principle is that a finished discovery is a debt** — a thing that must now be run, maintained, taught and governed, all of it subtracted from the next question — and so the moment the work is done, Praxis's attention has already left. That is why the lattice went to the Maintainers, the children to the Integrators, and the naming of a civilisation to whoever wanted it. *(See [The Praxis Circuit](The%20Praxis%20Circuit.md), "What they want.")*

**Heterodox is what happens when a Praxis researcher stays.** Not stays *longer* — stays with the *finished thing*, and turns around, and looks at what it did. Every recruit in the list above arrived by the same route: they followed a finding past the point where the Circuit's interest ended, and were told to stop asking. The instruction was not malice. From inside Praxis it is barely even a rebuke. It is a manager pointing out that the useful part is over.

Which produces the sharpest way to hold the two of them: **Praxis asks what is true. Heterodox asks what the answer did.** Same discipline; one additional question; and it is the question the parent institution is structurally incapable of asking, because asking it requires being present after the work is done.

It also puts Heterodox alongside a Circuit it would not expect to be compared to. The **Integrators** are the fragment of Praxis that cared about the *people* the work was done to. Heterodox is the fragment that cared about the *consequences* the work had. Both are the same institution minus the same refusal, and neither of them could stay inside it. **Praxis has now shed its conscience twice, in two different directions, and on both occasions experienced the loss as a staffing matter.**

## The Framework Problem

**What Heterodox is actually challenging:**

The Domain taxonomy is not just a classification system. It is the language every Gifted uses to understand their own minds. When a Gifted thinks about their abilities, they think in terms Praxis provided: "I have strong Intrusion, adequate Barriers, weak Kinesis." The taxonomy shapes perception. It shapes what Gifted believe they can do, which shapes what they attempt, which shapes what they achieve.

Heterodox's most radical claim is that this shaping is itself a form of constraint. A Gifted trained in the Domain system develops abilities *within* that system — they learn what the system teaches, they develop along the paths the system provides, they conceive of their potential in terms the system defines. A Gifted who develops outside the system — who has never been taught the seven Domains, who has no framework for categorizing their abilities into institutional boxes — sometimes develops differently. Not necessarily better. But differently, in ways the taxonomy can't fully account for.

This is the implication Praxis finds most threatening: not that the taxonomy is wrong, but that the taxonomy is *limiting*. That by teaching every Gifted to understand their abilities through the same seven-category framework, Praxis has inadvertently narrowed the range of what Gifted can conceive of doing. The framework doesn't just describe the power. It shapes it. And what it shapes the power into is what serves MetaConcert institutional needs.

**And this is why Heterodox did the one concrete, portable thing it is known for: it built new words.** If the vocabulary is the cage, a rival vocabulary is the first pry-bar — not the complete alternative map, which Heterodox freely admits it cannot draw, but a handful of tools for loosening the old one's grip. So Heterodox is where the counter-language comes from. *The power* for the Noesis. *Triggered* for Onset. *Technique* for protocol. *The Gifted*, held deliberately against *the Noetic*. These were not folk terms that drifted up on their own outside the lattice; they were coined, on purpose, by defected researchers who knew exactly what they were prying at — each one built to name the thing Praxis named without dragging Praxis's frame in behind it. It is why a single word can place a speaker so precisely: to say *the power* is not to lack the other word, it is to have been handed a choice and made it. And it is the clearest vindication Heterodox has of its own thesis about itself — that its gift to the resistance would not be a finished theory but a proof, a tool, a wedge. The theory has never left the seminar rooms. The words are in every safehouse in the world. You do not need to have read a line of Heterodox to carry its argument. You need only refuse to say *Noesis*.

**Eve as evidence:**

Eve Taranis Vire is the most significant data point Heterodox has, and the one they can study the least. Her abilities don't fit the Domain taxonomy cleanly. Her layered sensing — Garou danger sense, spiritual awareness, psionic Empathic Sense and Thoughtsense operating simultaneously as passive, always-on perception — is not something the seven-Domain framework predicts or explains. She developed outside institutional training. She has never been taught to think about her abilities in Domain terms. And she can do things the taxonomy says shouldn't be possible.

Heterodox cannot study Eve directly (she is protected, hidden, and not interested in being a research subject). They study her from reports, from testimony, from fragments. Every fragment suggests the same thing: the map is not the territory, and the territory is larger than the map.

Heterodox's error, if it has one, is not in doubting the map. It is in assuming the doubt only ever points to a *bigger* map — that everything Eve does, correctly catalogued, would still turn out to be psionics, just of an unrecognized kind. Some of what she does may not be psionics at all. Her own reports document the seam — Garou instinct, spiritual awareness, and genuine Domain-shaped perception, all braided together — without drawing the harder conclusion: a phenomenon that resembles psionics is not always psionics, any more than smell is a strange form of taste, or sight a weird form of hearing. "The territory is larger than the map" is the easy half of the thought. The harder half — that some of what sits past the map's edge was never in the same country to begin with — is the one even Heterodox has not yet been willing to think.

## Inter-Project Relationships

**With Fractal:** The most intellectually productive relationship among the Projects. Fractal questions the MetaConcert's political framework. Heterodox questions its epistemological framework. Both are doing intellectual work that other Projects find admirable and impractical. The difference: Fractal has reached its conclusions and advocates for them. Heterodox is still researching and resists premature conclusions. Fractal finds this caution frustrating. Heterodox finds Fractal's certainty epistemologically premature. They argue extensively and productively.

**With Iconoclast:** A collaborative relationship built on shared technical curiosity. Iconoclast reverse-engineers Maintainer implementations. Heterodox questions the Praxis theory underlying those implementations. Together they represent a two-layer challenge to the MetaConcert's technical and theoretical foundations. Iconoclast provides data (here's what binding constructs actually do). Heterodox provides analysis (here's why they might work differently than Praxis thinks).

**With Railroad:** Railroad appreciates Heterodox's work in the abstract but needs practical tools, not theoretical frameworks. The relationship is cordial and limited. Where it becomes specifically valuable: Heterodox's understanding of alternative training approaches informs how Railroad and CoG train Gifted children outside the MetaConcert's pedagogical framework. If the Domain taxonomy is limiting, then training children outside it might produce Gifted who are not limited in the same ways.

**With Rebel:** Almost no interaction. Rebel needs operational intelligence and combat support. Heterodox provides neither. Rebel considers Heterodox a more esoteric version of Fractal's impracticality. Heterodox considers Rebel's approach orthogonal to the kind of change that matters.

**With Parasyte:** Valuable intelligence source. Parasyte operatives embedded in Praxis occasionally leak research findings — edge cases, anomalous data, internal disagreements about taxonomy — that Heterodox cannot acquire independently. This intelligence is rare and precious. It is also dangerous, because Praxis is the Circuit most likely to notice when their classified research shows up in Heterodox publications.

**With Lacuna:** Operational dependency for any in-person research activity. Heterodox researchers meeting to share findings need the counter-surveillance that Lacuna provides.

## The Uncomfortable Truth

Heterodox's most uncomfortable finding is not about the MetaConcert. It is about themselves.

They are attempting to think outside the MetaConcert's epistemological framework while using cognitive tools that were largely developed inside it. Most Heterodox researchers were Gifted who were trained — however briefly, however incompletely — within the Domain system before they left or escaped. The categories they are trying to transcend are the categories they think with. The framework they are critiquing is embedded in how they perceive their own abilities.

This is not a problem they can solve by trying harder. It is a structural limitation of their position: you cannot fully escape a conceptual framework from inside it. You can identify its boundaries. You can push against them. You can document the places where reality doesn't match the map. But you cannot draw a new map without using the cartographic tools the old map gave you.

Heterodox knows this. Their best researchers are honest about the limitation. The question of whether a truly alternative framework for understanding the power is achievable — whether it is possible to understand psionic mechanics in ways that are genuinely free of Praxis's assumptions — remains open. Heterodox's contribution may not be the alternative framework itself. It may be the documented proof that the existing framework is incomplete, which is itself valuable even if the replacement never arrives.

Praxis finds this possibility — that Heterodox might be right about the limitation without being able to provide the solution — more disturbing than the possibility that Heterodox has a complete alternative theory. An alternative theory can be evaluated, tested, and potentially refuted. A documented gap in the existing theory that nobody can fill is a permanent source of institutional uncertainty.

## Quote

*"Praxis built the map. Every Gifted alive navigates by it. We're not saying the map is useless — it gets you from A to B. We're saying there are places that aren't on the map. Abilities that don't fit the categories. Techniques that shouldn't work according to the theory but do. Edge cases that got filed as anomalies because the alternative was admitting the taxonomy is incomplete. We're collecting the anomalies. We're mapping the gaps. And the gaps are not small."*

**— Heterodox researcher, encrypted correspondence**
