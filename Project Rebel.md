# Project Rebel

**Mission:** Direct military action against MetaConcert infrastructure

**Philosophy:** "They will not reform. They must be fought."

**Methods:** Sabotage, assassination, armed resistance

---

## What They Do

Rebel is the militant wing of Project resistance. While Railroad rescues, Fractal philosophizes, and Iconoclast engineers, Rebel fights. They are the blunt instrument — force, violence, direct confrontation with MetaConcert infrastructure and personnel.

The distinction between Rebel and the other Projects matters. Rebel *assaults*. They storm White Rooms to grab Gifted mid-Correction. They ambush Integration transports to free newly Expressed children en route. They attack Maintainer hubs, Inquisition safe houses, Panopticon monitoring stations. They are the Project that kicks in the door.

But Rebel is not Railroad. Once a target has been liberated — a child pulled from an Integration transport, a Gifted freed from a White Room — Rebel hands them off. Railroad takes custody, gets them to a bolthole, provides the training and sanctuary that Rebel cannot. Rebel doesn't teach bracing. Rebel doesn't run safe houses. Rebel creates the violent opening that makes extraction possible, and then other Projects do the work of turning that opening into a life.

**Primary operations:**
- Assault White Rooms and Integration transports to free Gifted in MetaConcert custody
- Assassinate Inquisitors and Panopticon monitors
- Sabotage MetaConcert facilities and infrastructure
- Disrupt lattice infrastructure through blunt-force attacks on Maintainer hubs
- Raid Preserver archives and leak classified information
- Armed defense of Railroad safe houses under MetaConcert attack
- Retaliation for MetaConcert atrocities

**The philosophy:**
The MetaConcert is fascist, irredeemable, and expanding. Peaceful resistance and philosophical arguments have failed. Only force will create change.

**The cost:**
Every Rebel action triggers Inquisition response. Every assassinated Councilor gets replaced with a hardliner. Every act of violence justifies increased MetaConcert authoritarianism.

Rebel knows this. They fight anyway.

## Who They Are

**Typical Rebel operative:**
- Former Inquisitors who broke with the system
- Gifted who lost everyone to White Rooms
- Combat-trained Kinesis and Control specialists
- People with nothing left to lose
- Skilled in guerrilla tactics and asymmetric warfare
- Strong Kinesis (their primary combat domain)
- Adequate Detection (counter-surveillance)
- Willing to die for the cause

**Organization:**
- Cell-based structure for operational security
- "Commanders" lead individual cells
- Coordinated through encrypted communications
- Some cells more extreme than others
- Debate over acceptable targets and collateral damage

**Cultural identity:**
- See themselves as freedom fighters, not terrorists
- Grim acceptance of necessary violence
- Honor fallen comrades who died fighting
- Reject MetaConcert's "utilitarian murder" while committing their own
- Understand the moral contradiction, fight anyway

## External Alliances

### Virtual Adepts (PRIMARY ALLY)

- Shared fight against authoritarian information control
- VAs provide technical support, encryption, hacking tools
- Both use distributed networks and guerrilla tactics
- Philosophical alignment: information/minds want to be free
- VAs help Rebel evade Panopticon surveillance
- Some VAs provide direct magical support in operations

**Why this works:**
- VAs understand asymmetric warfare against superior force
- Both fight Technocracy/MetaConcert respectively
- Similar tactics: hack the system, expose corruption, direct action
- Mutual respect for technical competence and revolutionary commitment
- VAs appreciate Rebel's sophisticated operational security

**Coordination:**
- VAs provide counter-surveillance tools
- Help Rebel cells communicate without lattice detection
- Hack into MetaConcert databases for intelligence
- Some joint operations against mutual enemies (Technocracy-MetaConcert cooperation)

**Other allies:**
- Get of Fenris (respect for honorable combat)
- Sabbat (shared hatred of Camarilla-MetaConcert alliance)
- Some rogue Technocracy operatives (Tradition-aligned)
- Desperate Gifted who see violence as only option

## Operations and Tactics

**Notable Rebel actions:**

**The Atlanta White Room Liberation (2018):**
- Rebel cell stormed White Room during termination
- Killed three Inquisitors, freed two flagged Gifted
- One freed Gifted later snapped (became psidolon, killed family)
- Other integrated into Railroad network, still stable
- MetaConcert response: increased White Room security, faster execution timelines

**The Preserver Archive Raid (2020):**
- Rebel operatives infiltrated Archive facility
- Stole unedited historical records
- Leaked evidence of MetaConcert historical revisionism
- Several Preservers killed in the raid
- Triggered internal MetaConcert scandal
- MetaCouncil increased archive security, executed suspected leakers

**The Chicago Lattice Disruption (2021):**
- Coordinated attack on Maintainer hub
- Temporarily destabilized regional lattice
- Hundreds of Gifted experienced painful disconnection
- Several died from severance trauma
- MetaConcert rebuilt stronger infrastructure
- Rebel cells divided over acceptable collateral damage

**Assassination Campaign (Ongoing):**
- Targeted killings of Inquisitors and Panopticon monitors
- Success rate: approximately 40%
- MetaConcert response: increased security, faster recruitment
- Debate within Rebel: is this achieving anything?

## The Rebel Dilemma

**The problem with armed resistance:**

Every Rebel victory creates MetaConcert propaganda:
- "See? Projects are violent extremists"
- "This justifies preemptive action"
- "We must increase security"
- "Dissent enables terrorism"

**The cycle:**
1. Rebel attacks MetaConcert target
2. MetaConcert increases authoritarian measures
3. More Gifted radicalize and join Rebel
4. Rebel attacks escalate
5. MetaConcert becomes more authoritarian
6. Repeat

**Both sides are trapped:**
- Rebel: "If we stop fighting, tyranny wins"
- MetaConcert: "If we ease restrictions, attacks increase"
- Neither can back down without appearing weak
- Spiral continues

**The question nobody can answer:**
Is Rebel making things better or worse?

## Internal Tensions

**Targeting debates:**

**Infrastructure vs. Personnel:**
- Some cells target only facilities (White Rooms, archives)
- Others assassinate individuals (Inquisitors, Councilors)
- Debate over whether killing people is ever justified
- Argument over "acceptable casualties"

**Surgical strikes vs. Broad attacks:**
- Precision operations minimize collateral damage
- Broader attacks have greater impact but higher civilian risk
- The Chicago Lattice incident split opinion
- Some cells went rogue over this disagreement

**Coordination with other Projects:**
- Railroad: "Your violence gets our safe houses discovered"
- Fractal: "You're becoming what you fight"
- Parasyte: "You make infiltration impossible"
- Rebel: "We do the dirty work you're too scared to do"

**The extremist problem:**
Some Rebel cells have gone too far:
- Attacking Integrators (who work with children)
- Operations with high civilian casualties
- Torture of captured Inquisitors
- Becoming indistinguishable from what they oppose

Mainstream Rebel leadership tries to maintain standards, but cells operate independently. Some have been disavowed. Some continue anyway.
