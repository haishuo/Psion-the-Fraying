# Project Iconoclast

**Mission:** Technical dismantlement of the lattice and reversal of binding constructs

**Philosophy:** "They built it. We can take it apart."

**Methods:** Binding construct analysis, lattice architecture exploitation, severance operations

---

## What They Do

Iconoclast is the Project that the Maintainers fear most — because Iconoclast understands what they built.

The distinction between Iconoclast and Rebel is fundamental. Rebel breaks things. Rebel assaults White Rooms to free Gifted mid-Correction. Rebel attacks Maintainer hubs to disrupt regional lattice networks. Rebel uses force — Kinesis, combat, destruction — and accepts the collateral damage as the cost of resistance. When Rebel hits a lattice node, binding constructs collapse violently, cascade effects kill people who were nowhere near the attack, and the MetaConcert rebuilds the infrastructure stronger within months.

Iconoclast takes things apart. They approach the lattice as an engineering problem — not something to be smashed but something to be understood, analyzed, and systematically dismantled at the architectural level. Where Rebel's attacks produce temporary disruption and permanent escalation, Iconoclast's work produces permanent liberation one person at a time.

Their primary function — the thing that makes them irreplaceable in the Project ecosystem — is **removing people from the lattice safely.**

This is the service that every apostate needs and almost no one can provide. The Maintainers know how to perform clean severance — they do it routinely in White Rooms as part of termination procedures. But the Maintainers bury that knowledge deep. Severance technique is among the most closely guarded expertise in the MetaConcert, because a Gifted who can sever their own lattice connection can walk away from the surveillance state entirely. The institution has every reason to ensure that this capability remains exclusively institutional.

A Gifted who wants to leave the MetaConcert has three options: find an Iconoclast who can perform clean severance; do it themselves, which is painful, dangerous, and frequently results in cognitive damage because the binding construct is integrated deeply enough into the host mind that ripping it out tears something important loose; or stay connected and hide behind Barriers, which means remaining visible to the lattice and living in permanent fear of Panopticon detection.

Most successful apostates found an Iconoclast. The ones who didn't — like Marcus Thiel, who severed his own connection alone — carry the scars of doing it without help. Marcus survived. Not everyone does.

**Primary activities:**
- **Severance operations** — removing willing Gifted from the lattice through clean dissolution of binding constructs. This is the core mission and the service no other Project can provide.
- Study binding construct architecture through whatever means available — defected Maintainers, captured documentation, independent reverse engineering
- **Lattice disruption** — identifying structural weaknesses in the lattice architecture and exploiting them, often in collaboration with Lacuna. Not Rebel's blunt-force hub destruction, but targeted interference with specific lattice segments — degrading coverage in areas where other Projects need to operate, creating persistent weak points the Panopticon must divert resources to monitor.
- Map lattice topology to identify coverage gaps, structural vulnerabilities, and the specific points where targeted interference produces maximum disruption with minimum cascade risk
- Provide technical consultation to other Projects on lattice-related operations
- Develop countermeasures against lattice-based detection and tracking

**The challenge:**
Binding constructs are Maintainer work — some of the most sophisticated psionic engineering in existence. Understanding them well enough to safely reverse them requires either Maintainer training or years of independent study. Iconoclast has both: defected Maintainers who brought their knowledge with them, and self-taught engineers who have built their understanding from fragments. Neither source is complete. The defectors' knowledge is often outdated (the Maintainers update their methods). The self-taught engineers' understanding has gaps that can be fatal in practice.

The Maintainers deliberately make this harder. Binding construct architecture is not standardized — each Maintainer's installation technique has individual variation, which means every severance operation requires assessment of the specific construct before the procedure begins. The Maintainers did not introduce this variation intentionally as a security measure, but the MetaConcert has recognized its defensive value and does not require Maintainers to standardize. Every binding is slightly different. Every severance is a custom operation.

An Iconoclast severance attempt that goes wrong produces the same cascade damage as forcible disconnection — severance trauma, potential death, collateral harm to nearby lattice connections. The difference between Iconoclast and Rebel is not that Iconoclast is safe. It is that Iconoclast is *trying* to be safe, and sometimes succeeding.

## Who They Are

**Typical Iconoclast operative:**
- Former Maintainers who defected (the rarest and most valuable recruits)
- Self-taught psionic engineers who reverse-engineered binding theory from observation
- Gifted with strong Barriers and Projection aptitude (the Domains closest to binding construct work)
- Technical minds drawn to the lattice as an engineering problem rather than a political one
- Patient, methodical personalities — this work cannot be rushed
- People who believe that "permanent and irreversible" is an engineering claim, not a metaphysical truth, and engineering claims can be tested

**Organization:**
- Small, tightly compartmented cells organized around specific research projects
- More research lab than resistance cell
- Knowledge shared cautiously (operational security, but also because incomplete knowledge applied to binding constructs kills people)
- Fewer members than any other Project — the skill requirements are extreme
- Each cell typically built around one person with genuine Maintainer-level understanding

**Cultural identity:**
- Engineers first, revolutionaries second
- Obsessive attention to detail (one wrong parameter in a severance attempt means death)
- Quiet confidence that the lattice is a problem to be solved, not a permanent condition to be endured
- Frustration with Rebel's approach ("you're breaking what we're trying to understand")
- Deep respect for the Maintainers' technical achievement, combined with absolute commitment to undoing it

## External Alliances

**Glass Walkers (PRIMARY ALLY):**

The Glass Walkers are the Garou tribe that understands systems — technological, informational, structural. Where other tribes see the lattice as a spiritual abomination and leave it at that, the Glass Walkers see it as a *specific kind* of spiritual abomination: a Pattern Web construct, the Weaver's influence made literal in psionic architecture. Binding constructs are strands of the Web. The lattice is the Web given institutional form. And Iconoclast is doing something that resonates at the deepest level of Glass Walker spiritual identity: unweaving it.

**Why this works:**
- Glass Walkers understand systems well enough to understand what Iconoclast is doing — not just "breaking the lattice" but reverse-engineering specific architectural features
- The spiritual significance is real: every binding construct Iconoclast dissolves is a strand of the Pattern Web unwoven. This is not metaphor to a Glass Walker theurge. It is literal spiritual work.
- Glass Walker technical resources — their familiarity with information systems, network architecture, and the intersection of technology and spirit — provide frameworks that complement Iconoclast's psionic engineering
- Both groups think in terms of systems, architectures, and exploitable design constraints rather than in terms of brute force

**What Glass Walkers provide:**
- Conceptual frameworks from Weaver-spirit interaction that illuminate binding construct behavior in ways Maintainer training doesn't cover
- Urban infrastructure access (safe houses, communication networks, meeting spaces in Glass Walker territory)
- Spiritual insight into the Pattern Web aspects of lattice architecture — perspectives on binding constructs that come from the Umbral side rather than the psionic side
- Occasional theurge consultation for severance operations (some Glass Walker mystics can perceive binding constructs in the Umbra, providing a second angle of observation during procedures)

**The spiritual dimension:**
To Glass Walker mystics, Iconoclast's work is not just resistance — it is a form of Weaver-correction. The lattice binds minds into permanent pattern. Dissolving those bindings restores the possibility of change, growth, chaos — the Wyld's influence that the Weaver's web suppresses. Iconoclast doesn't frame their work in spiritual terms. The Glass Walkers do, and the framing gives the alliance a depth that pure tactical cooperation wouldn't have.

**Other allies:**
- Sons of Ether / Society of Ether (shared commitment to proving "impossible" is an institutional claim, not a physical law; Etherite technical creativity complements Iconoclast's psionic precision)
- Virtual Adepts (technical support, especially for lattice topology mapping)
- Bone Gnawers (identify with the oppressed; provide ground-level urban intelligence)
- Occasionally Rebel (when Iconoclast needs field assets for severance operations)

## The Severance Problem

**What Iconoclast is actually trying to do:**

Clean severance — the deliberate, controlled dissolution of a binding construct — is Maintainer work. The Maintainers do it regularly in White Rooms as part of termination procedures. They can disconnect a Gifted from the lattice without cascade damage, without killing them, without harming adjacent connections.

But the Maintainers only do this in institutional contexts — in White Rooms, under controlled conditions, as part of procedures that end in execution or Correction. They do not sever people and release them. They sever people and process them. The technique exists. The institutional will to use it for liberation does not.

Iconoclast is trying to acquire the technique without the institution.

**The progress so far:**

Iconoclast has achieved partial success. They can identify binding constructs — locate them within a mind, map their architecture, understand their connection topology. They can weaken constructs — reduce the signal strength, create partial disconnection that makes the subject less visible to Panopticon monitoring. They have, in a small number of cases, achieved full severance — complete dissolution of the binding construct, clean disconnection from the lattice, the subject walking away free and alive.

**The failures:**

They have also killed people. Severance attempts that miscalculated the construct's integration depth. Procedures that triggered cascade effects the operator couldn't contain. Subjects who survived disconnection but sustained cognitive damage from the process — binding constructs that had integrated deeply enough into the host mind's architecture that removing them tore something important loose.

Iconoclast documents its failures with the same precision it documents its successes. This is Maintainer inheritance — you don't learn from failures you don't record. The failure documentation is harrowing. It is also the basis for every subsequent improvement in technique.

**What full success would mean:**

If Iconoclast develops a reliable, repeatable method for clean lattice severance, the implications are transformative:
- Every initiated Gifted becomes potentially freeable
- Railroad's mission expands from interception-before-integration to liberation-after-integration
- The MetaConcert's claim that integration is permanent and irreversible collapses
- The lattice's function as a tool of permanent control is undermined at the architectural level

The Maintainers understand these implications. Iconoclast is the only Project they consider an existential threat — not because Iconoclast can destroy the lattice (Rebel can do that with enough force) but because Iconoclast can make the lattice *optional*. Destruction can be rebuilt from. Obsolescence cannot.

## Inter-Project Relationships

**With Railroad:** The most natural alliance. Railroad intercepts before integration; Iconoclast works after. Together they could theoretically cover the full spectrum — prevention and reversal. In practice, the partnership is limited by Iconoclast's small numbers and incomplete technique. Railroad sends Iconoclast subjects who are already integrated and want out. Iconoclast takes those cases when it can and lives with the cases it can't.

**With Rebel:** Tense. Rebel attacks lattice infrastructure that Iconoclast needs intact to study. The Chicago Lattice Disruption destroyed data Iconoclast was in the process of analyzing. Rebel considers Iconoclast too cautious. Iconoclast considers Rebel reckless with irreplaceable research material. They cooperate when Iconoclast needs field assets for severance operations, but the cooperation is grudging.

**With Fractal:** Philosophical respect, limited practical interaction. Fractal provides the moral argument for why the lattice should be dismantled. Iconoclast provides the technical possibility. Fractal admires Iconoclast's commitment. Iconoclast finds Fractal's operational uselessness frustrating but recognizes the intellectual value.

**With Parasyte:** Valuable but dangerous. Parasyte operatives embedded in the Maintainer Circuit are Iconoclast's most important intelligence source — they provide binding specifications, lattice topology data, and technical documentation that Iconoclast cannot acquire independently. The risk: if a Parasyte-Iconoclast intelligence channel is compromised, the Maintainers learn exactly how much Iconoclast knows about their architecture. This has happened. It went badly.

**With Heterodox:** A productive intellectual relationship. Heterodox questions Praxis's theoretical frameworks; Iconoclast questions the Maintainers' engineering implementations. Both are working on the assumption that the MetaConcert's understanding of its own systems may be incomplete or wrong. They share findings cautiously.

**With Lacuna:** Operational dependency. Iconoclast's work requires access to initiated Gifted in conditions where the lattice connection can be studied and manipulated without Panopticon detection. Lacuna provides the surveillance gaps that make this possible.

## The Uncomfortable Truth

Iconoclast's greatest asset is also its greatest vulnerability: they think like Maintainers.

Their defected Maintainers brought institutional knowledge, institutional methodology, and institutional habits of thought. The self-taught engineers have independently converged on similar approaches because the lattice is what it is and the engineering constraints are objective. Iconoclast's culture — precise, patient, obsessive about documentation, more concerned with getting it right than getting it done — is Maintainer culture with the institutional loyalty removed.

This means Iconoclast understands the lattice better than any other Project. It also means they understand the lattice the way the Maintainers do, which may not be the only way to understand it. Praxis designed the lattice. The Maintainers implement and maintain it. Both institutions have assumptions built into their understanding — assumptions about what binding constructs are, what they can do, what the limits are. Iconoclast has inherited those assumptions along with the knowledge.

The possibility that the lattice could be approached from a fundamentally different theoretical perspective — that binding constructs might be vulnerable to something the Maintainers never considered because it falls outside their framework — is the question Iconoclast cannot answer from inside its own methodology. It is also the question Heterodox is working on.
