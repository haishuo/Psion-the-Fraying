# Project Parasyte

**Mission:** Deep cover infiltration and intelligence gathering

**Philosophy:** "We change the system from within."

**Methods:** Appear loyal, feed intelligence to resistance, subtle sabotage

---

## What They Do

Parasyte operatives maintain cover as loyal MetaConcert members while secretly supporting Project resistance. They are the most dangerous Project—because nobody knows who they are. Including, sometimes, themselves.

**Activities:**
- Provide early warning about flagged Gifted (helps Railroad extract)
- Leak classified information to other Projects
- Sabotage operations from inside (lost files, delayed responses)
- Protect sympathetic members from Panopticon attention
- Gradually shift MetaConcert policy through bureaucratic influence
- Maintain cover for years or decades

---

## The Overlay: How Parasyte Actually Works

The question every other Project asks about Parasyte is: how? The lattice makes every initiated Gifted accessible to Panopticon observation at any time. The Panopticon can reach into any node on the network and read surface thoughts, analyze emotional patterns, assess behavioral signatures. A Gifted who is secretly disloyal must sustain that secret against a surveillance infrastructure that can examine them at any moment without warning. Master Barriers help, but no one maintains maximum Barriers shielding at all times for decades. Eventually the Panopticon accesses the node during a moment of lowered guard. Eventually they see something.

Parasyte solved this problem by not hiding.

The core technique is the **identity overlay** — and its origin is the MetaConcert's most uncomfortable institutional irony.

Praxis developed the overlay from their study of psidolon occupation mechanics. They provided it to the Operative Circuit for deep-cover infiltration of external factions — Technocratic installations, Tradition chantries, organizations with counter-intelligence sophisticated enough to defeat conventional cover. The MetaConcert sent Operative agents equipped with overlays into the Projects. Some of those agents went native. They brought the technique with them.

Parasyte exists because the MetaConcert built the weapon the resistance uses to subvert it from within. The overlay that lets a Parasyte operative pass as a loyal Inquisitor is the same overlay designed to let an Operative pass as a loyal New World Order agent. The Panopticon's monitoring systems cannot reliably detect it because the systems were designed to accommodate it — overlays are what authorized Operatives use on sanctioned missions. The detection gap is a design feature that became a vulnerability when the technique escaped institutional control.

The overlay suppresses the operative's true identity and replaces it with a fabricated one. The technique is a controlled, voluntary, reversible version of the process by which a psidolon inhabits a host — the operative's original self is pushed below the threshold of conscious access, and a constructed identity occupies the active layer.

The overlay is not a mask worn over the real self. It is a complete replacement: a fully realized identity — beliefs, loyalties, emotional responses — that runs as the operative's genuine self. When the overlay is active, the operative does not *pretend* to be a loyal Inquisitor. They *are* a loyal Inquisitor. Their surface thoughts are clean because they are thinking a loyal Inquisitor's thoughts. Their emotional responses are authentic because they are feeling a loyal Inquisitor's feelings. The Panopticon reads exactly what it expects because what it reads is real.

The overlay is not constructed from nothing. A complete fabricated identity built from scratch would have gaps — missing memories, inconsistent emotional associations, the kind of thin spots that a sufficiently thorough Intrusion interrogation might detect. Instead, the overlay is built on the operative's own history, modified where necessary. If the operative was born in Florida, the cover identity was born in Florida. If the operative trained as an Integrator, the cover identity's training history overlaps significantly. Lies are better with truth in them. The overlay uses as much of the original as it can, changing only what must change to produce a different loyalty and a different set of beliefs. This makes the cover identity robust — not a fiction draped over a person, but a *variation* of a person, convincing because most of it is real.

The overlay operates on the same fundamental principle as the MAD trigger constructs — a dormant psionic structure that sits inert until a specific signal activates it. In this case, there are two constructs: the overlay itself (which suppresses the real identity and runs the fabricated one) and the **restoration trigger** (which deactivates the overlay and surfaces the original self).

The shared principle stops at the construct. At the level of the *trigger* the two systems are exact opposites, and the difference decides everything about how each can be used. **MAD's signal is universal** — one instruction, identical for every construct on earth, carrying no content at all, which is what lets it cross a continent in an instant and why nothing can jam it. **Parasyte's signal is unique to one person** — a configuration cut for one mind and no other, which means it can never be broadcast, never fired at scale, and never delivered except by someone who holds that operative's particular shape.

**How the two selves coexist:**

The overlay does not destroy the original self. It suppresses it — pushing it below the threshold of conscious access the way a deep Memory suppression pushes a recollection below the threshold of retrieval. The original self is still there. It is simply not running. The overlay occupies the active layer of consciousness entirely: making decisions, forming relationships, doing the MetaConcert's work sincerely, competently, sometimes brilliantly.

But the mind is one mind. Both identities inhabit the same cognitive architecture, and experience accumulates in that architecture regardless of which identity is active. Everything the overlay experiences — every observation, every classified briefing, every operational detail — is laid down in the same memory structures the original self will access when restored. The overlay does not know it is generating intelligence. It is simply living its life. The intelligence is a byproduct of that life, retrievable later by someone the overlay doesn't know exists.

When the restoration trigger fires, the original self surfaces and the overlay is suppressed in turn. The original self finds weeks or months of accumulated experience waiting — not as a report or a summary, but as lived memory. They remember what the overlay did because it happened in their mind, in their body, with their senses. They process the intelligence, extract what matters, pass it to their handler through dead drops or other secure channels, and then reactivate the overlay. The cover identity resumes with no gap in continuity — they simply "lost" a few hours, which the overlay's self-consistent narrative fills with plausible mundane activity.

**The trigger mechanism:**

The restoration trigger must be something the Panopticon cannot accidentally activate through routine monitoring and the MetaConcert cannot deliberately activate through interrogation — but that a Project handler can activate intentionally.

The solution: a specific thought-pattern transmitted via Intrusion Domain. A passphrase, essentially, but not a word — a specific psionic signature, a mental shape that must be delivered directly into the operative's mind by someone who knows the exact configuration. Each operative's trigger is unique. The handler who holds it is the only person who can wake them up.

This creates the operational security the system requires. Routine Panopticon monitoring cannot stumble across the trigger because it is not a thought the operative has — it is a thought that must be transmitted to them from outside. MetaConcert interrogators cannot activate it because they don't know the configuration. Even a full Intrusion-based interrogation of the overlay identity produces nothing, because the overlay genuinely does not know the trigger exists.

**The uniqueness is not a preference. It is forced.** A trigger that could be fired at many operatives at once would have to be *the same trigger* for all of them — and a shared trigger is one the MetaConcert needs to discover exactly once. Worse than that: the MetaConcert invented the overlay. It has the technique, it has the lattice, and a uniform restoration signal is one it could place on the bus itself and watch the whole continent at the same moment, identifying every Parasyte operative in the institution by the simple fact of who suddenly changed. Every efficiency Parasyte might buy by standardising the trigger is an efficiency handed directly to the only adversary who could use it. So each trigger is cut for one mind, and the cost of that safety is the handler.

**The handler problem:**

Every Parasyte operative has a handler somewhere in the Project ecosystem who holds their restoration trigger. This creates a single point of failure that Parasyte finds acceptable because the alternative — self-triggered restoration — would mean the overlay identity could potentially discover and activate the trigger accidentally, or the MetaConcert could extract it through interrogation.

If the handler dies, is captured, or loses the trigger configuration, the operative is trapped in their overlay identity permanently. They will live out their life as a loyal MetaConcert member, never knowing they were anything else. Their intelligence will never be collected. Their sacrifice will never be known.

This has happened. Parasyte does not know how many times, because by definition there is no way to identify an operative whose handler has been lost. Somewhere inside the MetaConcert, there may be Gifted who were once Parasyte operatives, now permanently locked into overlay identities, doing the MetaConcert's work with genuine conviction and no memory of who they were before.

The thought keeps every handler awake at night.

**And there is no last resort.** Parasyte has no mass restoration and structurally cannot have one. There is no signal that surfaces every operative at once — no final order, no all-hands, nothing to send if the day ever arrives when the whole network is needed awake and pointed the same direction. Waking a specific buried person is an act performed on one particular mind, and acts performed on one particular mind do not scale (see *Broadcast* in [The Seven Domains — Game Mechanics](../Rules/The%20Seven%20Domains%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md)). Restoration happens one operative at a time, by one surviving handler at a time, over weeks, assuming the handlers are alive to do it.

Set that beside the weapon on the other side of the table. **MAD fires in an instant, everywhere, because its instruction is identical for everyone it touches.** The institution's doomsday device parallelises perfectly. The resistance's liberation cannot parallelise at all — and for exactly the same reason, read from the losing side of it. Freedom, in this setting, has to be delivered by hand, to one person, by someone who knew who they were.

Parasyte's people are the clearest case of it. If they are ever brought home, they will be brought home retail.

## The Toll

The identity overlay solves the surveillance problem. It does not solve the psychological one.

When the restoration trigger fires and the original self surfaces, they inherit the overlay's accumulated experience. Twenty years of running as a loyal Inquisitor means twenty years of memories of being someone who believed in preemptive execution, who respected the doctrine, who felt the institutional camaraderie of the Inquisition, who killed people and processed the killing through the Inquisition's moral framework and found it *sufficient*. Those memories are not erased when the original self returns. They are *there* — laid down in the same cognitive architecture, experienced in the same body, felt with the same emotions. The original self must integrate them.

This is why Parasyte operatives "forget which identity is real." It is not a failure of willpower or psychological weakness. It is an inevitable consequence of two identities inhabiting the same mind: both fully realized, both authentic during their runtime, both leaving their mark on the same accumulated memory. The original self who resurfaces after years of dormancy finds that the overlay's experiences have weight. The overlay's relationships feel real because they were real. The overlay's convictions feel earned because they were earned — over years of genuine experience inside the institution.

Some operatives handle this by maintaining strict separation during their active periods — processing intelligence quickly, reactivating the overlay, minimizing the time the original self spends confronting the overlay's accumulated life. Some develop a philosophical framework for holding two authentic selves in parallel. Some break. The break, when it comes, is not dramatic — it is the quiet moment when the original self, surfaced by the trigger, looks at the overlay's life and decides it is the better one. They tell the handler they're done. Or they don't tell the handler anything, and simply stop responding to the trigger.

When this happens, Parasyte loses an operative not to the MetaConcert's counter-intelligence but to the operative's own accumulated experience of the life they were pretending to live.

There is also the deeper problem — the one Praxis documented and Parasyte does not discuss with recruits. The overlay is derived from psidolon occupation mechanics, and it carries an attenuated version of the same structural risk: a consciousness suppressed beneath another consciousness for long enough begins to erode. The overlay was designed with protections the psidolon mechanism lacks — the restoration trigger, the architectural safeguards, the deliberate reversibility. But the underlying dynamic is the same. An operative who has been running the overlay for twenty years has had their original self suppressed for twenty years. The original self, when restored, may find that it has lost resolution. Not dramatically — not the way a psidolon's host degrades into incoherence — but measurably. Edges softened. Convictions that felt absolute now feel negotiable. The sense of self slightly less sharp than it was before.

Parasyte's operational guidance accounts for this: handlers are trained to assess original-self coherence during trigger cycles and to recommend extraction if degradation exceeds acceptable parameters. In practice, "acceptable parameters" is a judgment call made by a handler who needs the intelligence the operative provides, about an operative whose continued deployment serves the resistance's most critical function. The parameters have a tendency to remain acceptable for longer than they should.

## Who They Are

**Typical Parasyte operative:**

Could be anyone:
- Respected Inquisitor who lost faith in preemptive execution
- Panopticon monitor horrified by what they're ordered to do
- Integrator who can't stomach childhood indoctrination
- Operative who went native with Projects they were supposed to infiltrate
- Preserver maintaining secret unedited archives
- MetaCouncilor working for gradual reform

**Requirements:**
- Strong Memory (to construct and maintain the identity overlay)
- Strong Intrusion (prerequisite for Memory work; also used for the trigger mechanism)
- Strong Barriers (to protect the overlay architecture from deep Intrusion during interrogation)
- Willingness to undergo the overlay procedure — to have your real self suppressed and replaced, trusting that someone else will wake you up
- Psychological resilience to integrate the overlay's accumulated experience during active periods
- The specific courage required to let someone else hold the key to who you really are

**The horror:**
The overlay does not merely *permit* the operative to do the MetaConcert's work. The overlay *is* someone who does the MetaConcert's work, sincerely and competently. When the overlay identity executes a flagged Gifted, it is not an act of cover maintenance. It is a genuine professional action taken by someone who genuinely believes in preemptive execution doctrine. The operative's original self, when they resurface and access the shared memory, must live with what their other self did in earnest.

- The overlay executes flagged Gifted because the overlay believes in the doctrine
- The overlay monitors the lattice because the overlay is a good Panopticon analyst
- The overlay participates in White Room terminations because the overlay is a disciplined Inquisitor
- The overlay enforces MetaConcert doctrine because the overlay is a loyal member

None of this is performance. That is the point. That is what makes it undetectable. And that is what makes the accumulated memory unbearable.

**The justification:**
"By staying inside, I save more lives than I take. The intelligence I provide helps Railroad extract dozens. Yes, *I* killed three people this year — or the version of me that runs when I'm asleep did. Railroad saved twenty because of what I learned while I wasn't myself. The math works. The math has to work."

## External Alliances

**None officially:**

Parasyte cannot risk contact with other supernatural factions. Any alliance would be operational security disaster.

**Indirect cooperation:**
- Information passed through cutouts and dead drops
- Intelligence shared with Railroad through anonymous channels
- Warnings delivered through untraceable methods
- No direct meetings, no acknowledgment of allegiance

**The isolation:**
Parasyte members are alone. Can't trust MetaConcert colleagues. Can't reveal themselves to Projects. Can't coordinate with each other (don't know who else is Parasyte).

Some maintain contact with single Railroad handler. Some operate completely solo. Most die without anyone knowing they were resistance.

## The Moral Abyss

**What Parasyte operatives live with:**

*"I killed her. I looked into her eyes and I killed her. She was flagged for critical fraying. Maybe she was. Maybe she wasn't. I'll never know. But I'm the Inquisitor they trust, the one who never hesitates, the one who always follows orders. So I killed her. And next week, I'll leak the kill list to Railroad, and they'll save four people before Inquisition arrives. Four lives for one. The math works. I tell myself the math works. I wonder how many I'll have to kill before I stop believing that."*

**The calculation:**
- Kill one to save four?
- Kill ten to save forty?
- Where is the line?
- When does the math stop working?
- When do you become the monster you're fighting?

**The answer:**
Nobody knows. Parasyte members make the calculation every day and hope they're still on the right side.

## Success and Exposure

**Parasyte victories:**
- Countless Railroad extractions enabled by advance warning
- MetaConcert policies softened through bureaucratic influence
- Classified documents leaked, exposing atrocities
- Several extreme Inquisitors quietly removed from service
- Gradual cultural shift toward questioning doctrine

**Parasyte failures:**
- Operatives exposed, interrogated, executed
- Deep cover agents who forgot they were resistance
- Members who cracked under pressure and confessed
- Those who became what they pretended to be
- Intelligence that arrived too late to save anyone

**The exposures:**

When Parasyte operative is caught:
- Full Intrusion interrogation (Memory specialists extract everything)
- Entire network potentially compromised
- Everyone they contacted becomes suspect
- Purges within MetaConcert Circuits
- Tighter security measures implemented
- Other Parasyte operatives exposed by association

**The recent disaster (2022):**
Senior Preserver revealed as Parasyte after twenty years deep cover. Under interrogation, gave up three Railroad safe houses and five other Parasyte operatives. Dozens dead. Railroad networks in chaos. Parasyte operations set back years.

Lesson learned: Nobody is safe. The lattice sees all eventually.

## The Parasyte-Railroad Connection

Railroad's effectiveness depends almost entirely on Parasyte intelligence. Without insider information, they would fail most interceptions.

**How it works:**

**Step 1 - Panopticon detects a Trigger:**
- New psionic signature appears on Detection sweeps
- Gifted child identified and located
- Information passed to Integrator Circuit
- Integration team dispatched

**Step 2 - Parasyte operative intercepts:**
- Parasyte (embedded in Panopticon or Integrators) sees the assignment
- Leaks information to Railroad through anonymous dead drop
- Provides: child's location, family name, Integrator ETA
- Maintains cover by appearing to process assignment normally

**Step 3 - Railroad receives intelligence:**
- Hours (sometimes only minutes) of warning
- Activate nearest Conductor and extraction team
- Race to reach child before Integrators
- Must convince family and extract quickly

**Step 4 - The race:**
- Railroad arrives: "Your daughter Triggered. They're coming to integrate her into surveillance network. We can hide her."
- Family: confusion, fear, disbelief
- Clock ticking: Integrators en route
- Decision must be made in minutes
- Success or failure determined by who the family trusts

**The timing is critical:**
- Too slow: Integrators arrive first, child integrated, lost forever
- Too fast: Might expose Parasyte source (how did Railroad know before official detection?)
- Perfect timing: extraction completed minutes before Integrators arrive

**Without Parasyte intel:**
- Railroad operates blind or relies on rumors
- Might hear about a Trigger days or weeks too late
- Child already integrated into lattice
- Success rate drops to 10-20%

**With Parasyte intel:**
- Advance warning before integration
- Specific location and timeline
- Know exactly when official team arrives
- Success rate increases to 60-70%

**The cost to Parasyte operatives:**

*"I flag the Onset. I prepare the integration assignment. I watch Integrators review the file—another child about to be hooked into the lattice. Then I slip a message to Railroad. Address. Family name. Timeline. Sometimes Railroad gets there first and the Integrators find an empty house. Sometimes they don't. When they don't, I'm often the one assigned to perform the integration. I hold that child's hand. I weave their mind into the lattice while they cry and ask for their parents. I tell them it's for their safety. I watch their mind go on the network. And I hate myself. But if I blow my cover, Railroad loses their source. No more warnings. No more interceptions. No more children saved. So I keep doing it. I integrate children into slavery so that other children might stay free. The math has to work. Please, let the math work."*

**Why Parasyte operatives are essential:**
- Only source of real-time Trigger intelligence
- Provide exact timing for successful interceptions
- Can sometimes delay Integrator deployment (buying Railroad time)
- Occasionally sabotage integration assignments (lost paperwork, wrong addresses)
- Risk everything to give children a chance at freedom

**The operational security:**
- Parasyte and Railroad never meet directly
- Information passed through dead drops, encrypted channels
- Multiple cutouts between source and Conductors
- If one link is compromised, others survive
- Some Conductors don't even know who their Parasyte source is
