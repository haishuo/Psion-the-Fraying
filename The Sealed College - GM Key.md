# The Sealed College — GM Key

> **OUT-OF-UNIVERSE. STORYTELLER ONLY.**
> This document does not exist in the setting. Nothing in it is common knowledge, Circuit doctrine, or recoverable by ordinary play. It is the author's key to a redaction the in-world texts deliberately withhold — granted to you here so *you* know the answer, not so the characters can. The in-world material (the `## The Attribution` sidebars in each Circuit doc, and `### The Sealed College` in [The Praxis Circuit](The%20Praxis%20Circuit.md)) is written to keep this sealed. This file is the one place it is written plainly. Keep it that way.

---

## In-universe status: who actually knows

Be strict about this. The secrecy *is* the concept; if the knowledge leaks freely, the whole device collapses.

- **Openly claimed — every Gifted "knows":** Only two Circuits name their founder in daylight — the **Panopticon** (Jeremy Bentham) and the **Inquisition** (Tomás de Torquemada). These appear in the public Circuit docs. Even here, "knowing" means the name is *honored*; the proof that the man was Gifted sits, with everything else, in the **Black Archive** — the living, psionic successor to the burned **Black Files**, carried as memory-constructs by a handful of Praxis's **Black Archivists.** *(See [The Praxis Circuit](The%20Praxis%20Circuit.md), "The Black Archive.")*
- **Sealed — a strict, per-Circuit gradient:** The founders of **Praxis, the Maintainers, the Integrators, the Preservers, and the Operatives** are confirmed only in the Black Archive. Who suspects, and how much:
  - **Praxis** — the Black Archivists and Circuit leadership *know.* The average researcher has, at most, heard rumors of a hidden archive, and likely not the term "Black Archive."
  - **Inquisition** — suspects *hardest,* and from the founding: they were the ones lied to (*"Is this everything?" / "Yes"*) and never believed it. But they cannot prove it and do not know the name. Of all Circuits but Praxis, they suspect the most.
  - **Preservers** — a faint unease at the very top only, and of a different kind: not remembered grievance but the memory-keeper's instinct that the record is *too* seamless. No proof, no history to anchor it, no seam they can find.
  - **Everyone else** (Panopticon, Maintainers, Integrators, Operatives) — effectively nothing. They postdate the founding and have no reason to know; and the Archivists are kept off-lattice precisely so even the Panopticon never catches a thread.

  A rank-and-file member of any Circuit, Praxis included, lives and dies never knowing their own patron's name.
- **Each Circuit's own "tradition" is a suspicion, not a fact:** an unofficial, half-remembered patron the seniors don't correct and can't confirm — the way a regiment feels about a battle honor. A Maintainer may *feel* Vauban is theirs. They cannot prove it, and the only thing that could settle it is the Black Archive — not a vault to be cracked but a handful of shielded minds, each trained to burn its contents before surrendering them.

**Table upshot:** a player character cannot simply *learn* this. See *Running it* below.

## The redaction: Praxis's own lineage

Named here and **nowhere else in the setting.** In the Praxis doc these people are described but never signed, because Praxis's founding document alone names no founder. If you ever need to put a face under the blank line, this is it.

- **Francis Bacon — the method.** *Novum Organum*'s interventional epistemology: you do not watch nature, you *constrain* it, "put nature to the question" (the era's own phrase for torture). *New Atlantis*'s **Salomon's House** — a secret college that decides which discoveries the world may keep — is the Black Archive's charter, in print, in 1626. Died stuffing a hen with snow to test whether cold preserves flesh: a man who died *measuring the preservation of a body.*
- **António Egas Moniz — the procedure.** 1949 Nobel for the leucotomy. Cognitive Reconstruction is the lobotomy in psionic form — *"stable, but not necessarily the same person they were before."* The horror is the **Nobel**: the establishment examined the deliberate blunting of a personality and called it a triumph, then **declined to rescind** the prize when the truth came out. Shot and paralyzed by one of his own patients. Modern "refinement" = **Walter Freeman**'s transorbital assembly line (~3,500 lobotomies, Rosemary Kennedy among them) — the disciple who industrializes what the founder was honored for.
- **Supporting facets (temperament, not headline):**
  - *Andreas Vesalius* — knowledge-by-dissection; the patron of the doc's coldest line, *"Dominate was a gift. This was an autopsy."*
  - *Julien Offray de La Mettrie* — *L'Homme Machine* (1747): the mind is architecture, not soul, and architecture can be rebuilt. The unspoken premise Reconstruction runs on.
  - *Santorio Santorio* — thirty years in a weighing chair quantifying his own body by the gram. Measurement with no stopping point; no cruelty, no motive, just the number written down.

**If you need one name:** **Bacon**, with Santorio's temperament and La Mettrie's excuse — and **Moniz** as proof the instinct reached the twentieth century still collecting medals under names the world agreed to admire.

## Running it at the table

- **The vault has a pulse.** The Black Archive isn't a vault to breach — it's a handful of minds, each of which will burn its contents before yielding them; the Storyteller note in the Praxis doc holds. Confirmation walks out on two legs or not at all — a leaker who *is* the fragment, a defecting Black Archivist (default: **Dr. Elaine Voss**, [Project Heterodox](Project%20Heterodox.md) / the Ashford-Cole Seat).
- **The basement that isn't there.** The outside world — Nosferatu, Tremere, NWO — believes the secret is a hidden vault of black-bound notebooks, because that is what it *was* before the pact, and no one told them it moved into minds. A party chasing "the Black Files" through Kindred rumor is chasing a fossil: there is no room to find, and Praxis is glad to let them look. Use it as a **false lead** that quietly reframes the real hunt — the truth was never a place; it's a person.
- **Suspicion is cheap; proof is sealed.** A sharp investigator can come to *believe* Vauban or Montessori was Gifted — a wight's origin, a rumor with a citation, a fragment someone swears surfaced once. Let **belief, not confirmation,** be the reward. The not-knowing is the flavor.
- **Don't let the college become trivia.** Its whole power is that it is withheld *even from the institution.* If the party collects the full list like a set, the redaction stops meaning anything. Dole out one thread at most, and make it cost.
- **The deepest one is the deadliest.** Vauban or Montessori surfacing is an unsettling revelation. **Bacon-and-Moniz surfacing** — the proof that Praxis's method and Cognitive Reconstruction descend from a named, honored human lineage — is closer to the "bomb beneath the floor" the Black Archive is described as. Treat it as the apocalyptic climax, not a clue.
