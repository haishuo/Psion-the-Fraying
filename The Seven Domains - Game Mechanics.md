# The Seven Domains: Game Mechanics

**Protocols, Techniques, and How to Roll Them**

*This is the mechanical companion to [The Seven Domains — Psionic Techniques](The%20Seven%20Domains%20-%20Psionic%20Techniques.md), which describes what each Domain is and feels like. This document gives the dice. It assumes the [Fraying engine](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md) for all Strain and the [character rules](Character%20Creation%20-%20Building%20a%20Gifted.md) for traits. Classic World of Darkness throughout: d10 pools, difficulty 6 default, successes counted, 1s cancel.*

*The techniques below are a representative sampling, not the complete canon. Storytellers should add, rename, and reskin freely.*

> **A note on terminology — technique or protocol.** Outside the MetaConcert — among the Projects, the unaffiliated, the Cult — a psionic ability is a **technique**. Inside the MetaConcert, internal doctrine calls the same thing a **protocol**: to an Inquisitor, Thoughtshield is "the Thoughtshield Barriers Domain protocol," catalogued and systematized by Praxis. The mechanics are identical; the word is a shibboleth. A character who says *protocol* was raised in the lattice. A character who says *technique* was not — or wants you to think so. This document uses *technique* as the neutral term and notes the MetaConcert register in each Domain header.

## Core Resolution

**Rolling a technique.** Roll the listed **Attribute + Domain** against **difficulty 6** (unless the entry says otherwise). Successes set the degree of effect.

- **Scaling** techniques grow with your Domain rating — the rating is the ceiling, successes are the reach within it.
- **Threshold** techniques require a minimum Domain rating to learn; once known, they simply function.

**Resistance.** Resistance to a psionic technique comes from a **purpose-built mental defense, and from nothing else.** **Raw Willpower does not resist Intrusion, Memory, or Puppeteer** — a mind guarded by will alone is *open*, and the psion rolls **unopposed** (barring a botch, they get in). What actually cancels successes is a real wall: another Gifted's **Barriers** (reflexive **Wits + Barriers**), a Mage's *actively raised* Mind-sphere shield, an Akashic's held Empty Mind, or a comparable ward the Storyteller rules purpose-built and currently up. Age, sheer will, and being a supernatural do **not** count by themselves — the test is *is there a wall, and is it up,* never *how strong is the will* (see [The Best Defense](The%20Best%20Defense%20-%20Barriers%20and%20the%20Mental%20Domain.md)). Note that the wall is the **Barriers Domain**, not bracing: bracing (baseline containment) keeps your own mind *in* but does nothing to keep an intruder *out*, so a **braced, Barriers-0 psion is as open to intrusion as any sleeper** — quiet, but unwalled. An **Unshielded** psion (unbraced) is worse still: open in *and* loud out, both doors gone, the whole horror of the state.

**The innate floor is Detection, and only Detection.** The one thing that comes with being a psion, below any dot, is the surface-*read*: an unbraced psion receives the genuine surface cognition of every unshielded mind nearby, and even a braced psion at Detection 0 can direct that sense at one mind and catch its messy, contextless broadcast (see the Detection note above). That is the "reader" a psion innately is. **Intrusion has no such floor** — reaching *past* the surface is a trained act, like the Barriers wall: at Intrusion 0 you can feel that a mind is holding something back, but not read what. And the innate surface-read is *not* the access **Memory and Control** require, which is a *Dig* beneath the surface — those still demand real Intrusion dots.

**The one thing Willpower still does.** The *will-targeting* Control techniques — **Suggestion, Compulsion, Emotion** — work by moving the will to *act*, so a target may **spend or roll Willpower to refuse an objectionable one**, wall or no wall. That is the will declining to be *moved*, not a barrier keeping the psion *out.* Everything that bypasses the will — reading, Memory-editing, Puppeteer driving the body — gets no such save. (This is also why a mundane can shrug off a *"jump off the roof"* Suggestion but cannot keep a psion from reading them or, once breached, puppeting them.)

**Being resisted is felt, no separate technique required.** Rolling Barriers against an incoming technique is never silent to the target — win or lose, it registers as contact, the way a blocked punch still lands as a hit. A Gifted always knows, after the fact, that *something* just tried them. This applies only to a discrete, resisted roll; it does **not** apply to the MetaConcert's ambient lattice access, which runs through the binding construct itself rather than a technique that has to beat your Barriers — that channel stays exactly as undetectable as ever. You can always tell when a person just tried you. You can never tell when the lattice is simply there.

**The Intrusion prerequisite.** To use **any Memory or Control technique**, you must first **hold Intrusion access**: succeed on Dig (or a stronger Intrusion technique) against the target and *keep concentrating*. (Reading their surface with Skim is *not* access — a broadcast tells you nothing about the door; you have to get past it.) Access lasts while you concentrate and stay in range. Lose it — distraction, the target breaking away, a failed sustained roll — and all Memory and Control work ends instantly. Against a shielded Gifted, *getting* access is the whole contest.

**Strain** is paid the moment you act, success or failure, per the [Fraying engine](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md). Each entry lists its Strain; remember the universal modifiers (Push +1, Sustained +1, Desperate +1 / Panicked +2, Range +1/band, Resisted +1).

**Botches** don't just fail — they bite: feedback to the user, a connection slammed shut, or, if the user is already at Stage 3+, an **involuntary Bleed** (loud, detectable, +1 Strain).

> **On gestures, words, and focuses.** Psionics is mental. No technique *requires* a spoken word or a gesture — the power is in the projection, not the performance. But many Gifted use a focus anyway: a muttered phrase, a fixed stare, the infamous hand-wave of a Suggestion. For most it is harmless habit and useful theater (a focus can grant the Storyteller's blessing of a −1 difficulty when it genuinely aids concentration). For newcomers it is a crutch they mistake for the mechanism. The hand does nothing. They do it anyway.

## Power and Craft: Domains Are Not Skills

A Domain is raw capability — what your mind can *reach.* It is not the same as knowing what to *do* with the reach, and the two are tracked separately. The Domain is the sensor; an **Ability** is the analyst. Two rules follow, and both reward the Gifted who is *clever* over the one who is merely *strong.*

**The Domain collects; the Ability comprehends.** A technique delivers raw psionic input or output. Turning that into something *useful* usually calls for a relevant Ability. Intrusion ●●●● lets you read a target's surface thoughts in full — but it is **Investigation** that tells you which thought is the clue, **Subterfuge** that flags the one that's a lie, **Empathy** that hears what the words are hiding, and the right **Knowledge** that tells you whether the formula you just lifted off their mind even matters. A Gifted with a towering Domain and no relevant Ability is a wiretap with no one listening on the other end — they intercept everything and understand none of it. The technique still succeeds on its **Attribute + Domain** roll; the Storyteller may then call for an **Attribute + Ability** roll to *act* on what it gathered, or simply rule that, absent the Ability, the raw result is so much noise.

**Cleverness costs less tether.** A technique's Strain cost assumes the obvious, brute method. A Gifted who understands the *mechanism* of what they are doing can often reach the same end for far less. The textbook case is Kinesis: the crude way to stop an oncoming car is to seize it and arrest two tons of momentum head-on — full Strain, and then some. The smart way, open to a Gifted with **Science** or a good intuitive feel for physics, is to shove it a few degrees off-axis: you are no longer fighting the car's mass, only redirecting its vector, and the same car ends up in the same wall for a fraction of the energy. When a player describes a genuinely cleverer application and backs it with a fitting Ability, the Storyteller may **reduce the technique's Strain by 1–2** (to a floor of 1), or grant a stronger effect at the same cost.

**The surgical read beats the open channel.** The two rules above meet most sharply in the work psionics is *for* — getting the truth out of someone who will not give it. The novice does the obvious thing: question the subject like any interrogator *and* hold an open Intrusion link the whole time, reading every answer as it lands. It works. It is also the longest, most expensive road to the answer — the equivalent of solving a problem by brute summation when there is a trick — and it frays you white before the subject cracks, because every round of that open channel is Strain you did not have to spend, and most of what pours through it is *noise:* surface panic, looping irrelevancies, the static of a frightened mind. You drown in signal and starve for information.

The disciplined Gifted works the other way. They run the interrogation on **skill** — Subterfuge to set the trap, Empathy to hear the tell, Interrogation to steer — and they keep the power *holstered,* spending it once, at the decisive moment. Picture a poker game in which you may glimpse your opponent's hand, but every glimpse frays you a little. You do not look every hand; you would untether before the night was out. You look at the one juncture that matters — when you push all-in, or are deciding whether to — and in that instant you read whether the bluff will hold. The rest of the night you wear a perfect face, because for the one hand that decided everything, you *knew.* A single read, placed where the whole interrogation turns, beats ten rounds of open-channel mind-reading every time, at a fraction of the cost — and the cleverness discount above applies. (The brute who holds the channel open and frays toward a Snap mid-interrogation has, in his way, earned that too.) This is the shape of every expert application, not interrogation alone: the skill carries the work, and the power is spent surgically, at the hinge.

The payoff is deliberate and large: a **skilled, educated** Gifted frays far slower than a powerful but crude one — not by buying more Tether (you cannot), but by spending the tether they have more wisely. Knowledge does not only make you more effective; it is the only thing besides discipline that makes the tether last. The Gifted who throws cars frays young. The one who knows where to *push* gets to grow old.

## Broadcast

Psionics was never point-to-point. Every unshielded mind broadcasts its surface for free, and an unbraced psion receives all of them at once — the innate floor is *already* a many-to-one channel, and always was. **Broadcast is that same channel run outward:** one act, addressed to every mind in reach at the same instant.

The cost is set by the **instruction**, not the audience.

**The convergence test.** A broadcast can carry only an instruction that is **identical for every mind and consults no individual mind's contents.** If resolving it means going into a particular head — locating a specific memory there, overcoming that person's will, excavating what only they know — it **diverges**, and divergence collapses the efficiency entirely: the work is then done per mind, serially, at full price each time, which for any real crowd means it cannot be done at all.

| Converges — broadcasts | Diverges — cannot |
|---|---|
| *"You did not see that."* | *"Forget Marcus Thiel"* — his face sits in different memories in every head |
| *"Look away."* | *"Walk into the harbor"* — every will must be beaten separately |
| *"Stop forgetting."* | Any Deep Dive, Thought Probe, or read of particular content |
| One false percept laid over a street | A different false percept per person |

The consequence is the one that matters: **violation cannot be parallelized.** Coercion is a per-mind contest by definition, so the coercive end of the catalogue — everything that frays you — stays permanently restricted to one target at a time. Broadcast reaches only the shallow, consonant end, which is exactly the end the Strain table already prices at nearly nothing. This is not a balance patch bolted on afterward. It is the same rule that governs everything else in this book: you pay for the violation, and violation is retail.

**Gate.** Originating a broadcast requires **Domain ●●●●** in the Domain concerned. (**Aegis** — Barriers ●●●● , the shield stretched over a crowd — is this rule already written down for one Domain.) **Kinesis can never broadcast:** the test excludes it for free, because there are no minds in the circuit to address. Physics has no cores.

**Cost.** The technique's base Strain, **+1 per order of magnitude of audience, to a maximum of +4.** Ten minds +1, a hundred +2, a thousand +3 — ten thousand and ten million both +4. Range bands stack as normal. Disposition applies as ever, and for nearly everything that can be broadcast at all it lands neutral or consonant: no one has a stake in having seen a car thrown.

**Resistance.** Roll **once**. You do not roll against each mind — that would be divergence. Instead, **defended minds are simply not included:** a raised wall, a held Empty Mind, a construct configured to refuse, and the instruction passes them by untouched. Which produces the signature scene. After a master lays *you did not see that* across a plaza of five thousand, the only people who saw it are the three who could have.

**A construct answers only the key it was cut for.** Passing the convergence test is not sufficient by itself — a broadcast does not *pick locks*, it shouts a key and only the locks cut for that key open. A generic instruction (*"release all suppressions," "everyone drop what you're holding"*) is perfectly convergent and does perfectly nothing, because the constructs it is shouting at were never built listening for it. This is the whole difference between the two great trigger networks in the setting. **MAD** is one key cut for millions of *identical* locks, installed deliberately over centuries so that a single word opens all of them at once. **[Project Parasyte](Project%20Parasyte.md)**'s restoration triggers are millions of *unique* locks with no master key — cut that way on purpose, because the locksmith is the enemy. Same technology; opposite configuration; opposite fate.

**Bleed is a broadcast with nobody steering it.** A frayed psion's involuntary emission is this same outbound channel, open, carrying no instruction and gated by nothing. That is why it is *loud*, and why it is the trained master's signature power and the dying psion's signature symptom running on identical machinery, read at opposite ends.

### The lattice

**Range is what confines an unassisted broadcast** to a room, a building, a block. The MetaConcert's lattice removes range as a term: an instruction placed on it reaches every bound mind, and every mind carrying a construct, at any distance. The lattice is not a network the MetaConcert happens to own — it is a **bus**, and this is what makes an act at the scale of a city possible at all.

**Receiving is ambient. Originating requires the key.** Any bound mind receives what crosses the lattice; this is the ambient hum that makes a live sweep so hard to distinguish from background traffic (see **Psionic Sense**), and it is why a Panopticon analyst holding Detection ●● genuinely commands city-scale perception. *Their reach is real, and it is not theirs.* Putting an instruction **onto** the bus is a separate privilege — held by very few, gated by the activation key, which is best understood not as an artifact but as the highest write credential on a system where nearly everyone has read. An Integrator with a grudge is not a city-scale weapon. They are a very well-informed reader.

Take a psion off the lattice and what is left is their own rating, in their own range band, originated by themselves. That difference — not protection, not the pension, not the safety of numbers — is the largest thing the MetaConcert actually offers, and the largest thing defection actually costs.

### Worked Example: The Prince's Ten Minutes

A Camarilla Prince cannot always prevent violence. Sometimes the Sabbat are coming, the ground is urban, and the breach is not a risk but a schedule. What he *can* do is call ahead.

> *"From 11:40 to 11:50, nobody in these six blocks remembers anything."*

This is close to the ideal broadcast workload, and the reason is worth understanding, because it is the whole of the professional's art. **Cleaning up afterward is divergent.** *Forget the vampire you saw* has to find a vampire inside each memory, and it sits in a different place in every head — which means identifying who was present, determining what each of them actually formed, accounting for everyone who wandered in or out, and cutting a bespoke working to fit each life. That is not one job. It is as many jobs as there were people, and it can never be *finished*, only abandoned — you cannot prove you found the last witness.

**Cleaning up beforehand is convergent,** because it indexes on a **clock** instead of on content. *Form no durable memory during this interval* needs to know nothing about anybody. Everyone in the field shares the interval; that is the only thing they have to have in common, and it is enough. This is the general principle behind every large operation worth the name: **index on what everyone shares — a time, a place — never on what they saw.** Content is precisely the thing that makes people different from one another, and difference is what costs.

**The spot check is sampling, not enumeration, and that is the entire saving.** Serial work does not vanish; crews still go in afterward and read a scattering of minds to confirm the kernel took. But the volume of that work is **decoupled from the size of the affected population.** You check thirty people whether the field covered six blocks or sixty. Set that against a reactive operation, where the serial labour scales one-to-one with the crowd and thousands of specialist-hours buy you an answer you still cannot certify. One broadcast plus a sample is not a marginal improvement on hunting witnesses individually. It is a different order of problem.

**And the failed spot check is not an error — it is a detection event.** Anyone who *did* retain the ten minutes was, by the rule above, excluded from the instruction: they hold a purpose-built defence that was up at the time. A crew that finds a mind still carrying the memory has not discovered a glitch. They have discovered a psion, a mage with a shield raised, a monk holding Empty Mind, or something stranger. Which means a proactive cleanup is *also a sweep*: it sorts an entire district into *ordinary* and *interesting* in a single pass, for free, and hands the institution a map of every defended mind inside the field. Nobody has to intend this. The exclusions identify themselves.

**What it does not fix.** Memory does not touch a recording any more than Mirage fools a lens. The district forgets; the district's phones do not. What is left afterward is missing time, unexplained injuries, displaced possessions, half-finished messages, and footage nobody can account for — a chronic, diffuse anomaly across six blocks instead of one coherent catastrophe. The MetaConcert files that as a win, and it is one. It is also exactly the soil that grows the sort of person who starts digging.

**Nobody outside knows any of this.** Every other mental practitioner in the World of Darkness pays per head — a Tremere asked to wipe five thousand is being asked for five thousand workings — so the entire supernatural world's instinct about mass memory work is calibrated correctly for everyone except the only people who can actually do it. The MetaConcert has never corrected the impression, and charges accordingly. See [The Operatives Circuit](The%20Operatives%20Circuit.md), "The Standing Order," for what that misapprehension is worth in a century of accumulated debt.

**And the cost nobody records.** A broadcast cannot consult individuals, so it cannot consult their **disposition** either — the Strain is priced on the aggregate. In six city blocks there are certainly people who would have refused: the man whose daughter was in that district, the woman who understood something during the worst hour of her life. They are not resisted. There is no contest, no roll, no will to overcome. **They are not even seen.** The psion pays nothing extra for them and never learns they were there. The instruction did not reach past their refusal; it never asked.

## Barriers
*The firewall. Defense, concealment, containment. MetaConcert register: Barriers Domain protocols. Base Strain 1.*

**Bracing** · *Below the Domain scale — not a Barriers dot, not rolled to possess*
The baseline containment every trained psion carries, the way a fledgling vampire carries a leashed Hunger — it keeps your own mind *in* (the **outbound** door), and its absence is the **Unshielded** state. It is *not* the Barriers Domain: bracing shuts your mouth, Barriers dots build the inbound wall, and a braced psion can sit at Barriers 0 (quiet but unwalled). **Re-bracing another** (an Integrator's work, or any Gifted with Barriers ●●●+) rolls **Manipulation + Intrusion**, diff 7, across roughly a scene of calm — note it is taught *through* Intrusion, not Barriers — ending the Unshielded state. **Strain 2** to the bracer. See the [Unshielded rules](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md).

**Thoughtshield** · Scaling ●–●●●●●
**Pool** Wits + Barriers · **Resists** incoming Detection & Intrusion · **Strain** 1 per scene maintained.
Your passive defense pool is always **Wits + Barriers**; activating Thoughtshield *raises* it — actively shielding adds your successes as bonus resistance for the scene and conceals your surface thoughts from reading. At ●●●●● it can hide you from a dedicated Panopticon search (but never your tether condition from Tether Sense, and strong emotion still spikes through). Maintaining maximum shielding is exhausting; most Gifted idle at ●●–●●●.

**Mind Barbs** · Threshold ●●
**Pool** set passively · **Strain** 1 to set (lasts until triggered) · **Resists** —
A trap. Anyone who attempts Intrusion against you takes **(your Barriers) dice of bashing-equivalent psychic pain** and disorientation (−1 to −2 to their next action). Passive once set; discourages casual probing.

**Feedback** · Threshold ●●●
**Pool** Wits + Barriers (reflexive) vs the intruder's Intrusion · **Strain** 1.
An active counter-strike against a *detected* Intrusion: your net successes are inflicted as psychic pain and may force the attacker to break contact (1 success = sting, 3+ = connection severed). Requires that you know you're being read.

**Deadening** · Threshold ●● · *(MetaConcert: the Analgesia protocol)*
**Pool** none to invoke (1 action) · **Strain** 1 · lasts a scene (holdable across scenes at Barriers ●●●●+).
Flood the mind's pain channels shut — a psionic analgesia that **suppresses all Health wound penalties** for the duration. You act at full dice though Hurt, Wounded, even Crippled. But it silences **pain, not damage**: you still bleed, still take Health levels, and still drop the instant damage reaches Incapacitated. You simply stop *feeling* the toll until it ends — and a Gifted who cannot feel how hurt they are will keep fighting into wounds that kill them.
**When it ends** — scene's close, you drop it, or you're knocked out — the pain returns all at once. If you are at Wounded or worse, roll **Stamina + Willpower (diff 7, −1 per Barriers dot above ●●)** or be **incapacitated by the shock for one turn.**
*Note:* Unlike the Akashic Brotherhood's somatic discipline — a trained body that *tolerates* pain — Deadening is pure psionic suppression of the signal, closer to a flood of endorphins than to mastery. It also dulls the body's honest warnings: a Gifted running Deadening cannot feel the ache, body-rejection, and dissociation that signal fraying, and is far likelier to push past limits they can no longer sense. It suppresses wound penalties only — **never the Fraying Stage penalties**, which are structural degradation, not pain. The tether frays all the same. You just won't feel it happen.

**Aegis** · Threshold ●●●● · *(extends Barriers to others)*
**Pool** Wits + Barriers · **Strain** 2 (+1 per person beyond three) · maintained per scene.
Extend your shielding over willing others or a small space — a Lacuna safehouse made quiet, a cleanup team kept off the Panopticon's read, mundanes hidden during a breach. Protected minds use the *higher* of their own Barriers or your active successes.

## Kinesis
*The loud one. Force on the physical world. MetaConcert register: Kinesis Domain protocols. Base Strain 4 — the highest in the game, by design.*

**Telekinesis** · Scaling ●–●●●●●
**Pool** Wits + Kinesis · **Strain** 4 (+1 per turn sustained) · **Resists** Dodge/Athletics if used to attack a person.
Move, lift, hurl. The rating sets mass — ● coins/keys, ●● a person, ●●● a car (combat-capable), ●●●● structures/self-levitation, ●●●●● building-scale. Successes set precision and speed. As an attack, treat as a ranged strike; damage scales with rating and successes (a ●●● hurl is roughly a car door to the chest). Every public use is a cleanup event.

**Deflection** · Threshold ●●
**Pool** Wits + Kinesis (reflexive) · **Strain** 2 · **Resists** the incoming attack.
A quick kinetic swat — knock aside a bullet, blade, or thrown object. Successes subtract from the attack's successes. Cheaper and faster than raising a full Kinetic Barrier, but one thing at a time.

**Kinetic Barrier** · Threshold ●●●
**Pool** Stamina + Kinesis to raise · **Strain** 4 (+1 per turn maintained) · soaks physical damage.
A sustained force-field around the body, soaking **(your Kinesis) dice** against bullets and blows. Not mental Barriers — this protects flesh, not mind. The per-turn Strain makes it a desperate measure, not a default.

## Intrusion
*The inbound door. Reaching **past** the surface a mind shows — to the truth it holds back, or to project something into it. Resisted by Barriers. The key Memory and Control require. MetaConcert register: Intrusion Domain protocols. Base Strain 2.*

**Dig** · Scaling ●–●●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Cognitive Penetration protocol)*
**Pool** Perception + Intrusion · **Resists** Wits + Barriers · **Strain** 2.
Reach past what a mind is broadcasting to what it holds beneath — the lie under the calm answer, the feeling being suppressed, the thought kept off the surface. The standard way to *establish Intrusion access* for Memory, Control, and the deeper Intrusion work. ● catches that something is being held back; ●●●●● reads the held content clean. Trivial on the unwalled; a contest against raised Barriers. *(Reading the surface a mind is openly broadcasting is a different act, and a different Domain — see **Skim**, under Detection.)*

**Mindspeech** · Threshold ●●
**Pool** Perception + Intrusion · **Strain** 1 to a willing mind, 2 to force a channel · **Resists** Wits + Barriers (if unwilling).
Two-way mental conversation — silent coordination, contact across a room or (with range modifiers) further. Willing minds (or lattice-linked allies) open instantly; an unwilling mind must be breached first and resents the intrusion.

**Psionic Scream** · Threshold ●●
**Pool** Wits + Intrusion · **Strain** 2 · **Resists** Willpower (each target).
An omnidirectional mental burst — the psionic flashbang. Everyone in range, **friend and foe**, suffers disorientation (−1 per net success, to a brief stun on 3+). Crude, indiscriminate, and loud. A panic button.

**Thought Probe** · Threshold ●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Directed Cognitive Interrogation protocol)*
**Pool** Perception + Intrusion · **Strain** 2 (+1 if resisted) · **Resists** Wits + Barriers.
Targeted extraction — "where is the safehouse?" Requires Intrusion access. Pulls specific information the target knows, faster and deeper with more successes. The Inquisition's interrogation staple.

**Deep Dive** · Threshold ●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Cognitive Extraction protocol)*
**Pool** Intelligence + Intrusion · **Strain** 3 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers (but usually used on the immobilized).
Extended excavation of buried memory and subconscious. Requires a restrained, unconscious, or non-resisting target and real time. Can surface what the target themselves has forgotten — and can break a mind handled carelessly (botch risks lasting harm to the subject).

**Mirage** · Threshold ●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Perceptual Overlay protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Intrusion · **Strain** 2 (+1 per additional sense, target, or scene sustained; +1/turn in combat) · **Resists** Wits + Barriers.
Overlay a mind's *live* perception — conjure a sensory impression that isn't there, or suppress one that is. It feeds the senses; it does **not** touch stored memory or thought (that's Memory), which is why it sits beside Mindspeech and Psionic Scream as projection *into* a mind rather than editing *of* it. The signature use is the infiltrator's deflection — not invisibility, but becoming **not worth noticing**, a "somebody else's problem" the target's attention slides off. Successes and rating set fidelity and scope: ●●● manages a single sense and target (the guard's eyes skip the open door); higher Intrusion sustains a full multi-sensory scene across a group.
**Limits:** it edits *perception, not reality* — a suppressed obstacle still trips you, a conjured wall stops no bullet. It works only on **minds**: cameras, recordings, and sensors are unfooled. A target handed hard contradictory evidence may roll **Perception + Wits** to pierce it. And it edits only the live stream — when it drops, the target may realize they were fooled unless you pair it with Memory.

**The Fugue** · Threshold ●●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Cognitive Fugue protocol)*
**Pool** Wits + Intrusion · **Strain** 3, +1 per turn sustained (the initiator's cost — a Gifted opponent pays their own Strain normally for whatever they do inside; a non-Gifted opponent spends their own native resource instead, ST's call) · **Requires** an already-held Intrusion access (Dig or stronger, sustained) · **Resists** Willpower + Barriers — no partial effect; a defended mind simply refuses to be pulled in.
Drag a mind into direct, unmediated cognitive contact — no language, no senses in between, concept meeting concept. This is not travel and there is no destination; both minds stay exactly where they are. But a mind cannot process an exchange that unmediated as anything but *something happening somewhere* — the way a dream feels like a place while the body never leaves the bed — so each renders the exchange as legible metaphor: a library, a battlefield, a storm, a chessboard. Nothing here is real in the sense that matters to Forces, Life, or Matter — there is no substance for those Spheres to grip, the same absence that leaves Kinesis empty-handed. It is translation, not a location, and both minds are inside the metaphor together. The rarest technique in the Domain to *learn* — full rules for what happens once inside are in [Combat and Conflict — Game Mechanics](Combat%20and%20Conflict%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md), "The Fugue: Mental Duels." Few Gifted ever learn it, the way few surgeons specialize in the brain — but the ones who do use it the way a Garou uses Crinos: it is their arena, not their last resort.

## Control
*The most invasive. Requires Intrusion access. MetaConcert register: Control Domain protocols. Base Strain 3. The dirtiest Domain that isn't Kinesis — Control makes witnesses who remember. Named for its far end: not* domination *(a command, one and done) but* **control** *— the continuous, moment-to-moment operation of another body while its owner is locked inside, watching.*

> **These Strain ratings are the *neutral* cost.** Because Control by its nature works *against* will, it is the Domain that most often runs **dissonant** — and dissonance is what frays (see **Disposition** in [The Fraying](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md)). A suggestion someone already wanted costs almost nothing; forcing an unwilling mind runs dear; an *anathema* command — self-harm, betrayal of a loved one — costs a fortune and a Snap Check. This is the quiet cruelty of the mechanic: the interrogator who forces unwilling minds for a living is the one who frays toward the psidolon fastest. Control does not merely make witnesses. It makes *monsters* — and the first one it makes is you.

**Suggestion** · Threshold ●● · *the "Mind Trick"* · *(MetaConcert: the Behavioral Prompt protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Control · **Strain** 2 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers, or Willpower if the suggestion is objectionable.
A single, light, plausible command planted in a compliant or unsuspecting mind — "you've already seen my papers," "there's nothing interesting down that hall." It works best when the target wasn't going to care much anyway; net successes must beat the target's resistance, and a suggestion the target would *strongly* refuse simply fails (escalate to Override or Compulsion for that). Wears off quickly and leaves the target faintly puzzled.
*Note: this is the technique surrounded by the hand-wave folklore. The gesture is pure theater — newcomers ape it from stories and swear by it. The override is mental and works just as well with hands in pockets. Veterans find the wave embarrassing.*

**Emotion Manipulation** · Threshold ●● · *(MetaConcert: the Affective Induction protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Control · **Strain** 3 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers.
Push an emotional state directly — fear, calm, anger, attraction. Subtler than Puppeteer; the target may not realize the feeling is imposed. Successes set intensity. Fades when you stop concentrating. Useful for interrogation (induce fear) or de-escalation (force calm).

**Compulsion** · Threshold ●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Directive Implantation protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Control · **Strain** 3 · **Resists** Willpower.
Plant a specific behavioral compulsion that **persists briefly after you release access** — "you cannot leave this room," "you must tell the truth." An irresistible urge rather than direct puppetry; duration and force scale with net successes.

**Puppeteer** · Scaling ●–●●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Cognitive Command protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Control · **Strain** 3 (+1 per turn sustained) · **Resists** *at the border only* — the Intrusion-access contest **is** the defense; once access is held, **Willpower does not apply** and Puppeteer simply functions.
Seize the body outright — not a command but a **continuous operation**. You do not tell the target to act; you *drive*, moment to moment, and their voluntary body is yours to run as if it were your own for as long as you hold on. It **enters through the mind but does not alter it**: Intrusion opens the connection, and Puppeteer follows that connection *past cognition* to seize voluntary motor authority — reading nothing, editing nothing, leaving the mind wholly intact and wholly awake. That is *why* the target stays aware behind their own eyes, feels their body stop being theirs, and **remembers every second** — a conscious passenger, not a corrupted will.

**Its defense is unlike anything else's, and this is the point.** Dominate is resisted by *will* — it addresses the will, so a strong enough will refuses the command and can throw it off even after it lands; the contest is fought inside, and re-fought. Puppeteer addresses the *body*, so once the connection is established there is nothing left to contest: Willpower, however towering, is screaming in a room it has been locked out of. **The entire defense is the border** — Barriers keeping Intrusion from ever establishing access (a trained mind resists that contest on Barriers; an untrained mind or mundane on raw Willpower — but that is a *border* defense, keeping them out, not an inner one that fights them once in). Hold the wall and you are untouchable. Lose it and there is no second line: the instant they are in, they have the controls. This is the internal spine of the whole Domain — its lighter techniques (Suggestion, Compulsion, Emotion) bargain with the *will* and can be refused by Willpower even after access; Puppeteer bypasses the will, and only the wall ever mattered.

Holdable only while you concentrate; the per-turn Strain caps duration hard. ● forces simple acts; ●●●●● is total puppetry — every voluntary muscle, down to whether they may speak (the autonomic floor stays beyond reach: no stopping a heart, and a held breath ends in a blackout that breathes on its own). It occupies the far corner of *both* axes at once — the dirtiest act short of Kinesis, and the most dissonant in the game — which is why it makes the angriest witnesses, why professionals reach for Memory instead, and why puppeting someone into self-harm is **anathema**, the Strain-table corner that frays *you* toward the psidolon in the same stroke. (Same power at the root as Dominate; the medium changed everything — see [The Interred](The%20Interred.md).)

**When it is the right tool — rarely, and briefly.** Praxis catalogued Cognitive Command because completeness demanded it, and noted in the same breath that it expected the protocol warranted almost nowhere. Nearly every problem has a cheaper answer (see *The Locked Door*, [Combat and Conflict](Combat%20and%20Conflict%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md)). The one case Puppeteer owns is the **guaranteed physical act against a will you cannot bargain with**: the hostage-taker who will *never* be talked into lowering the knife because he believes — perhaps correctly — that lowering it means his death; the target whose will is too fortified, too motivated, or simply not the kind of mind that Suggestion and Compulsion move. Every technique that *asks* loses to how badly he means it. Puppeteer does not ask. If you are through his defenses, his will is not in the conversation — you open his hand and the knife drops, and how much he wants to hold on is irrelevant. That is the whole of its use: convert *if I get in* into *then it is certain*, and pay for the certainty in tether. **And you pay by the second.** Puppeteer is active driving, never a set-and-hold construct (contrast *Holding is not sustaining* in [The Fraying](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md)) — the Strain never stops climbing while you hold the reins. Seconds is a normal use; a few minutes is the far edge of what even a legendary tether survives; there is no version that runs for an hour. The Gifted who tries to keep a puppet for the long haul is writing their own Snap, one turn at a time.

## Projection
*Separating mind from body. The most personally dangerous Domain — a snap while projected is instant Untethering. MetaConcert register: Projection Domain protocols. Base Strain 3.*

**Astral Step** · Scaling ●–●●●●●
**Pool** Wits + Projection · **Strain** 3 (**+1 per range band**) · your body lies vulnerable.
Full separation of consciousness. ● reaches line-of-sight, ●●●●● spans the globe and touches the Deep Umbra. Distance is the killer: every range band is +1 Strain, and a Snap Check while projected means you don't come back. Many Gifted refuse to learn this Domain at all.

**Remote Viewing** · Threshold ●●
**Pool** Perception + Projection · **Strain** 2 · "projection lite."
Extend awareness to observe a distant place without full separation. Safer and cheaper than Astral Step, limited to observation. The reconnaissance tool of choice.

**Dreamwalk** · Threshold ●●●
**Pool** Wits + Projection · **Strain** 3 · **Resists** Willpower (if hostile).
Project into a sleeping mind's dreams — to speak unseen, to glimpse what the dreamer hides from themselves, to plant an image that feels like their own nightmare. Intimate, deniable, and a favorite of recruiters and quiet interrogators. The waking target often recalls only "a strange dream."

**Possession** · Threshold ●●●●● · **FORBIDDEN**
**Pool** Wits + Projection · **Strain** 6 + **automatic Snap Check** · **Resists** Willpower + Barriers.
Attempt to seize another body while projected — what psidolons do to survive. Succeed and you are in someone else's flesh; fail and you scatter. For a living Gifted this is voluntary self-Untethering by degrees; the Inquisition executes anyone caught even researching it. Listed for completeness and dread, not for casual play.

## Memory
*The scalpel. The professional's preferred tool — precise, self-cleaning, and the engine of the Masquerade's maintenance. Requires Intrusion access. MetaConcert register: Memory Domain protocols. Base Strain 2.*

**The Mind-Palace** · Threshold ● · *(no MetaConcert protocol name — there is nothing here to launder)*
**Pool** Intelligence + Memory · **Strain** 1 to encode, 1 to retrieve in full detail, **0 to hold** · **Resists** nothing — the mind is your own, or the material was entrusted willingly.
Hold information as a stable indexed construct in your own mind: your memories, or knowledge someone gave you to keep. Successes set fidelity and how long it holds before it needs refreshing. Disposition does not apply — there is no second will in the circuit to be consonant or dissonant *with*, which is the whole reason a lifetime archive is survivable where a career of field-work is not. A set construct **decays** untended and must be revisited periodically (a cheap Memory action); a mind has a finite **capacity**, and packed past it the borrowed material begins to crowd the self. Guarding a palace is a **Barriers** function, not a Memory one.
**Note:** the cost is in the making and the tending, never in the having — see *Holding is not sustaining* in [The Fraying — Game Mechanics](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md).

**Mind Wipe** · Threshold ●● · *(MetaConcert: the Mnemonic Excision protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Memory · **Strain** 2 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers.
Erase a specific memory or fact. Successes set how cleanly — crude wipes leave gaps the mind worries at; ●●●●● leaves no seam. The basic cleanup tool.

**Alter Memory** · Threshold ●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Mnemonic Redaction protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Memory · **Strain** 2 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers.
Change details within a real memory rather than erasing it — hair color, the day, who was present. Subtler and harder to detect than wholesale fabrication because the scaffolding stays real. The professional's tweak.

**False Memory** · Threshold ●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Mnemonic Fabrication protocol)*
**Pool** Manipulation + Memory · **Strain** 3 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers.
Implant a fabricated memory. Must be plausible to take; successes set complexity and durability. "The accident was brake failure, not a flying car."

**Memory Rewrite** · Threshold ●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Mnemonic Reconstruction protocol)*
**Pool** Intelligence + Memory · **Strain** 3 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers · time-intensive.
Wholesale reconstruction of a memory complex — elaborate false histories, deep-cover legends, total erasure of a person from someone's life. Hours of work for big jobs; the master cleanup and the technical heart of Correction.

**False Thoughts** · Threshold ●●●●
**Pool** Manipulation + Memory · **Strain** 2 · self-targeted, maintained.
Prepare a deceptive surface-thought profile so that when someone *does* read you, they find a cover story instead of the truth. The deep-cover Operative's armor; exhausting to hold under scrutiny.

**Lockbox** · Threshold ●●●
**Pool** Intelligence + Memory · **Strain** 2 · self or willing subject.
Seal specific memories of your own behind an internal barrier, inaccessible even to you until you choose to open them — so a captured agent has nothing to surrender and a Deep Dive finds a locked door. Forcing one open is a contested extraction at high difficulty. Parasyte and Operative tradecraft.

**Binding Construct** · Threshold ●●●●●
**Pool** Intelligence + Memory (with Projection support) · **Strain** 4 · Maintainer specialty.
Install a persistent psionic construct in another mind. The **lattice-binding** configuration is core Maintainer competence (this is Integration). The **dormant-trigger** configuration is the MAD doctrine's seed and is classified above Maintainer level. Invisible to standard Detection; identifying or cleanly severing one demands master expertise working in reverse. Rough severance is the Chicago-Lattice-Disruption kind: traumatic to host and network alike.

## Detection
*The outbound read. Everything a mind broadcasts and cannot help broadcasting — presence, surface thought, shown emotion, psionic signature, tether. Passive, and the least straining Domain. Content-reading is defeated only by a mind that isn't broadcasting (bracing, a held Empty Mind, a disciplined calm); presence-sensing, by active Barriers concealment. MetaConcert register: Detection Domain protocols. Base Strain 1.*

> **The innate floor.** Before any dot, a psion *receives* — that is what being one is. An unbraced psion takes in genuine surface cognition from every unshielded mind nearby: real thoughts, emotions, images, activated memory fragments, not vague impressions. The signal is rich and true; what it is not is *usable* — it arrives indiscriminate, poorly attributed, simultaneous, contextless, hard to interpret. A firehose of other people's minds with no valve and no labels. **Bracing** is the valve (it gates the flood to something survivable, and contains your own broadcast in the same act — see [The Fraying](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md) §9). **Detection dots are trained attention within the gated field:** each removes a limitation — *select* one mind from the crowd, *attribute* a thought to its thinker, *hold* a single stream, read cleanly *at range.* The raw sense collects everything; skill (dots, and the right **Ability** to comprehend the content) decides what you can use.

**Skim** · Scaling ●–●●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Superficial Cognitive Assay)*
**Pool** Perception + Detection · **Strain** 1.
Direct the surface-sense at one chosen mind and read what it is openly broadcasting — thoughts, emotions, images. ● catches strong feeling and the gist of loud thought; ●●●●● is a clean, attributed map of active consciousness. **Not resisted by Barriers or Willpower** — it reads a broadcast, and the only defense is not to broadcast: a **braced** mind, a held **Empty Mind**, or a disciplined calm returns *nothing.* Trivial on the unbraced (mundanes, sleepers, the Unshielded). *Reaching past the surface to what a mind holds back is a different act — see **Dig**, under Intrusion.*

**Thoughtsense** · Scaling ●–●●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Cognitive Sweep)*
**Pool** Perception + Detection · **Strain** 1.
Sonar for minds. Detects the presence and position of active consciousness — ● the immediate area, ●●●●● a city-scale map of mental signatures. This reads *presence,* not content, so it is defeated not by bracing but by active **Thoughtshield** concealment; useless against the truly mindless.

**Empathic Sense** · Scaling ●–●●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Affective Assay)*
**Pool** Perception + Detection · **Strain** 1.
Reads the emotional state a mind is broadcasting rather than thought. Catches what Thoughtsense misses — Wraiths (pure emotion), heightened states, the residual emotional "echo" left in a place where something terrible happened (●●●●●). A calm, meditative, or braced mind broadcasts little and evades it. *(It reads the emotion a mind shows; how hard they are* suppressing *one is a **Dig** read, beneath the surface.)*

**Psionic Sense** · Threshold ●●
**Pool** Perception + Detection · **Strain** 1.
Feel psionics being used nearby — and identify the Domain, though not the specific technique. The Panopticon's tripwire for unauthorized activity. Detection activity is the one Domain this doesn't resolve cleanly: the MetaConcert's ambient lattice hum is *itself* a constant, low-grade Detection-shaped signal, so a live sweep by Thoughtsense or Empathic Sense reads against a noise floor that's always there. Telling a genuine sweep apart from routine background lattice traffic takes 3+ successes, or Detection ●●●● — below that, it's just static that might be someone, might be nothing. This never resolves *who* is sweeping or *what* they're after — only that a sweep, distinct from the ambient hum, is happening nearby.

**Resonance Sense** · Threshold ●●●
**Pool** Perception + Detection · **Strain** 1.
Identify the *kind* of supernatural before you — Kindred read "wrong/dead," Garou "wild," Mages "fractured," each a distinct resonance. Sees through basic concealment. Essential Operative kit.

**Trace** · Threshold ●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Psionic Signature Index)*
**Pool** Perception + Detection · **Strain** 2 · **Resists** Wits + Barriers (of the quarry).
Follow a psionic signature back toward its source — a recent technique's residue, or a specific mind you have read before and "locked onto." The hunter's tool: how the Panopticon turns a flag into an address. A live psionic connection may also be traced back to its source, allowing a defender to attempt to identify an active intruder before the connection is broken — resisted by the intruder's Barriers rather than a quarry's. Counter-intelligence tradecraft, not just a hunter's tool.

**Tether Sense** · Threshold ●●●● · *(MetaConcert: the Tether Integrity Assay)*
**Pool** Perception + Detection · **Strain** 1.
Perceive the metaphysical condition of another's tether — healthy, strained, fraying, critical — directly, the way a structural engineer reads stress fractures. This is what flags a Gifted for the White Room before any behavioral symptom shows. The Panopticon's defining instrument, and one most Gifted never learn. Pierces even master Thoughtshield where tether *condition* is concerned.

**It reads material as well as condition, and that is what it was actually built for.** Every living thing has a tether, so the technique returns a reading on almost anything with a mind: a mundane's, a Gifted's, and — since the Embrace reforges rather than deletes — a **Kindred's**, which registers as unmistakably present and unmistakably not living, a tether that was burned and recast around something else. None of these is an alarm. The alarm is **absence.** A psidolon is the one thing in the setting that returns *nothing where something must be*, and the answer is trusted precisely because nothing else gives it — a fact that only holds because the undead have tethers too. If Kindred read as tetherless, the psidolon alarm would be worthless in any city with vampires in it, which is every city. Tether Sense is a fraying gauge in daily practice. It is a **psidolon detector** by design.

## Storyteller Notes

**Pool sizes run small.** A starting specialist throws perhaps 6–8 dice (Attribute 3 + Domain 3, plus an Ability where relevant); against a shielded Gifted, half of that gets cancelled. Psionics is precise, not overwhelming — the drama is in narrow margins and the Strain you spend chasing one more success.

**Let resistance feel like the network-security model.** Barriers is a firewall: it doesn't stop the attempt, it degrades the result. Narrate a partly-cancelled Skim as static, fragments, the *shape* of a thought without its content.

**Reskin freely.** These names and numbers are scaffolding. A Heterodox self-taught Gifted might have a technique the taxonomy has no box for; a Cult "gift" might be a reskinned Suggestion with a transcendence gloss. The seven Domains are Praxis's map, not the territory — and the map being incomplete is canon.
