# The Seven Domains: Psionic Techniques

**The Building Blocks of Mental Power**

---

## Overview

Psionic abilities are organized into seven fundamental Domains, each representing a distinct aspect of mental manipulation. Every Gifted has natural aptitude in some Domains and struggles with others. Mastery comes through practice, discipline, and accepting the strain each use places on the tether.

**The Seven Domains:**
- **Barriers** - Mental defense and fortification
- **Kinesis** - Telekinesis and kinetic manipulation
- **Intrusion** - Telepathy and mind reading
- **Control** - Mind domination and compulsion
- **Projection** - Astral travel and consciousness extension
- **Memory** - Information manipulation within minds
- **Detection** - Sensing supernatural signatures

**A note on the Trigger:** The power reaching through the tether requires sufficient tether resilience — not everyone who might develop the power does so, and individuals with insufficient tether resilience who force a Trigger do not survive the attempt. Training remains essential even for those with sufficient resilience; the physiology enabling a Trigger does not guarantee safe use without proper technique, any more than having the physiology to perform a split guarantees performing one without injury.

---

## Understanding Techniques

Within each Domain, specific abilities are called **Techniques**. Techniques function in two ways:

**Scaling Techniques:**
- The technique itself scales with your Domain rating
- Example: Thoughtsense at Detection ● gives basic nearby mind detection
- Thoughtsense at Detection ●●●●● gives precise, long-range mental mapping
- You learn the technique once, it grows with your Domain mastery

**Threshold Techniques:**
- Require minimum Domain rating to learn
- Once learned, can be used at various power levels
- Example: Memory Rewrite requires Memory ●●● minimum
- Can then be used from ●●● (basic) to ●●●●● (total reconstruction)
- Represents techniques too complex or dangerous for novices

## Domain Boundaries: The Network Security Model

Understanding how Domains interact is crucial to effective psionic practice:

**BARRIERS (Firewall):**
- Blocks incoming mental connections
- Different types and strengths of protection
- Can't stop attempts, just makes success harder
- Passive defense maintained within strain limits

**DETECTION (Packet Sniffing):**
- Reads the surface a mind broadcasts — the traffic it is already sending in the clear
- Trivial on the unbraced (mundanes, sleepers, the Unshielded — all transmitting)
- Can't force anything, only picks up what's being sent
- Against **bracing** or a held **Empty Mind**: nothing is broadcast — the wire is silent

**INTRUSION (Unauthorized Access):**
- Active reach *past* the broadcast surface, into what a mind holds back
- Reads the held content, or projects *into* the live mind — but does not edit (that's Memory)
- Must get past the **Barriers** wall first
- Skim sniffs the traffic; Intrusion breaks into the host

**MEMORY (File Manipulation):**
- REQUIRES Intrusion access first
- Edit, copy, delete mental information
- Cannot work without being "inside" the mind
- Intrusion + Memory often used together

**CONTROL (Remote Administration):**
- REQUIRES Intrusion access first
- Seize control of another's actions
- Most invasive form of mental manipulation
- Override victim's will entirely

**KINESIS (Physics Server Manipulation):**
- Manipulating physical reality, not minds
- Extremely high tether strain
- "Hacking" the laws of physics directly
- Flashy, powerful, dangerous to user

**PROJECTION (Remote Connection):**
- Consciousness separates from body while tethered
- Like a diver with umbilical cord to surface
- Astral travel, remote viewing
- If tether snaps while projected, you become psidolon

## Dirty vs. Clean Psionics

Among the Gifted, certain Domains are called **dirty** — not because they cost more, but because they leave something behind for someone else to clean up. (The MetaConcert, which does the cleaning, files the same distinction as *high-* and *low-attribution*: how much of an incident can be traced back to a mind.)

**This is not the same axis as Strain.** Dirt is about what you *left;* Strain is about what you *forced* — see the disposition dial in [The Fraying — Game Mechanics](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md). The two come apart constantly, and the gap between them is where tradecraft lives. A consonant nudge in a crowded lobby is free and filthy. An anathema override in a sealed room is a **clean** atrocity: no witness, no cleanup, nothing to file — and it frays you white. The Gifted who confuse the two do not last. They mistake *nobody saw me* for *it cost me nothing*, and the tether keeps its own books regardless of what the paperwork says.

**DIRTY DOMAINS (High Attribution, Heavy Cleanup):**

**KINESIS** - The worst offender:
- Visible to everyone (flying objects, kinetic impacts)
- Physical evidence everywhere
- Witnesses by the dozens
- "Great, you threw a car. Now we need Memory teams for an entire city block."
- Considered brutish by sophisticated Gifted
- BUT genuinely useful in combat situations

**CONTROL** - Obvious and temporary:
- Victim fully aware they've been overridden
- Massive violation creating angry witnesses
- Only works while maintained (tether strain limits duration)
- Victim remembers everything when you stop
- "You made someone rob a bank. Now they're screaming about mind control to police."

**CLEAN DOMAINS (Low Attribution, Minimal Cleanup):**

**MEMORY** - The preferred tool:
- Edit what they remember about the event
- No anger because they don't remember violation
- Self-cleaning (mind accepts false memory as real)
- "Why Control someone when you can make them believe they wanted to do it?"

**INTRUSION** - Clean if done right:
- Victim may not know you were there
- No physical evidence
- Read and leave quietly
- Only messy if careless

**DETECTION** - Completely invisible:
- Pure observation
- Zero evidence
- Nobody knows you're watching

**BARRIERS** - Pure defense:
- No witnesses unless someone attacks mentally
- Leaves no trace

**PROJECTION** - Mostly invisible:
- Astral form not typically visible to mundanes
- Can scout without physical presence
- Only dirty if you interact with physical world

**The MetaConcert's hierarchy of respect:**
1. Memory - "The scalpel. Precise, elegant, self-cleaning."
2. Intrusion - "Necessary for Memory. Intelligence gathering."
3. Detection - "No cleanup needed. Professional."
4. Barriers - "Shows discipline and training."
5. Projection - "Useful but risky to yourself."
6. Control - "Amateur hour. Creates witnesses."
7. Kinesis - "Might as well announce 'PSIONICS HERE' with fireworks."

## Broadcast: Why Five Thousand Costs Barely More Than Five

Here is the thing the other splats cannot do, and the reason the MetaConcert is a government rather than a guild.

A mage who wants to reach a crowd is working against arithmetic and against Paradox: the effect has to cover them, and every pair of eyes that sees a vulgar act makes the reckoning worse. Worse than that, and this is the trap with no floor — **you cannot fix vulgar with vulgar.** The cover-up is witnessed by the same crowd that witnessed the thing it was covering. There is no exit from that loop that costs less than the act did.

A Gifted has no loop, because the power raises no Paradox at all. The cost is private and paid to the tether. And the reach is not arithmetic, because psionics was never point-to-point to begin with — every unshielded mind pours its surface out for free, and a psion standing in a crowd receives *all of them at once*. The channel was always one-to-many. It simply spent most of its history pointed the wrong way.

Turn it outward and you get **broadcast:** one act, every mind in reach, at once.

**The useful comparison is a graphics processor.** You do not address the thousands of cores one at a time. You hand them a single instruction and they all run it simultaneously, and it is fast *precisely because nothing branches* — the moment different cores need to do different work, the parallelism collapses and you are back to doing everything one at a time. That is exactly the shape of this. As long as the instruction is simple and identical for every mind, five thousand and five million are not meaningfully different jobs.

*"You did not see that."* — identical for everyone, and it needs to know nothing about anybody. It costs a master real tether across a crowded plaza, and it is entirely payable.

*"Forget the man in the grey coat."* — the grey coat is filed in five thousand different places in five thousand different lives. There is no single instruction. This is five thousand jobs, and nobody has that much tether.

**Which yields the law, and it is the most important sentence in this section: violation cannot be parallelized.** Overcoming a will is a contest with one particular person, and a contest is not an instruction. So everything at the coercive end of this book — everything that actually frays you toward the psidolon — is permanently, structurally retail. You can make a city forget. You will never make a city obey.

That is not a mercy anyone designed. It is a property of the machinery, and it is the reason the MetaConcert's authority over its own people has to be built out of leverage, dependency, Correction and the Inquisition rather than a single broadcast order. Their tyranny is labour-intensive. If it were not, there would be no Projects, and no one left to write this.

## Domain: Barriers

**Philosophy:** Mental defense is the foundation of psionic practice. Even the most powerful Gifted is vulnerable without proper shielding.

**Primary Use:** Protecting against mental intrusion, manipulation, and detection

**Tether Strain:** Low, and **it never becomes permanent damage.** Holding a wall is an active contest, so it tires you — but nothing leaves the body, so the Strain of defending never hardens into Fray, and rest clears it completely. A psion can hold the line all night and wake up whole. The *offensive* half of the Domain is another matter: Mind Barbs, Feedback and an Aegis stretched over other people all reach back out through the door, and reaching out frays like anything else.

### The Unshielded State

Before a newly Triggered Gifted is **braced**, they exist in the **Unshielded** state: uncontrolled bidirectional thought leakage with no containment structure. (The MetaConcert's clinical term for the condition is *Exposed*; Gifted in the vernacular call it being *Raw*.) Thoughts leak out, others' thoughts leak in, no filter exists. The state is not itself tether damage — the tether is intact at onset — but it is what *permits* tether damage, because the missing skin is missing outbound as well as in. An untrained mind does not only leak; it **reaches**, involuntarily and unknowingly, and the tether pays for every reach whether or not the mind attached to it understood what it did. **Fraying is physics, not pedagogy.** The Unshielded fray blind, from acts they cannot identify, with no concept of a cost and therefore no reason to stop.

The Unshielded state is also psionically loud, which is how Panopticon Detection sweeps locate newly Triggered children. Left untreated, prolonged Unshielded state produces escalating psychic overload: cognitive destabilization, identity fragmentation, and symptoms clinically resembling severe psychiatric conditions — and, in those who last longest, accumulated damage nobody has been measuring. Bracing is the intervention that resolves all of it at once, which is why it is the first thing anyone is taught and why the Integrators move quickly.

### Known Techniques

**Bracing** (Foundational pre-technique — no Domain rating required)
- The first technique taught to every newly Triggered Gifted
- Not a formal Barriers technique but the experiential foundation on which all Barriers work is built
- Taught through controlled Intrusion: the Integrator enters the child's mind gently, holds a presence, and instructs the child to push back
- The child learns what a mental boundary feels like by producing one — experiential learning that cannot be described, only demonstrated
- Once bracing is established, the Unshielded state resolves: bidirectional thought leakage stops, psychic overload recedes
- Bracing is *not* the Barriers Domain: it is a bidirectional membrane — a *skin* — gating the inbound flood down to something survivable and containing the outbound broadcast in the same act, where Barriers dots build the trained inbound wall against directed Intrusion. Skin, not armor. It sits below the Domain scale — the felt sense of a boundary before the child has vocabulary for one, or any wall of their own
- Everything else in Barriers training (and all other Domain training) builds on this foundation
- Systematized by Praxis; taught by Integrators

*Practical notes:* Bracing is not rated on the Domain scale because it is not a technique in the conventional sense — it is the precondition for technique. A Gifted who cannot brace cannot learn Thoughtshield, because Thoughtshield requires the ability to feel and maintain a mental boundary, and bracing is where that ability is first established. The pedagogical method — Intrusion followed by instruction to resist — means that every Gifted's first experience of their own Barriers capability is pushing back against someone else's mind inside theirs. This is why Integrators, not Barriers specialists, teach it: the teaching requires Intrusion, not Barriers.

**Thoughtshield** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●)
- Mental invisibility and protection — the formal development of what bracing begins
- ● Basic: Muffle surface thoughts, create mental "static"
- ●● Adequate: Significant protection against casual Detection
- ●●● Strong: Can hide from most Panopticon monitoring
- ●●●● Advanced: Genuine privacy, exhausting to maintain constantly
- ●●●●● Master: Near-perfect mental concealment, blocks even dedicated searches

*Practical notes:* This is the primary privacy tool for Gifted in the lattice. Even master-level Thoughtshield can't hide strong emotions completely, and maintaining maximum shielding constantly is exhausting. Most Gifted keep it at ●●-●●● most of the time.

**Mind Barbs** (Threshold: ●●)
- Defensive mental landmines
- Trigger when someone attempts Intrusion
- Deal psychic pain/disorientation to attacker
- More effective at higher Barriers ratings
- Can discourage casual mental probing

*Practical notes:* Popular among Operatives and those who expect mental attack. The Inquisition teaches this as standard defensive doctrine.

**Deadening** (Threshold: ●●)
- Psionic analgesia — floods the mind's pain channels shut
- Suppresses the felt experience of pain without touching the injury
- Blocks pain, not damage: you still bleed, still weaken, still die
- When it ends, the suppressed pain returns all at once
- Distinct from blocking thought — this blocks the body's signal to the mind

*Practical notes:* The technique every field operative wishes they had and every veteran warns against leaning on. Where the Akashic Brotherhood's Do trains the body to endure pain, Deadening simply switches the signal off — an endorphin flood rather than discipline. Its quiet danger is twofold. A Gifted who cannot feel their wounds will fight on into ones that kill them. And — worse — they cannot feel the ache, the body-rejection, the creeping dissociation that warn of fraying. Many a Gifted has run Deadening through a desperate hour and learned, only when it lifted, how close to the snap they had pushed. It blocks pain, never the fraying underneath it.

**Feedback** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Active counter-attack against mental intrusion
- Delivers painful psionic static to attacker
- Can force attacker to break connection
- Requires awareness of the attack
- Higher ratings allow more sustained feedback

*Practical notes:* The difference between Mind Barbs (passive trap) and Feedback (active response). Requires some skill to use effectively without damaging yourself.

**Aegis** (Threshold: ●●●●)
- Extends your shielding over others or a space, not just yourself
- Protected minds benefit from your Barriers as if it were their own
- Covers willing allies, a room, a vehicle, mundanes during a breach
- The strain of holding it scales with the number of minds sheltered
- The foundation of every quiet space the resistance depends on

*Practical notes:* The signature working of Project Lacuna, and the reason a safehouse can be more than four walls. An Aegis cast over a cleanup team keeps the whole crew off the Panopticon's read; cast over a frightened mundane family, it muffles the psychic noise of a supernatural incident until the Memory specialists arrive. Maintaining it over many minds is exhausting, and the instant it drops, everyone beneath it is visible again at once. To hold an Aegis is to be the wall that hides everyone behind you, and to know exactly what it costs to be that wall.

## Domain: Kinesis

**Philosophy:** The most physically obvious Domain. Direct manipulation of kinetic energy and matter through mental will alone.

**Primary Use:** Telekinesis, physical combat, moving objects

**Tether Strain:** EXTREMELY HIGH - Kinesis users burn through tether integrity faster than any other Domain

### Known Techniques

**Telekinesis** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●)
- Move objects with mental force
- ● Basic: Small objects (coins, keys), line of sight
- ●● Adequate: Person-sized objects, some distance
- ●●● Strong: Car-sized objects, combat applications
- ●●●● Advanced: Large structures, sustained levitation
- ●●●●● Master: Building-scale manipulation, devastating force

*Practical notes:* The iconic psionic ability. Extremely useful in combat but creates massive cleanup problems. Every public use requires Memory team deployment. Strain increases with weight and distance.

**Kinetic Barrier** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Create force fields for physical protection
- Blocks bullets, blades, physical attacks
- Drains constantly while maintained
- Not the same as mental Barriers (different Domain)
- Higher ratings allow stronger, larger shields

*Practical notes:* Despite the name, this is Kinesis Domain, not Barriers. Protects body, not mind. Extreme tether strain makes sustained use dangerous.

**Deflection** (Threshold: ●●)
- A quick, reflexive flick of kinetic force
- Knocks aside a single incoming attack — a bullet, a blade, a thrown object
- Faster and far cheaper than raising a full Kinetic Barrier
- Handles one threat at a time, in the instant
- Requires awareness of the attack to turn it

*Practical notes:* The Kinesis user's emergency reflex — the difference between a bullet that hits and one that goes wide. Where a Kinetic Barrier is a sustained wall paid for every turn it stands, Deflection is a single desperate swat, gone the moment it's done. The few Rebels and Operatives who carry Kinesis at all tend to learn this first: it is the cheapest thing the loudest Domain offers, and nearly the only use quiet enough not to paint a target on yourself the moment you try it.

## Domain: Intrusion

**Philosophy:** The inbound door. Reaching *past* the surface a mind shows — to the truth it holds back, or to project into it. Where Detection reads the broadcast, Intrusion goes beneath it. This is the gateway Memory and Control require.

**Primary Use:** Reaching the held-back truth, gathering intelligence, enabling Memory and Control techniques

**Tether Strain:** Moderate to high (active penetration past a defended surface creates strain)

**Critical Note:** Intrusion is REQUIRED before Memory or Control techniques can function. Reading a mind's *broadcast* (Skim, Detection) is not access — you must reach *past* the surface to manipulate.

### Known Techniques

**Dig** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●)
- Reach past the surface a mind is broadcasting to what it holds beneath
- ● Basic: sense that something is being held back; the shape of a suppressed feeling
- ●● Adequate: the lie under a calm answer; a guarded thought
- ●●● Strong: read held content in detail; establish sustained access
- ●●●● Advanced: rapid and deep; hold access against a wall
- ●●●●● Master: complete sub-surface reading

*Practical notes:* The fundamental Intrusion technique, and the standard way to establish access for Memory and Control. Where **Skim** (a Detection technique) reads what a mind is openly broadcasting, Dig reaches under it — the feeling being suppressed, the thought kept off the surface, the lie beneath the words. Works freely on an unwalled mind; against a trained Gifted you pit your Intrusion against their Barriers.

**Thought Probe** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Direct extraction of specific information
- Requires successful Intrusion access (a held Dig) first
- Target must be focused on or recently thought about information
- A targeted, forceful Dig for one fact
- Inquisition staple for interrogation

*Practical notes:* "Where is the safe house?" "What did you tell them?" Targeted information extraction rather than general reading. Higher ratings allow deeper, faster extraction.

**Deep Dive** (Threshold: ●●●●)
- Dangerous deep exploration of mind
- Requires immobilized, non-resistant target
- Access to buried memories, subconscious
- Extended time required
- Risk of damaging target's psyche
- Very high tether strain

*Practical notes:* This is what the Inquisition does during serious interrogation. Target usually restrained, unconscious, or otherwise unable to resist. Can break minds if careless.

**Psionic Scream** (Threshold: ●●)
- Omnidirectional mental burst
- Causes disorientation, pain, confusion
- Affects everyone in range (ally and enemy)
- Crude but effective panic button
- Range and intensity scale with rating

*Practical notes:* The mental flashbang. Useful when surrounded or desperate. Indiscriminate effect makes it dangerous in populated areas.

**Mindspeech** (Threshold: ●●)
- Direct mind-to-mind communication
- Carries actual conversation, not merely surface reading
- Effortless with a willing mind; forced channels are possible but resented
- Range extends with effort and strain
- The basis of silent coordination in the field

*Practical notes:* What most people imagine telepathy to be, and the one Intrusion technique that is not fundamentally a violation. Lattice-bound Gifted reach each other this way as a matter of course; off-grid cells rely on it for silence where a radio would be overheard. Forcing a channel into an unwilling mind is another thing entirely — it means breaching their Barriers first, and the recipient always knows they have been spoken into. A whisper inside the skull is very hard to mistake for one's own thought.

**Mirage** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Overlays a mind's live perception — conjure what isn't there, or suppress what is
- The infiltrator's deflection: not invisibility, but becoming *not worth noticing*
- Scales from a single overlooked detail to a full false scene laid over a crowd
- Edits perception, not reality — and works only on minds, never on machines
- A target with hard contradictory evidence may see through it

*Practical notes:* The quintessential ghost-in-the-building technique, and the closest a Gifted comes to moving through a place as though they were never in it. The clean operator does not make themselves invisible — invisibility is a thing people notice the absence of. They make themselves a *somebody else's problem:* the guard's eyes pass over the open door, the analyst's gaze slides off the figure at the terminal, and no alarm sounds because, as far as every living mind in the building is concerned, there is nothing to sound one about. Its great humility is the camera. Mirage fools minds, and only minds — the lens has none, the recording does not forget, and more than one overconfident operative has slipped past every guard in the building only to be undone by a thirty-dollar webcam. And it overlays perception without touching memory: pair it with a Mind Wipe to close the loop, or the people you fooled will, in time, come to wonder.

**The Fugue** (Threshold: ●●●●●) *(MetaConcert: the Cognitive Fugue protocol)*
- Two minds in direct contact, concept meeting concept, with no language or senses in between
- Not a place and not a journey — both minds stay exactly where they are; each *renders* the exchange as somewhere, because a mind has no other way to hold it
- Requires an already-held Intrusion access, sustained; a defended mind refuses outright, with no partial effect
- Kinesis does not function here, and neither does casting — there is no substance for either to grip
- The rarest technique in the Domain to learn, and the one arena in the World of Darkness that belongs to the Gifted

*Practical notes:* The capstone, and the only technique in the book whose vernacular and institutional names are the same word — because there was no honest older name to refuse. Praxis did not launder this one; Praxis **built** it. Before them, two masters meeting mind-to-mind was real, formless, and unteachable, described afterward in metaphors no two survivors shared; Praxis studied enough of those clashes to find the shape underneath and wrote a curriculum for it. That is the deepest reach of the taxonomy anywhere in this book: even mind-war, at its rawest personal extremity, now happens inside a form a research institution designed to be legible. The counter-vocabulary has a defiant word for nearly everything. For this it has nothing. *(Full rules for what happens once the contact is established are in [Combat and Conflict — Game Mechanics](Combat%20and%20Conflict%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md), "The Fugue: Mental Duels." Use it the way a Garou uses Crinos — an arena, not a last resort.)*

## Domain: Control

**Philosophy:** The most invasive Domain. Direct override of another's will and bodily control.

**Primary Use:** Forcing actions, compelling obedience, dominating minds

**Tether Strain:** High to very high (fighting another consciousness)

**Critical Note:** Control REQUIRES successful Intrusion first. You must be "inside" their mind before you can seize control.

### Known Techniques

**Puppeteer** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●) *(MetaConcert: the Cognitive Command protocol)*
- Force specific actions while victim remains aware
- REQUIRES active Intrusion
- Victim remembers being controlled afterward
- Can only maintain while concentrating (tether strain limits duration)
- ● Basic: Simple actions, short duration
- ●● Adequate: Complex actions, several minutes
- ●●● Strong: Extended control, nuanced actions
- ●●●● Advanced: Multiple commands, sophisticated behavior
- ●●●●● Master: Complete puppet control, extended duration

*Practical notes:* The Gifted's raw mental seizure of another body — and, though almost no Gifted alive knows it, the ancestor of vampiric Dominate. Where the Kindred version works through the Blood (eye contact, proximity, and the power to rewrite memory so the victim never knows), Puppeteer works through pure mind: instant, needing no gaze, reaching as far as a thought — but overt. The victim is fully conscious, feels their body stop being theirs, and remembers every second. It **enters through the mind but does not alter it**: Intrusion opens the connection, and Puppeteer follows that connection *past cognition* to the body's motor controls — reading nothing, editing nothing. That is exactly why the mind stays awake to witness. The difference from Dominate is not degree but *kind*: Dominate commands the **will** — a single instruction the victim's own mind carries out — where Puppeteer bypasses the will entirely and **drives the body**, continuously, an intact mind held prisoner and made to watch its own hands obey. That is also why the Domain is called Control and not Domination: this is not a command that runs and ends, it is the moment-to-moment operation of another person for as long as you concentrate — you own every voluntary muscle they have, down to whether they may speak.

And it is why the *defense* against it is unlike any other. Dominate is resisted by will — a strong enough will refuses the command, even after it lands, which is how a Tremere elder shrugs off a neonate. Puppeteer cannot be resisted that way, because it never addresses the will: once someone is past your Barriers and holding Intrusion access, your Willpower is screaming in a room it has been locked out of. The whole defense is the wall at the border. Let them in and it is already over — which is the exact terror the [Barriers ruling](The%20Best%20Defense%20-%20Barriers%20and%20the%20Mental%20Domain.md) is built around, seen from the losing side. The medium makes the difference: blood is insidious and sinks in; mind is immediate and shows. This is why it creates the angriest witnesses in the game — the mind was entered but never *altered*, only caged — and why sophisticated Gifted consider it unforgivably dirty and reach for Memory instead. The MetaConcert files it, with characteristic warmth, as the Cognitive Command protocol. *(On its kinship with vampiric Dominate, see [The Interred](The%20Interred.md).)*

**Emotion Manipulation** (Threshold: ●●)
- Alter emotional states directly
- Fear, calm, anger, attraction
- Subtler than Puppeteer
- Victim may not realize emotions are artificial
- Wears off when concentration ends

*Practical notes:* More subtle than Puppeteer but still requires Intrusion. Useful for interrogation (induce fear) or de-escalation (force calm). Effects fade quickly after release.

**Compulsion** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Plant specific behavioral compulsions
- "You must tell the truth"
- "You cannot leave this room"
- "You will forget what you saw"
- More subtle than Puppeteer
- Can persist briefly after Intrusion ends

*Practical notes:* Middle ground between Puppeteer (obvious control) and Memory manipulation (editing thoughts). Creates irresistible urges rather than direct control.

**Suggestion** (Threshold: ●●)
- A single, light, plausible command slipped into a compliant mind
- Works best on the unsuspecting and the weak-willed
- Cannot force an action the target would strongly refuse
- Subtler than Puppeteer, briefer than Compulsion
- Wears off quickly, leaving only faint puzzlement

*Practical notes:* The famous one — "you don't need to see my papers," "there's nothing down that hall worth checking." It is the gentlest thing the Control Domain can do, and the most deniable, which is exactly why it is buried under more folklore than any other technique: the theatrical hand-wave, the lowered voice, the steady unblinking stare. None of it does anything. The override is mental and works just as well with your hands in your pockets — but newcomers learn the gesture from stories and swear by it, and veterans find the whole performance quietly embarrassing. The wave is not the power. People do it anyway.

## Domain: Projection

**Philosophy:** Separation of consciousness from body. The most personally dangerous Domain—one snap and you're psidolon.

**Primary Use:** Astral travel, remote viewing, consciousness extension

**Tether Strain:** Very high (you're literally stretching your tether)

**Critical Warning:** If tether snaps while Projected, you immediately become Untethered. No second chances.

### Known Techniques

**Astral Step** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●)
- Separate consciousness from body
- ● Basic: Nearby locations, line of sight
- ●● Adequate: Same building, familiar places
- ●●● Strong: City-wide range, unfamiliar locations
- ●●●● Advanced: Continental distances
- ●●●●● Master: Global range, Deep Umbra access

*Practical notes:* Your body remains vulnerable. Tether strain increases with distance. The further you project, the closer you are to snapping. Many Gifted refuse to learn this Domain at all.

**Remote Viewing** (Threshold: ●●)
- Observe distant location without full projection
- Safer than full Astral Step
- Limited interaction capability
- Less strain than full projection
- Useful for reconnaissance

*Practical notes:* "Projection lite." You're not fully separated, just extending awareness. Still dangerous but less so than full Astral Step.

**Dreamwalk** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Project into the mind of a sleeping person, into their dreams
- Speak with them unseen; observe what their waking guard conceals
- Plant an image that surfaces to them as their own nightmare or revelation
- Intimate, deniable, and difficult to prove
- The dreamer usually recalls only "a strange dream"

*Practical notes:* A favorite of recruiters, gentle interrogators, and anyone who prefers their work to leave no fingerprints. The sleeping mind drops most of its guard, and a careful Dreamwalker can learn — or quietly suggest — a great deal without the target ever knowing they were visited. The Cult uses it to whisper transcendence to the vulnerable in their sleep; Parasyte uses it to pass a word no surveillance can intercept. It carries the standard price of all Projection: while you walk through another's dream, your own body lies somewhere undefended.

**Possession** (Threshold: ●●●●●)
- Attempt to take control of another body while projected
- EXTREMELY dangerous and forbidden by MetaConcert
- Essentially voluntary transformation into psidolon
- If you succeed, you're in someone else's body
- If you fail, you're scattered
- Automatic execution if discovered

*Practical notes:* This is what psidolons do to survive. For living Gifted to attempt it is considered crossing the line into becoming Untethered voluntarily. The Inquisition kills anyone caught even researching this technique.

## Domain: Memory

**Philosophy:** The surgeon's scalpel of mental manipulation. Precise, clean, elegant—the preferred tool of professionals.

**Primary Use:** Editing memories, implanting false information, erasing knowledge

**Tether Strain:** Moderate to high (depends on complexity of manipulation)

**Critical Note:** Memory REQUIRES successful Intrusion first. Cannot manipulate memories without mental access.

**Why Memory specialists dominate:** They ARE the cleanup crew. After every Masquerade breach, every Kinesis incident, every Control fiasco—Memory specialists make witnesses forget.

**The thing masters know and nobody else does:** erasure does not destroy a memory, it destroys the mind's *route* to it. Wiping cuts the paths of association a mind uses to reach something; the material stays put, unreachable, and — unlike a vampire's buried memory — never surfaces on its own. Genuine destruction requires *overwriting*: laying new structure over old until nothing beneath survives, which is slow, invasive, and only practical on a subject who is not going anywhere. That is the whole distinction between **Mind Wipe** (a stroke) and **Memory Rewrite** (hours), and it is the reason **Correction** requires a White Room rather than a quiet word in a car park. It also means nearly every memory the MetaConcert has ever taken is still in the head it was taken from, waiting for someone who knows to look. *(Full treatment, including why recovery can never be industrialised, in [The Seven Domains — Game Mechanics](The%20Seven%20Domains%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md), "Memory.")*

### Known Techniques

**The Mind-Palace** *(Preservation)* (Threshold: ●)
- Hold information — your own memories, or knowledge entrusted to you — as a stable, structured construct in your own mind, indexed for perfect recall
- The most basic Memory technique, taught to every candidate in their first months as a study aid and the foundation of all later Memory work
- **Encoding** a memory into the palace and **retrieving** one in full detail each cost Strain; simply *holding* an encoded construct costs nothing — it sits behind your Barriers indefinitely, like any settled memory
- Left untended, a construct slowly **decays**; a keeper must periodically revisit and refresh it — a ritual act, not a taxing one — to hold it perfect across years
- A single mind has a **capacity ceiling**; packed past it, the borrowed material begins to crowd and blur the self
- Guarding a palace against Intrusion, Detection, or the lattice is a **Barriers** function, not a Memory one — the two Domains together are what make a private archive truly private

*Practical notes:* Every Gifted can do a little of this; almost none build it into anything. In the ordinary case it is a memory athlete's trick — hold the dossier, the face, the sequence, without notes. Taken to its limit it becomes an identity and an institution. The **Preservers** are nothing but this technique at civilizational scale: the MetaConcert's true archive sits on no shelf but distributed across their palaces. The **Black Archivists** of Praxis carry the institution's buried history the same way. And the apostate [Marcus Thiel](Marcus_Thiel.md) keeps his murdered sister in a palace built for one — the same tool, the same *Memory-to-hold, Barriers-to-guard* build, turned to love instead of an archive. Because holding costs nothing and only tending does, a lifetime archive is sustainable where constant field-work would fray a Gifted to death; the limit is not Strain but capacity, decay, and the slow risk that a mind too full of other people's memory forgets which memories are its own. *(On why passive holding does not fray, see [The Fraying — Game Mechanics](The%20Fraying%20-%20Game%20Mechanics.md); on the living archives, [The Preservers Circuit](The%20Preservers%20Circuit.md) and [The Praxis Circuit](The%20Praxis%20Circuit.md), "The Black Archive.")*

**Mind Wipe** (Threshold: ●●)
- Erase specific memories or knowledge
- Requires Intrusion access
- Can target specific events, people, information
- Leaves gaps that mind may notice
- ●● Basic: Recent memories, simple erasure
- ●●● Strong: Older memories, complex events
- ●●●● Advanced: Multiple memories, pattern erasure
- ●●●●● Master: Surgical precision, no detectable gaps

*Practical notes:* The basic cleanup tool. "You didn't see the vampire." "You don't remember the flying car." Creates amnesia for specific events. Crude use leaves obvious gaps. Note that the dot ratings above buy *cleanliness of the cut*, never permanence — every wipe on this ladder severs a route rather than destroying the material, and a Master's seamless work is exactly as recoverable as an apprentice's ragged one by anyone with cause to dig. What the Master actually buys is that nobody ever acquires the cause.

**False Memory** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Implant completely fabricated memories
- Requires Intrusion access
- More complex than simple erasure
- Must be plausible to take hold
- Higher ratings allow more complex fabrications

*Practical notes:* Better than simple erasure. Instead of gap, victim has false memory. "The car accident was caused by brake failure, not telekinesis." Requires skill to make memories believable.

**Alter Memory** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Modify existing memories rather than erase/create
- Change details while keeping framework
- More subtle than False Memory
- Harder to detect than wholesale fabrication
- Maintains internal consistency better

*Practical notes:* The professional's tool. Tweak what they remember rather than replace it. "The attacker had brown hair, not blonde." "It happened Tuesday, not Wednesday." Subtle changes are harder to detect.

**Memory Rewrite** (Threshold: ●●●●)
- Complete reconstruction of memory
- Total control over what victim remembers
- Can create elaborate false histories
- Extremely complex and time-consuming
- Risk of creating inconsistencies
- Very high tether strain

*Practical notes:* The master technique. Rebuild entire memory structures. Used for deep cover, witness protection, or making someone forget they ever knew you existed. Requires hours of work for complex rewrites.

**False Thoughts** (Threshold: ●●●●)
- Create deceptive surface memory profile
- Protects against Intrusion/Detection
- Makes your mind "read" as having different memories
- Useful for deep cover operations
- Maintaining false profile is exhausting

*Practical notes:* Operatives use this for deep cover. When someone Skims your surface (or Digs beneath it), they find the cover story instead of the truth. Different from **bracing** (which stops you broadcasting at all) and **Barriers** (which blocks a reach-in): this is for when you *are* read anyway — you can't always stay silent, so instead you make the surface they find a convincing lie.

**Lockbox** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Seal specific memories of your own behind an internal barrier
- Locked memories are inaccessible — even to you — until you choose to open them
- A captured agent has nothing to surrender that they can reach
- Forcing one open is a contested extraction at high difficulty
- Can be set on a willing subject as readily as on oneself

*Practical notes:* The counterpart to False Thoughts, and the deep-cover operative's last line of defense. Where False Thoughts builds a convincing lie for an intruder to find, Lockbox simply removes the truth from reach — a Deep Dive strikes a sealed door instead of a hidden room. Parasyte and Operative tradecraft both lean on it: the real name, the real handler, the real mission, locked away where even a Deep Dive under torture cannot retrieve what the agent themselves can no longer access. The cost is obvious and chilling — until you choose to unlock it, that piece of your life is gone to you too.

**Binding Construct** (Threshold: ●●●●●)
- Implant a persistent psionic construct in another mind that remains anchored independently of the installer's ongoing attention
- The construct's configuration determines its behavior: lattice constructs maintain persistent network connections; dormant constructs sit inert until a specific signal activates them
- Active binding constructs are the mechanism of the MetaConcert lattice — each initiated Gifted carries one, installed by Maintainers during Integration
- Dormant binding constructs are the mechanism of the MAD doctrine — inert until triggered, at which point suppressed content surfaces as the target's own genuine recollection
- Requires precision Memory and Projection work at the intersection of both Domains; not a standard Memory technique but a Maintainer specialty
- Installed constructs are invisible to standard Detection; identification requires master-level expertise and knowledge of what to look for

*Practical notes:* This technique is the technical foundation of MetaConcert authority. Maintainers learn the active lattice-binding application as core professional competency. The dormant trigger variant is classified above Maintainer Circuit level; most Maintainers can work out the theoretical relationship between the two configurations without being told, which is why the restriction exists and why it is imperfect. A Gifted who fully understands how the lattice binding works, and then learns that the same technique can be configured to sit dormant in a non-Gifted mind for centuries until a signal releases it, typically requires some time to process the implications for everything the MetaConcert has ever done for the Camarilla. Clean severance of an installed construct — whether lattice binding or dormant trigger — requires the installer's equivalent of expertise working in reverse. Rough severance, as in the Chicago Lattice Disruption, is violent removal: traumatic to the host and to anyone nearby in the network.

## Domain: Detection

**Philosophy:** The outbound read — everything a mind broadcasts and cannot help broadcasting. The eyes and ears of psionic work, and the one thing a psion innately *is*: a receiver.

**Detection is not an act you perform. It is an act you *stop* performing.** Every other Domain adds something to the world; this one subtracts something from yourself. Using it is not reaching out but **releasing the hold** — letting through what bracing spends every waking moment keeping out — and closing it again afterward. The Domain's whole art is the width of that gap and how precisely it can be aimed. *(A psion at work does not look like someone straining. They look like someone who has briefly stopped concentrating.)*

**Primary Use:** Reading surface thought and emotion, sensing minds and supernatural entities, feeling psionic activity, reading tether condition

**Tether Strain:** **None.** Reading a broadcast is not a reach — the signal was already arriving, and the Domain only opens a specific gate rather than the whole floodgate. Nothing leaves you, so nothing wears. *You cannot fray by listening.* (Two exceptions, both of which are reaching rather than receiving: reading past the range at which the broadcast already reaches you, and **Trace**, which runs back down a live connection against a resisting quarry. Originating a Detection **broadcast** costs too — receiving is free, transmitting never is.)

**The innate floor:** Every psion passively *receives* — an unbraced one takes in the genuine surface cognition of every unshielded mind nearby: real thoughts, emotions, images, memory-fragments, rich and true, but indiscriminate, unattributed, simultaneous, contextless — a firehose with no valve. **Bracing** is the valve; **Detection dots** are the trained attention that turns the flood into a clean, directed, attributed read. That raw reception is not the same as *usable* Detection, which still takes active technique — and it is nothing like Eve's controlled, always-on, fray-free sense, which is a different phenomenon entirely (see [Eve](Eve.md)).

### Known Techniques

**Skim** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●)
- Direct the surface-sense at one mind and read what it is openly broadcasting — thought, emotion, image
- ● Basic: strong feeling, the gist of loud thought
- ●● to ●●●● : active thought streams, then detail, then several minds at once
- ●●●●● Master: a clean, attributed map of active consciousness
- Reads only what is *broadcast:* a **braced** mind, a held **Empty Mind**, or a disciplined calm returns nothing
- **Not resisted by Barriers or Willpower** — the only defense is not to broadcast

*Practical notes:* The workhorse read, now placed correctly. It picks up the surface a mind is leaking — trivial on the unbraced (mundanes, sleepers, the Unshielded), silent against the composed. It is *not* how you reach what someone is hiding; that is **Dig**, under Intrusion, which pushes past the surface and is what Barriers defends against. Skim reads the mask; Dig reads beneath it.

**Thoughtsense** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●)
- Active "radar pulse" detecting nearby minds
- Detects presence of active thought/consciousness
- ● Basic: Immediate area, vague presence
- ●● Adequate: Building-scale, general locations
- ●●● Strong: City block, specific positions
- ●●●● Advanced: District-wide, detailed mapping
- ●●●●● Master: City-scale, precise mental signatures

*Practical notes:* Works like sonar for minds. Detects "here are conscious beings." Effective against living humans, most supernaturals with active minds. Ineffective against truly mindless creatures (zombies, some constructs). Higher ratings provide greater range and precision.

**Empathic Sense** (Scaling: ● to ●●●●●)
- Detects emotional states and intensity
- Different from Thoughtsense (emotion vs. thought)
- ● Basic: Strong nearby emotions
- ●● Adequate: Subtle emotions, general mood
- ●●● Strong: Nuanced reading, emotional history
- ●●●● Advanced: Emotional patterns, trauma echoes
- ●●●●● Master: Residual emotional imprints in locations

*Practical notes:* Picks up passionate/emotional entities regardless of thought. Effective against Wraiths (who are basically pure emotion), emotional spirits, heightened states. Can detect emotional "echoes" in places where intense emotions occurred. A calm, meditative mind might evade this. Emotionless automaton evades Empathic but not Thoughtsense.

**Psionic Sense** (Threshold: ●●)
- Detects active use of psionic Techniques
- Sense when someone nearby uses Intrusion, Kinesis, etc.
- Range scales with Detection rating
- Can identify Domain being used
- Cannot identify specific technique

*Practical notes:* "Someone just used psionics nearby." Panopticon uses this to detect unauthorized activity. Can feel when someone Intrudes on a mind, throws telekinetic force, etc. Like feeling ripples in mental space.

**Resonance Sense** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Identifies type of supernatural entity
- Distinguish Kindred from Garou from Mage
- Sees through basic supernatural concealment
- Each type has distinct "signature"
- Higher ratings provide more detailed information

*Practical notes:* "That's not human. That's... vampire? Werewolf?" Essential for Operatives dealing with other supernatural factions. Kindred feel "wrong" (dead thoughts). Garou feel "wild" (Beast). Mages feel "fractured" (Avatar). Each has distinct resonance.

**Trace** (Threshold: ●●●)
- Follow a psionic signature back toward its source
- Tracks the residue of a recently used technique, or a mind read before and "locked onto"
- Turns a detected event into a direction, and a direction into a location
- Resisted by the quarry's Barriers and caution
- The bridge between noticing something and finding someone

*Practical notes:* How the Panopticon turns a flag into an address. Psionic use leaves a wake, and a skilled Detection specialist reads that wake the way a tracker reads bent grass and broken twigs. A Gifted who has read a particular mind even once can sometimes lock onto its signature and find it again across a city. This is the hunter's instrument, and the deepest reason the loudest mistake a fugitive can make is simply to use their power: every technique is a flare sent up into the dark, and Trace is the eye that watches where it falls.

**Tether Sense** (Threshold: ●●●●)
- Detects tether strain in other Gifted
- Panopticon specialty for monitoring fraying
- Sees metaphysical integrity — and material — of a tether
- Reads mundanes, Gifted, and Kindred alike; only a psidolon reads as nothing
- Can identify critical fraying before snap
- Requires concentration and proximity

*Practical notes:* This is what Panopticon uses to flag people for White Rooms. See the tether's condition, detect dangerous strain levels. Extremely valuable, requires master-level Detection skill. Most Gifted never learn this—it's Panopticon's secret weapon.

*What it was built for:* the fraying gauge is the day job. **Tether Sense is a psidolon detector.** Every living thing has a tether, and the Embrace reforges rather than deletes one, so the technique returns *something* on nearly everything with a mind — a mundane's hemp, a Gifted's steel, a Kindred's burned-and-recast third thing that is plainly there and plainly not living. None of that is an alarm. The alarm is the reading that comes back **empty**: no tether where one must be. The answer is absolute because nothing else in the world gives it, and that is only true because everything else *has* one. A Panopticon analyst can sweep a room full of Kindred and read seven wrong-but-present tethers without a flicker of concern, then go rigid at the eighth chair, where a charming man is telling a story and the world says there is no one sitting there.

## Cross-Splat Interactions

**Against Garou:**
- The Beast is the variable — the more ascendant it is, the less purchase any mental technique finds.
- **In Crinos or frenzy:** mental work fails. Skim returns only raw rage — "KILL TEAR DESTROY," obvious and useless. Control and Memory simply don't take; there is no coherent will to seize and no stable mind to edit, only the storm. Dominating a Crinos Garou is trying to control a hurricane.
- **In Homid or Lupus (the calm forms):** the Beast is quiet and mental techniques work — but a Garou is never *only* human. Even at rest the Beast is a low, ever-present resistance, so they are harder to read and harder to bend than an ordinary mortal.
- **Spiritually attuned Garou** — theurges, elders, the ritual-disciplined — are harder still. Years of spiritual practice function like trained Barriers; some can feel a clumsy Intrusion the way a mortal feels a draft on the back of the neck.
- **Detection** works in any form. It just confirms what you already suspected: *very angry.*
- **Kinesis** is unaffected — physics does not care about the Beast. A thrown car hits a werewolf exactly as hard. Whether it *stops* one is another question.

**Against Kindred:**
- Feel subtly "wrong" to Detection (predatory, dead)
- Vampiric Dominate vs Barriers is **not a threshold** — never resolve it as whose rating is higher. Dominate is the diluted copy; Barriers is the wall built to stop the original. Against a raised wall of any rating the attempt is *hazardous*, and against a real specialist it is a mistake: there is no rote, no known key, because the Barrier is active and adaptive rather than a static lock. An elder with centuries of blood to burn can still get through — but only by improvising at the limit of the Discipline, never casually, and never without opening the line back (see [The Best Defense](The%20Best%20Defense%20-%20Barriers%20and%20the%20Mental%20Domain.md))
- The deterrent is asymmetric *risk*, not low odds. A Kindred who fails against a psion has not merely been repelled; they have handed a trained mind a live channel and a face to remember
- Memory techniques work but inconsistently (Beast complicates things)
- Some memories tied to Beast resist editing
- **Age is not a defense.** A Methuselah running no active mental Discipline is *open* — the psion rolls unopposed. What buys something is a Discipline held **up**, deliberately, at the moment it matters.
- **Auspex:** Telepathy finds nothing on a *braced* psion (no surface leaking — the same defense that beats Skim); pushing past that is a reach and meets **Barriers**, with the two-way door. Aura Perception works normally, and **fraying shows**. Auspex also detects psionic activity in progress, which is the only way a Kindred learns they were read.
- **Presence works on psions exactly as on anyone.** Barriers stops intrusion, not *stimulus* — you cannot wall your way out of finding someone impressive. (Contrast psionic Emotion Manipulation, which is Barriers-resisted because it *reaches in* and installs a feeling; Presence broadcasts a quality of the user that the target's own mind answers.)
- **Obfuscate:** the eyes lose, Detection does not. Obfuscate edits the *observer*; Detection receives a broadcast the subject cannot switch off. The psion registers **that a mind is there and that something is wrong** — no clean read, no fix, no identification. Concealment survives; the target's certainty of being alone does not. Same reasoning covers **Chimerstry**.

*(Full treatment, including the Mind Sphere and the general test — does the effect **reach into** the mind, or **act on the world** the mind perceives? — in [Cross-Splat Edge Cases](Cross-Splat%20Edge%20Cases.md), "The Disciplines, Specifically.")*

**Against Mages:**
- Mind Sphere practitioners use Avatar to bend reality
- Psions use raw mental force (no Avatar, no reality negotiation)
- Different metaphysical mechanics entirely
- Both can affect each other but through different vectors
- Gifted often have advantage in pure mental combat (specialists vs. generalists)

**Against Wraiths:**
- Thoughtsense doesn't detect them (no active thoughts)
- Empathic Sense DOES detect them (pure emotion)
- Intrusion/Control extremely difficult (no stable mind to access)
- Kinesis works normally (physical manipulation)

## Practical Guidance for Players

**Building your character:**

**Memory/Intrusion specialist (Interrogator/Cleaner):**
- Need high ratings in both Domains
- Memory ●●●●● with Intrusion ● is useless
- Balanced investment (both ●●● minimum)
- This is Inquisition interrogator build

**Control/Intrusion specialist (Dominator):**
- Same problem as Memory specialist
- Need Intrusion to make Control work
- High Control with low Intrusion = unusable power
- This is infiltration/manipulation build

**Detection specialist (Scout/Intelligence):**
- Can function with Detection alone
- Add Barriers for protection
- Don't need other Domains to be effective
- This is Panopticon build

**Kinesis specialist (Combat):**
- Operates independently of other Domains
- Add Barriers for defense
- Extreme tether strain limits sustained use
- This is "last resort" or Rebel build

**Projection specialist (Reconnaissance):**
- Extremely dangerous to user
- Most personally risky Domain
- Often combined with Detection
- This is Operative build (if they use it at all)

**Barriers specialist (Defense/Privacy):**
- Essential for everyone at some level
- Pure Barriers specialist is support role
- Usually combined with other Domains
- This is Parasyte survival build

## Common Mistakes

**"I'll just use Memory without Intrusion"**
- Does not work. Memory requires mental access.
- Must successfully Intrude first, then Memory.
- This is why Memory specialists invest heavily in Intrusion.

**"I'll use Kinesis for everything"**
- Extreme tether strain will snap you fast
- Creates massive cleanup problems
- MetaConcert considers this dirty and reckless — the highest-attribution habit a Gifted can form
- Combat use is valid, casual use is stupid

**"Control is better than Memory"**
- Control creates angry witnesses who remember
- Memory makes them forget or believe they wanted it
- Control is temporary, Memory is permanent
- Professionals use Memory, amateurs use Control

**"I don't need Barriers, I'll just avoid conflict"**
- You're in the lattice. You're always being monitored.
- Without Barriers, your thoughts are public.
- Even ●● Barriers provides crucial privacy.
- This is how you get flagged by Panopticon.

**"Projection seems safe"**
- It is the MOST dangerous Domain to user
- Tether snap while projected = instant psidolon
- Many experienced Gifted refuse to learn it
- Distance and duration both increase danger

## Notes for Storytellers

**Tether strain by Domain (rough guidelines):**

**Lowest strain:**
- Detection (passive observation)
- Barriers (defensive, not forcing anything)

**Moderate strain:**
- Intrusion (active but read-only)
- Memory (invasive but not fighting physics)

**High strain:**
- Control (fighting another consciousness)
- Projection (stretching tether, distance matters)

**Extreme strain:**
- Kinesis (wrestling with physical reality)

**Remember:**
- Strain accumulates with use
- Recovery requires rest and time
- Pushing while already strained risks snap
- Different Gifted have different tolerances
- Stress, emotion, and desperation increase strain

**For combat encounters:**
- Kinesis users are powerful but burning their tether
- Detection specialists provide tactical awareness
- Intrusion/Memory specialists control aftermath
- Control specialists can end fights but create witnesses
- Barriers specialists protect team from mental attack
- Smart Gifted avoid combat entirely (too much strain, too much cleanup)
