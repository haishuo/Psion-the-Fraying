# The Tether and Fraying: Metaphysical Mechanics

**The Price of Projection**

*Note on terminology: "fraying" refers specifically to the mechanical process of tether strain described in this document. It is also the splat subtitle — Psion: The Fraying — because fraying is the condition every Gifted lives under and the foundation on which the MetaConcert's entire institutional apparatus is built. "Unshielded" refers to the distinct pre-training state of newly Expressed Gifted — not tether damage, but the absence of containment. These are different conditions on different axes. (The two are deliberately named so they cannot be confused: fraying and its endpoint, Untethering, are tether damage; the Unshielded state is a shield that was never built. The MetaConcert's clinical term for the Unshielded state is **Exposed**; Gifted in common speech call it being **Raw**.)*

## What Is the Tether?

The tether is a universal feature of human existence.

Every human being has one. It is the invisible metaphysical structure binding three components of consciousness into unified existence: mind, body, and soul. Without it, coherent existence is not possible. It is not something the Gifted have and others lack. It is something everyone has, with the crucial variation being how much load it can bear.

**It is not:**
- A physical object
- Visible to normal sight
- Something that can be "strengthened" permanently
- Renewable or replaceable
- Something you can train to be "better"
- Unique to the Gifted

**It is:**
- Present in every human being
- Absolutely essential for coherent existence
- Variable in resilience between individuals
- Subject to strain from psionic use
- Capable of recovery with proper rest
- Permanently broken if pushed beyond limits

**The resilience variation:**

Tethers vary in resilience the way bones vary in density. Most people's bones serve their intended purpose — bearing the load of ordinary life — perfectly well. Some people have higher or lower bone density. The variation is meaningful in specific contexts but doesn't make someone a different kind of being.

Most tethers are perfectly functional for ordinary human life. The variation only becomes relevant when psionic Expression is attempted. The Gifted are those whose tether resilience falls above the threshold necessary to survive Expression. This is a structural variation, not a categorical difference in kind.

**The split analogy:**

Performing a full split requires specific physiological capacity. Most people have legs. Not everyone's physiology permits a full split even with dedicated training. Those who can perform a split still need training and practice — the capacity doesn't guarantee the ability to use it well. Those who attempt a split without the necessary physiology injure themselves. The Gifted are those with the structural capacity for the split who have also attempted it. The split itself still strains the relevant structures. It still requires careful practice and recovery. The capacity to survive it does not mean it costs nothing.

**The analogy's limit:**

The MetaConcert's clinical framework sometimes implies the Gifted have special metaphysical architecture that ordinary humans lack. This framing serves institutional purposes — it makes the Gifted feel categorically special and therefore more invested in Gifted institutions. The tether variation that produces the Gifted is roughly as cosmically significant as bone density. The institution built around it is a totalitarian surveillance state.

## The Three Components

**MIND:**
- Consciousness, thought, awareness, will
- The part of you that thinks, decides, experiences
- In psionics, this is what you project outward
- Can exist briefly without body (Projection)
- Cannot exist indefinitely without anchor

**BODY:**
- Physical form, biological processes
- The vessel that houses consciousness
- Provides sensory input and physical interaction
- Remains vulnerable while mind projects
- Can survive without mind (coma, brain death)

**SOUL:**
- Metaphysical essence, spiritual anchor
- The "you-ness" that persists beyond thought and flesh
- Connection to deeper reality structures
- Least understood component
- Necessary for coherent personhood

**The tether binds all three.**

When a Gifted uses psionics — especially Projection — they temporarily separate mind from body while the tether keeps them connected. It's like holding your breath underwater while a rope connects you to the boat. The rope (tether) lets you explore below the surface (project) while maintaining connection to safety (body/soul anchor).

But ropes fray. And if the rope breaks while you're underwater, you don't make it back to the surface.

## Failed Expression

Before discussing fraying, it is necessary to address a different and more final failure: what happens when someone with insufficient tether resilience attempts to Express the Talent.

Failed Expression is not fraying. It does not look like fraying. It bears no meaningful resemblance to the gradual tether strain described in the rest of this document.

When an individual whose tether resilience falls below the threshold for Expression attempts to project psionically, the tether fails under the first load before any ability expresses. The results are immediate and catastrophic. There is no psidolon. There is no coherent consciousness that persists in any form. There is no aftermath in the conventional sense. The individual ceases to exist as a coherent entity.

The MetaConcert documents these cases. Their files are clinical and brief. There are no follow-up entries because there is nothing to follow up on.

The Cult of the Unbound has observed more failed Expressions than any other organization due to the nature of their activities — they actively encourage Expression in vulnerable adults, many of whom have not been assessed for tether resilience. They have developed informal assessment frameworks based on accumulated observation. They do not discuss this in recruitment contexts. The transcendence narrative they offer requires the outcome of Expression to be transformation or, at minimum, death-as-ascension. Failed Expression — immediate catastrophic dissolution before any ability expresses, no psidolon, no aftermath — does not fit the narrative.

The frequency of failed Expression is unknown. MetaConcert data is limited to cases that came to their attention. Undocumented cases in isolated populations — particularly those reached by the Cult or operating alone — are suspected to be significantly more common than the official record reflects.

## The Unshielded State

Before discussing fraying — the cumulative tether damage that comes from psionic *use* — it is necessary to address a condition that precedes use entirely: the Unshielded state.

When a Gifted first Expresses, they have a functional tether and active psionic capability but no containment. No training. No technique. No mental boundary between their own thoughts and the thoughts of everyone around them. The result is uncontrolled bidirectional thought leakage: their thoughts broadcast outward, external thoughts flood inward, and no filter exists to distinguish self from signal.

**The Unshielded state is not tether damage.** The tether is intact. The problem is entirely one of containment — the absence of any trained structure to manage what the tether now permits. This is the critical distinction between Unshielded and fraying: fraying is damage to a trained mind from overuse; Unshielded is the absence of training in an intact mind. The two conditions occupy different axes of the same metaphysical system.

**Characteristics of the Unshielded state:**
- Uncontrolled surface thought broadcast (psionically "loud")
- Involuntary reception of nearby minds' surface thoughts
- No ability to distinguish own mental content from external signal
- Escalating psychic overload as the mind receives more input than it can process
- Distinctive psionic signature detectable by Panopticon sweeps — this is how newly Expressed children are located

**What prolonged Unshielded state produces:**

Left untreated, the Unshielded state is not static. It escalates. The psychic overload compounds: more signal floods in, the mind has less capacity to process it, the inability to filter produces cognitive destabilization. The progression is measurable:

- **Early stage:** Confusion, disorientation, difficulty distinguishing own thoughts from external. Frightening but manageable if intervention comes quickly.
- **Intermediate stage:** Identity fragmentation begins. The boundary between self and others blurs. The Gifted may experience thoughts, memories, or emotional states that are not their own as if they were. From the outside, this resembles dissociative symptoms.
- **Advanced stage:** Severe cognitive destabilization. Symptoms are clinically indistinguishable from DID, psychosis, or schizophrenia — not because those conditions are present, but because the phenomenology of sustained psionic overload produces similar presentations. The mind is not diseased. It is overwhelmed.
- **Critical stage:** Cognitive architecture begins to collapse under sustained overload. At this point, even if bracing is established, the damage may require Cognitive Reconstruction — Praxis's clinical procedure for rebuilding stable cognitive structure from a compromised state.

**Resolution: Bracing**

The Unshielded state is resolved by bracing — the foundational containment technique taught by Integrators to every newly Expressed Gifted. Bracing establishes the first mental boundary: a proto-Thoughtshield that stops the bidirectional leakage and gives the mind the structure it needs to begin processing psionic input in a controlled way. Once bracing is established, the child is no longer Unshielded, and everything else in their training — Domain theory, formal technique, lattice orientation — builds on this foundation.

The full mechanics of bracing are documented in the Domains reference under Barriers.

**Why this matters for the rest of this document:**

The Unshielded state establishes that the Gifted face two distinct categories of risk: the risk of overload from insufficient containment (Unshielded → psychic overload → cognitive damage) and the risk of degradation from excessive use (fraying → critical fraying → Untethering). The first is resolved by training. The second is managed by discipline but never eliminated. A Gifted who has been properly trained and braced will never be Unshielded again — but they will fray, eventually, if they use their abilities long enough and hard enough. The tether is finite. The training doesn't change that. It only determines whether the Gifted has the containment necessary to use what the tether permits without being destroyed by the input before the tether even comes under strain.

## How Fraying Works

**Fraying is cumulative strain on the tether from psionic use.**

Every time you project consciousness beyond its natural boundaries — every mind you read, every object you move with thought alone, every memory you edit — you stress the tether. The amount of strain depends on:

**Intensity of projection:**
- Minor techniques (Detection pulse): minimal strain
- Moderate techniques (Intrusion, basic Memory work): noticeable strain
- Major techniques (extended Control, heavy Kinesis use): significant strain
- Extreme techniques (long-distance Projection, Reality manipulation): severe strain

**Duration:**
- Quick use: strain accumulates slowly
- Sustained use: strain accumulates rapidly
- Continuous use over hours/days: exponential accumulation

**Frequency:**
- Occasional use with rest between: manageable
- Repeated use without recovery: dangerous
- Constant use with no downtime: catastrophic

**Emotional state:**
- Calm, controlled use: normal strain
- Stressed, desperate use: increased strain
- Panicked, survival use: severe strain
- Using psionics while already fraying: multiplied risk

**Individual variation:**
- Some Gifted have more resilient tethers above the Expression threshold
- Some fray faster from less use
- Genetics, spiritual constitution, unknown factors
- No reliable way to predict individual tolerance before finding limits

## Stages of Fraying

Fraying progresses through observable stages. These are clinical descriptions used by Panopticon monitors and Integrator trainers.

### Stage 1: Minimal Strain (Normal Use)

**Symptoms:**
- Slight fatigue after extended psionic use
- Mild headaches following particularly complex techniques
- Brief moment of disorientation when returning from Projection
- Easily dismissed as normal tiredness

**Tether condition:**
- Microscopic stress, like muscle soreness after exercise
- Fully recoverable with adequate rest (hours to a day)
- No permanent damage
- No detection by Panopticon (within normal parameters)

**Risk level:** Safe
**Recovery time:** 6-12 hours rest
**MetaConcert response:** None (normal operation)

### Stage 2: Moderate Strain (Approaching Limits)

**Symptoms:**
- Persistent low-grade headaches
- Noticeable delay between thought and physical action
- Occasional sensory "glitches" (phantom sensations, visual static)
- Difficulty maintaining focus
- Sleep disturbances or unusually vivid dreams
- Physical exhaustion disproportionate to activity

**Tether condition:**
- Significant stress, like a strained muscle
- Requires extended rest to recover (days)
- Repeated strain at this level risks progression
- Detectable by Panopticon if they're actively monitoring you

**Risk level:** Caution advised
**Recovery time:** 2-5 days complete rest from psionic use
**MetaConcert response:** Warning issued, monitored more closely

### Stage 3: Severe Strain (Dangerous Territory)

**Symptoms:**
- Constant dull ache in head/body
- Significant dissociation from physical sensation
- "Numbness" in limbs or delayed pain response
- Involuntary minor psionic Expressions (stray thoughts broadcast, small objects moving)
- Difficulty distinguishing between thought and action
- Body beginning to feel "foreign" or "wrong"
- Severe sleep disruption or projection during sleep

**Tether condition:**
- Deep strain, approaching damage threshold
- Recovery requires weeks of complete rest
- Permanent damage possible if pushed further
- Actively flagged by Panopticon monitoring

**Risk level:** High danger
**Recovery time:** 2-4 weeks absolute prohibition on psionic use
**MetaConcert response:** Mandatory rest period enforced, possible White Room observation

### Stage 4: Critical Strain (Pre-Snap)

**Symptoms:**
- Severe body rejection (pain when consciousness "settles" back into body)
- Persistent dissociation (feeling like observer of own life)
- Involuntary projection during stress or emotion
- Stuttered coherence (gaps in thought processes, verbal hesitation)
- Identity flickering (brief moments of not recognizing self)
- Physical symptoms: nosebleeds, tremors, difficulty coordinating movement
- Intense fear/anxiety (instinctive recognition of danger)

**Tether condition:**
- Critically weakened, microscopic "tears" forming
- Recovery uncertain even with extended rest
- One more significant use could cause snap
- Tether resonance pattern visible to Panopticon Detection

**Risk level:** Imminent snap danger
**Recovery time:** Months of complete rest, outcome uncertain
**MetaConcert response:** Immediate White Room extraction or field termination

### Stage 5: The Snap (Point of No Return)

**What happens:**
- The tether breaks completely
- Mind separates from body/soul anchor
- Consciousness becomes untethered
- Immediate transformation into psidolon
- No recovery possible

**Two outcomes:**

**Weak Gifted (most common):**
- Insufficient psionic strength to maintain coherence
- Consciousness dissipates within seconds/minutes
- Death (or whatever passing into non-existence is)
- Merciful compared to alternative

**Strong Gifted (rare, catastrophic):**
- Enough power to maintain coherent consciousness
- Become psidolon — cloud of psionic energy
- Immediate desperate need to find host body
- Survival requires parasitic existence
- Identity will fragment over time/hosts

## Recovery Mechanics

**Fraying CAN be reversed through proper rest and recovery.**

**What helps:**

**Complete rest from psionic use:**
- Most important factor
- No techniques, no projection, no mental manipulation
- Even "safe" Detection use can delay recovery
- Absolute prohibition on all Domains

**Time:**
- Different stages require different recovery periods
- Stage 1: hours
- Stage 2: days
- Stage 3: weeks
- Stage 4: months (if recovery possible at all)

**Physical rest:**
- Sleep, proper nutrition, low stress
- Body and mind are connected — physical health supports recovery
- Meditation and mental calm help
- Avoiding emotional extremes

**Distance from psionic activity:**
- Being away from other Gifted using powers
- Reducing exposure to psionic "background noise"
- Some find mortal company restful
- CoG sanctuaries provide spiritually calm environments

**What does NOT help:**

**Techniques or "tether strengthening":**
- No Domain can repair the tether
- Projection to "examine" your tether makes it worse
- Folk remedies and Project theories are mostly wishful thinking
- The tether recovers on its own or not at all

**Drugs or medication:**
- Mundane medicine doesn't affect metaphysical structures
- Some drugs might reduce symptoms (pain, anxiety)
- But they don't accelerate recovery
- May mask warning signs, leading to worse decisions

**Therapy or counseling:**
- Can help with psychological stress
- Reduces emotional strain that worsens fraying
- But doesn't directly repair tether
- Mostly valuable for coping with necessary rest period

**Spiritual practices:**
- Some Garou mystics claim certain rituals help
- Unproven and inconsistent results
- May provide placebo comfort
- CoG use spiritual practices as part of holistic rest approach

**The MetaConcert position:**
Rest works. Time works. Nothing else reliably works. Stop looking for shortcuts and just REST.

**The Projects' position:**
MetaConcert isn't wrong about rest being necessary, but their "rest period" is enforced at gunpoint and ends in execution if you don't recover fast enough.

## Individual Variation

**Not all Gifted fray at the same rate.**

There are two distinct types of tether variation among people with sufficient resilience to have expressed the Talent:

### Resilience Threshold

Whether the tether is strong enough to survive Expression at all. This is binary in outcome — either you survive first Expression or you don't — but the underlying spectrum is continuous. Those who survive Expression have cleared the threshold. Those who don't are documented in MetaConcert files that nobody discusses at orientation.

This is the variation that determines who is Gifted. It is not a special metaphysical gift. It is a structural variation, like bone density.

No test exists to determine whether someone has cleared the threshold before they attempt Expression. The MetaConcert's Integration protocols are designed to guide Expression safely in those who have already expressed on their own. They do not involve testing tether resilience in advance; the data for that does not exist in a form useful for prediction.

### Fraying Rate

How quickly the tether degrades under psionic use. This varies significantly between individuals with sufficient resilience. This is the variation the MetaConcert monitors and the Projects struggle with.

**Factors affecting fraying speed:**

**Natural resilience above threshold:**
- Some people have inherently more resilient tethers
- Genetic component suspected but not proven
- Spiritual constitution matters (Garou hybrid like Eve proves this)
- No way to test or predict before Expression

**Domain specialization:**
- Kinesis users fray faster (extreme strain)
- Detection users fray slower (minimal strain)
- Projection users play with fire (distance = danger)
- Balanced use across Domains spreads strain

**Training and discipline:**
- Integrator-trained Gifted understand limits better
- Self-taught may push too hard from ignorance
- Or may be more cautious from fear
- Knowledge helps but doesn't prevent fraying

**Psychological factors:**
- Anxiety and stress accelerate fraying
- Calm, controlled use is safer
- Desperation makes everything worse
- Mental discipline matters (Julian Vire proved this)

**Age at Expression:**
- Children have more "flexible" tethers (some theories)
- Adults expressing late may fray faster
- Or may have better self-control
- Data is inconclusive

**Power level:**
- More powerful Gifted can do more damage to themselves
- But also might have stronger base tethers
- High power + low discipline = rapid fraying
- High power + high discipline = sustainable use

**The frustrating truth:**
You don't know your limits until you find them. And by then it might be too late.

## Warning Signs and Self-Monitoring

**How do you know you're fraying?**

**Physical indicators:**
- Persistent headaches (especially after psionic use)
- Unusual fatigue or exhaustion
- Coordination problems or clumsiness
- Sensory distortions (sounds wrong, colors off, touch delayed)
- Nosebleeds or other minor hemorrhages
- Tremors or muscle twitches

**Mental indicators:**
- Difficulty focusing or maintaining attention
- Feeling "disconnected" from surroundings
- Thoughts feeling "slow" or "sticky"
- Increased anxiety or paranoia
- Difficulty making decisions
- Gaps in awareness or lost time

**Psionic indicators:**
- Techniques taking more effort than usual
- Reduced precision or control
- Involuntary minor Expressions
- Pain during or after projection
- Difficulty "settling back" into body
- Stray thoughts broadcasting to nearby Gifted

**The problem:**
Many warning signs are subtle and easy to rationalize:
- "I'm just tired from staying up late"
- "It's stress from the mission"
- "Everyone has headaches sometimes"
- "I'll rest after this one more thing"

**The MetaConcert solution:**
Panopticon monitors everyone constantly. You don't have to recognize the signs — they'll tell you when you're critical. Then they'll execute you to be safe.

**The Projects' problem:**
No Panopticon monitoring means relying on self-awareness and peer observation. Both are fallible. People hide symptoms. People lie to themselves. People die.

## The Snap Point

**When does the tether actually break?**

**There is no precise threshold.**

Unlike a rope that breaks at a specific tension, the tether degrades gradually and unpredictably. Think of it like:

- Ice cracking under weight (you don't know exactly when it breaks)
- A rubber band stretched too far (fails suddenly after gradual weakening)
- Structural failure in buildings (small stresses accumulate until catastrophic collapse)

**Factors that can trigger snap:**

**One major strain while already critical:**
- Attempting master-level technique while Stage 4
- Long-distance Projection while severely frayed
- Sustained combat psionic use while weakened

**Cumulative minor strain:**
- Repeated small uses without recovery
- "Just one more Detection pulse" dozens of times
- Death by a thousand cuts

**Stress/emotion while fraying:**
- Fear, rage, desperation amplify strain
- Survival situations push people past limits
- Adrenaline makes you ignore warning signs

**Unexpected resistance:**
- Trying to Intrude a mind with stronger Barriers than expected
- Control target resisting harder than anticipated
- Kinesis manipulation encountering unexpected weight/resistance

**The horrible uncertainty:**
You never know if the next use is the one that snaps you. Stage 4 might last days or minutes. You might recover or snap. No one can tell you for certain.

**This is why the MetaConcert executes at Stage 4:**
They can't know if you'll snap tomorrow or next week. But they know you MIGHT. And that's enough.

## Misconceptions and Myths

**Common false beliefs about fraying:**

**MYTH:** "Stronger Gifted don't fray as fast"
**REALITY:** Power level doesn't determine fraying rate. Discipline and usage patterns matter more.

**MYTH:** "You can train your tether to be stronger"
**REALITY:** The tether is what it is. You can learn to use it efficiently, but you can't strengthen it.

**MYTH:** "Certain techniques don't cause fraying"
**REALITY:** All psionic use causes strain. Some techniques strain less, but nothing is free.

**MYTH:** "If you're careful, you'll never fray"
**REALITY:** Careful use is safer, but life happens. Stress, emergencies, desperate situations — eventually you push too hard.

**MYTH:** "The MetaConcert executes people who aren't really critical"
**REALITY:** True. Their false positive rate is enormous. But their false negative rate is nearly zero.

**MYTH:** "Outside the lattice, you can use powers freely"
**REALITY:** Without monitoring, you might not notice fraying until too late. Freedom from surveillance doesn't mean freedom from consequences.

**MYTH:** "Fraying is reversible up until the snap"
**REALITY:** Mostly true, but Stage 4 recovery is uncertain. Permanent damage is possible before snap.

**MYTH:** "Untethering is evolution/transcendence"
**REALITY:** It's fragmentation and eventual loss of identity. The Cult lies.

**MYTH:** "Love/willpower/faith can prevent snapping"
**REALITY:** Julian Vire had incredible willpower. His tether still had limits. Mental strength helps discipline, not tether integrity.

**MYTH:** "Eve's immunity means a cure is possible"
**REALITY:** Eve's condition is unique hybrid biology, not a technique that can be learned or replicated.

**MYTH:** "The Gifted have special metaphysical architecture that ordinary humans lack"
**REALITY:** The tether is universal. Every human has one. The Gifted have tethers resilient enough to survive psionic Expression — a structural variation, not a categorical difference. The MetaConcert's clinical framework sometimes implies otherwise because treating the Gifted as categorically distinct serves institutional purposes. The tether variation that produces the Gifted is roughly as cosmically significant as bone density. The institution built around it is a totalitarian surveillance state.

## The Metaphysics of Projection

**Why does projection specifically strain the tether?**

Projection is the fundamental psionic act — extending consciousness beyond its natural boundaries. Every psionic technique involves some form of projection:

**Detection:**
- Project awareness outward to sense minds/entities
- Minimal strain (brief, limited extension)

**Intrusion:**
- Project consciousness into another mind
- Moderate strain (active penetration, resistance)

**Memory/Control:**
- Project will into another's mental structures
- High strain (manipulating complex systems)

**Kinesis:**
- Project mental energy into physical reality
- Extreme strain (fighting physics itself)

**Projection (Domain):**
- Full separation of consciousness from body
- Severe strain (maximum extension, maintained duration)

**The distance factor:**

The further consciousness extends from the body, the more the tether stretches. Like a rubber band:
- Short distance: manageable tension
- Medium distance: noticeable strain
- Long distance: dangerous stress
- Extreme distance: approaching snap point

**The return:**

When you pull consciousness back to body, there's a moment of "settling" where mind/body/soul re-integrate. When fraying is severe, this process becomes painful or difficult — body rejection, like an immune response against the returning mind.

## Why No Cure Exists

**Fundamental metaphysical limitations:**

The tether is not a disease to be cured or an injury to be healed. It's a structural component of existence. Asking "why can't we fix the tether?" is like asking "why can't we make muscles that never get tired?" or "why can't we make bones that never break?"

**Biological/spiritual binding:**
- The tether is woven into your fundamental existence
- You can't replace it without replacing yourself
- No technique, technology, or magic can rebuild it
- Even Mage reality-bending doesn't affect it (different metaphysical layer)

**The Eve exception proves the rule:**
- Eve doesn't fray because her hybrid nature bypasses normal mechanics
- Not because she "fixed" her tether
- Not because she "strengthened" it
- But because Rage and Talent interact uniquely in her specific case
- Cannot be replicated, taught, or transferred

**What this means:**

Every Gifted lives with a fundamental limitation. Use your powers, manage the strain, rest when needed, and hope you don't push too far. There is no upgrade path. No cure. No transcendence.

Just discipline, caution, and the constant awareness that your greatest power is also your greatest vulnerability.

## For Storytellers

**Using fraying in gameplay:**

**Fraying as resource management:**
- Players must balance power use against recovery time
- Creates tension in extended missions
- Forces difficult choices (use power now and risk fraying, or conserve and risk failure)

**Fraying as horror:**
- Describe progressive symptoms in detail
- Physical sensations of dissociation
- The creeping fear that you're losing yourself
- Other characters noticing changes

**Fraying as character development:**
- How does your character respond to limits?
- Do they push through warning signs?
- Do they become paranoid about fraying?
- How do they feel about execution for suspected fraying?

**Fraying as plot driver:**
- NPC begins showing symptoms
- Party must decide: report them or protect them?
- MetaConcert flags PC for White Room
- Race against time to recover or escape
- Cult targets fraying character with "transcendence" pitch

**Key principles:**
- Make fraying feel real and frightening
- Don't let it be abstract — describe concrete symptoms
- Create situations where players must choose between power and safety
- Show NPCs at different stages so players understand progression
- Remember: Stage 4 is death sentence from MetaConcert perspective
